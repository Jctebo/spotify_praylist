# v358 — Autoplay on by default + visible toggle

## 1 · Scope

- **Requirement (John, voice call 2026-11-10 ~07:41 CT):** "Can you also make autoplay the default in the app — it seems like after it plays a prayer combination it pauses. So can you make autoplay on by default and make a visible toggle throughout the app?"
- **Original §1 diagnosis (superseded):** the package read the pause as the block-end stop (v351 decision 2) and proposed chaining past it. John corrected the premise twice — "No. It's been stopping WITHIN a block" (07:55 CT) and the pinpoint (15:28 CT): "Autoplay still doesn't work between tracks. It just pauses after a pair. The queue is there. But it doesn't keep playing." The item stood HELD at Scope on the wrong diagnosis.
- **Decisions (§1 package, uncontradicted defaults, approved 2026-11-10 15:30 CT):** (1) chaining scope — any start plays forward through the whole presented day: pairs chain, and at a block's end playback continues into the next block until the day ends · (2) toggle rides the dock + Now Playing + Settings · (3) the setting lives on the device, like the playback rate — default ON · (4) run level To Ship.
- **DEVIATION NAMED in §1:** v351 decision 2 (2026-11-10 04:26 CT) set a tapped block queue to STOP at the block's end; autoplay-ON-by-default supersedes that as the default — with the toggle OFF the v351 stop behavior stands exactly.
- **Rules that stand either way:** autoplay never starts playback by itself (a restored v355 queue still stands paused; one tap continues) · the sleep timer's stop is final (the track-mode divert stands ahead of every advance and chain; the minutes-mode stop is a pause that never reaches the ended handler) · Play All is unchanged in build (it already holds the whole day; its end is the day's end).
- **Approval (John, Ora thread, 2026-11-10 15:30 CT):** continuous advance between tracks is the expected baseline behavior; autoplay is ON by default; the visible toggle is the ability to turn that behavior OFF, restoring the stop; run level TO SHIP. Where the original §1 and this approval differ, the approval governs. The diagnosis below governs the foundation: the fixed advance is the behavior the toggle controls.

## 2 · Plan

- **Diagnosis first (this section's evidence, gathered under the 15:28 CT pinpoint before any build):** reproduction harness (the v351 mold modernized — all migrations, the production enclosure posture) at trunk 1f05e8b, pre-fix run `harness-v358-repro5.log`.
- **Reproduction:** with the podcast enclosure host refusing direct browser reads (the production §2.3 posture) and the Daily Rosary pass-through download delayed 30 s, a Morning block queue built at tap time played the Invitatory pair and the Morning Prayer pair, played `intro:daily-rosary`, then stood dead on `daily-rosary`: its descriptor carried the raw enclosure URL, the element read `error 4`, readyState 0, paused — **the queue standing at index 6 of 39 (Play All) / 6 of 11 (block play), its remaining entries unplayed.** Exactly John's pinpoint.
- **Root cause (defect in shipped code, the v342 seam — not designed behavior):** (a) an own/intention descriptor's snapshot URL is `streamUrl || enclosureUrl` — the raw enclosure until the pass-through download lands and the blob URL swaps into the fresh day build (§2.3: the enclosure host refuses browser reads; the blob is the only playable form), so the snapshot URL is a *download state*; (b) the standing queue keeps the snapshot descriptor; (c) `queueLiveDesc` — the advance-time re-check — substituted fresh descriptors only for external slots, on its comment's assumption that own availability "is a payload fact, not a download state," which is false for the URL; (d) `startDesc` played the dead URL, `play()`'s rejection was swallowed (`.catch(function () {})`), and with no `error` listener no `ended` ever fired — the queue halted silently with its entries standing. Whether a given pair stopped depended on whether its download had landed before the queue was built, which is why the failure presented as "after a pair" at varying points.
- **Fix plan (smallest, at the seam):** `queueLiveDesc` re-resolves own + intention descriptors against the fresh day build by (episodeId, block) — the v360 occurrence discipline — substituting the fresh descriptor (blob URL once landed) and passing over a fresh descriptor standing unavailable; the external path stands byte-identical. Plus a bounded `error` handler: within a start's own window, re-resolve the standing entry — a landed download restarts it on the fresh URL; the same URL means the download may still be in flight, so wait and re-check on a bounded clock (20 × 1.5 s — the previous pair's minutes are the usual budget); only an entry that still cannot stand passes over (queue) or clears (single play). Paused loads standing by (loadResume, the sleep hold) error outside a start's window and are untouched.
- **Feature plan:** device-local store `opn-autoplay-v358` (only an expressly stored 'off' reads OFF; a fresh store reads ON) · `chainForward(fromDesc)` at the ended handler: on queue exhaustion (autoplay ON) and on a single play's end (autoplay ON), start the fresh whole-day build past the ended track — located by (episodeId, block) — filtered by the standing `unplayedOnly` discipline; OFF keeps today's stops exactly · the sleep divert stands ahead of the chain · toggle on three surfaces (dock button `dock-autoplay` · Now Playing `np-autoplay` beside speed/sleep · Settings Playback section above Source mapping, `set-autoplay`), one store, labels synced from it · SW pin bump to `ora-pro-nobis-v358-1` · no migration, no Worker route, no payload or nightly change (0016 stays free).
