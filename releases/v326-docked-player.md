### Discovery — the player as it stands (verified first-hand, trunk @ c59a32b)

1. **The shell already has the anchor points.** `app/worker/src/ui.ts` (`SHELL_HTML`): a sticky brand bar on top, `<main id="view" class="view">`, then `<audio id="audio" preload="none">` standing in the shell **outside** the view, then `<nav class="tabbar" id="tabbar">`. The tab bar is `position: fixed; bottom: 0; z-index: 30` with `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` — the phone's safe area is already handled at the tab bar. The view carries `padding: 14px 14px 96px` — the 96px bottom pad is today's clearance for the tab bar. Full-screen sheets (`.sheet`, the sleep sheet, the editors, and v318's Read view) stand at `z-index: 50`, covering the tab bar.
2. **All playback state is shell-scope and tab-independent — by v312's design.** `app/worker/src/client.ts` line 8–9: `audioEl` and the single `state` object (`queue`, `queueIdx`, `current`, `sleep`, …) live outside every render; no render resets them, so switching tabs never stops or resets playback. The dock is a second view onto state that already survives every tab — it needs no new state home.
3. **Where controls render today.** On the Prayers tab only: the Play All row (gold **Play All** · "the whole day, in order" · the **Sleep · …** ghost button, `id="sleep-btn"` — v320's control, its label IS its state) · a 42px round play button on every track card (`data-playkey`) · a per-block queue play button in each block header (`data-queue`). On the Intentions tab: the "Today's intentions — audio" chip bar, whose chips call the legacy `play(url, btn)` path — a one-off descriptor (`episodeId: 'url:…'`, title "Intentions") outside any queue. On Examination and Confession: **no playback surface at all** — audio started elsewhere plays on with nothing on screen naming it or able to pause it. That gap is this item's concrete use case.
4. **State reflection already exists, in one place.** `markPlaying(id)` (client.ts) sweeps `document.querySelectorAll('[data-playkey]')` and swaps each matching button's glyph (▶ / ❚❚); it is called from `startDesc`, the `pause` handler, and queue end. The sleep label updates through `updateSleepLabel()`, which addresses `#sleep-btn` by id — an element that exists only while the Prayers tab is rendered, and the label simply waits for the next render when it is not. Both patterns matter to the dock's design (see Risks).
5. **There is no in-app progress or seek surface today.** Nothing in the app displays position or duration for the playing track, and no in-app seek control exists; seek lives only in the Media Session handlers (lock screen). What exists to build on: the `timeupdate` listener already fires continuously and already drives `updatePositionState()` (Media Session), the v320 `sleepTick()`, and the throttled resume save — a dock progress line rides the same event at no new cadence cost. `audioEl.duration` / `currentTime` are the standing sources.
6. **The Read view (v318, built, not landed) wires to the same element.** On release/v318-app, `openRead(t)` builds a `.sheet readsheet` whose play button calls `playTrack(desc)` on the same descriptor the card uses, driving the same shell-level `audioEl` and queue; closing the sheet never touches playback. The dock must compose with this surface, not compete with it (decision D6).
7. **Descriptors carry almost everything the dock needs.** A queue descriptor is `{episodeId, title, url, available}`; the block a track stands under is present on the payload track (`_block`) at queue-build time but is not carried into the descriptor. A dock sub-line naming the block ("Morning · Intentions" style) needs the block carried in — a client-internal widening of `trackDesc`, no server or payload change.
8. **Nothing-played is a real, designed state.** The app opens paused by rule (v312: resume is offered, sound never autoplays). `state.current` is `null` until the first `startDesc`, and returns to `null` at queue end and at a lone track's end (the `ended` handler). The dock's visibility rule can key on exactly this — no new flag.
9. **Fonts.** The v312 Amendment-1 floor stands in `APP_CSS`: base 17px, card titles 17px, meta 14px, tab labels 13px. The dock's text follows the same floor (title at the card-title step, meta at the meta step, nothing under ~13px).

### Decisions for John (each with the Scope recommendation marked)

- **D1 — When the dock stands.** (a) **Only while something is loaded** — it appears at the first play and clears when playback ends and the queue empties (Spotify's behavior) · (b) always present, showing a "today" or Resume affordance before anything plays. **Recommendation: (a)** — before the first play there is nothing to control; an always-on bar takes ~60px from every screen permanently, and the pre-play affordance already exists as the Resume card on the Prayers tab. The app also opens paused by rule — an empty dock would contradict that quiet opening.
- **D2 — The dock's controls.** (a) **Play/pause + next, with a display-only progress line** · (b) play/pause only · (c) play/pause + previous + next, with tap-to-seek on the progress line. **Recommendation: (a)** — next is the mini-player's working control (the queue is the app's main listening shape); previous lives one tap away in the expanded player and on the lock screen; a display-only line cannot be mis-tapped mid-prayer, and seek already exists on the lock screen. If John wants previous in the dock, (c) without the seek is a small widening Plan can price.
- **D3 — What tapping the dock opens.** (a) **The Prayers tab, scrolled to the playing track's card** — the day screen is the full player: every control (Play All, per-block queues, Sleep, Read) already stands there · (b) a new dedicated now-playing screen (big title, seek slider, full transport, sleep) in the Spotify shape. **Recommendation: (a)** — smallest build, zero duplicated controls, and the day screen is where John's eye already goes; (b) is a second player surface to keep in agreement forever, and its only additions over (a) are a seek slider and larger art.
- **D4 — The in-screen controls.** (a) **All stay exactly as they are** — Play All row, per-track play buttons, per-block queue buttons, Intentions chips · (b) shrink or retire some now that the dock exists. **Recommendation: (a)** — the card buttons are how tracks are *chosen*; the dock only *transports* the current one. Retiring them would make the dock the only way to pause a card-started track from its own screen and would re-open v320's just-settled Play All row.
- **D5 — The sleep timer's place.** (a) **Stays in the Play All row** (v320's shape, shipped tonight) — the dock carries no sleep control in this release; one tap on the dock reaches the row · (b) a sleep button joins the dock (and the row keeps its own). **Recommendation: (a)** — the label-is-state pattern addresses a single `#sleep-btn` by id today; two live sleep labels are a sync obligation this release does not need. If John finds himself wanting it in the dock after use, that is a named follow-on, not a silent widening here.
- **D6 — The dock vs full-screen sheets.** (a) **Sheets cover the dock** (dock z-order: above the tab bar, below sheets) — the Read view and the editors open over it, as Spotify's mini-player is covered by its own modals; the Read view carries its own play/pause wired to the same element · (b) the dock floats above everything, sheets included. **Recommendation: (a)** — the Read view is a reading surface with its own control; a bar floating over its text costs reading room and buys nothing.
- **D7 — Intentions-tab chip plays.** (a) **The dock shows for them too** — title "Intentions", no next (there is no queue), play/pause working · (b) the dock appears only for Prayers-tab queue/track plays. **Recommendation: (a)** — one rule ("loaded ⇒ dock") with no special case; a segment started from the Intentions tab is exactly the case where John then walks to Examination with audio playing.

### Acceptance criteria

1. Before anything has loaded, no dock is rendered on any tab and no screen loses space to it; after the first load (card, Play All, block queue, Read view, or Intentions chip), the dock stands on all four tabs naming the loaded track and its block.
2. The dock's play/pause pauses and resumes the same audio the cards control; the dock, the cards, and the Read view never disagree about whether the current track is playing (one state, read from the element and the standing handlers).
3. The dock's progress line tracks the playing track's position and total, updating as `timeupdate` fires, and resets correctly at every track change.
4. Next from the dock advances by the standing queue rules — missing tracks passed over and named on their cards as today; at queue end (and at a lone track's end) the dock clears.
5. Tapping the dock's body lands on the Prayers tab with the current track's card in view; playback (position, queue, sleep state) is untouched by the tap.
6. With the dock visible, every tab's content scrolls fully clear of the dock and the tab bar; the tab bar's safe-area padding is preserved; with the dock hidden, the standing 96px clearance is exactly what stands (no dead band).
7. The sleep timer behaves exactly as v320 shipped — arm, countdown label in the Play All row, fade, timed stop holding the queue, end-of-track hold — with the dock present, and the dock's presence changes none of v320's harness results.
8. After v318 lands: opening the Read view covers the dock; its play/pause drives the same track; closing it returns to the dock exactly as it stood.
9. **John's phone check at Review** (the pinned criterion, the v312/v320 mechanism): on the Review candidate deployment, on his Z Fold 8 — the dock renders at a comfortable size with its text at the app's reading floor, its buttons thumb-sized, the safe area correct, and a full listen (start on Prayers, walk every tab, pause and resume from the dock, tap through to the day screen) feels right. ### 2.2 DOM and state design (ui.ts + client.ts)

- **Element:** one shell-level element added to `SHELL_HTML` in ui.ts, between `<audio id="audio">` and `<nav class="tabbar">`, rendered once at boot, never part of any tab render:
  `<div id="dock" class="dock hidden"><img class="dockthumb" src="/logo.png" alt=""><div class="docktext"><div class="docktitle" id="dock-title"></div><div class="dockmeta" id="dock-meta"></div></div><button id="dock-play" class="playbtn" aria-label="Play or pause"></button><button id="dock-next" class="docknext" aria-label="Next track">&#9197;</button><div class="dockprogress"><i id="dock-progress-fill"></i></div></div>`
- **Contents per D2:** logo thumb · title (17px card-title step) · meta line (14px meta step: the track's block, else the stored day's label) · play/pause (the standing 42px `.playbtn` roundel) · next · the progress line as a 3px track pinned to the dock's bottom edge. Text floor per §1 item 9 — nothing under 13px.
- **Position and z-order:** `.dock { position: fixed; left:0; right:0; z-index: 40; background: var(--navy); color: var(--cream); }` — above the tab bar (z-30), below sheets (z-50, D6), clear of the sticky brand bar (top, z-30, never overlaps). The dock's `bottom` is **measured, not hardcoded**: at boot and on `resize`, client.ts sets `--tabh` on `:root` from `tabbar.offsetHeight` (which already includes the safe-area padding), and the dock sits at `bottom: var(--tabh)`. The tab bar itself is untouched — its safe-area padding, markup, and CSS stand byte-for-byte.
- **Clearance arithmetic (the number §1 left to Plan):** while the dock stands, `--dockh` is set from `dock.offsetHeight` (expected 60px: 56px row + 3px progress + hairline) and APP_CSS gains `body.dock-on .view { padding-bottom: calc(96px + var(--dockh)); }`. Dock hidden ⇒ `body.dock-on` absent ⇒ the standing 96px pad is exactly what stands (criterion 6, no dead band). Both values are measured at runtime, so a safe-area or fold change re-derives them; the harness asserts the arithmetic, not a constant.
- **State machine — derived, no new flag:** the dock is visible ⟺ `state.current !== null` (D1, §1 fact 8). One function, `updateDock()`, reads `state.current` + `audioEl.paused` and renders title/meta/glyph/progress/next-disabled, toggling `.hidden` and `body.dock-on`. It is called from: `startDesc` · the element's own `play` and `pause` events (these two cover every path, Media Session included) · `advanceQueue` (advance and the end-clear) · the `ended` handler · `sleepTrackEnd` (all three sub-cases) · the Read view's post-open `markPlaying` point. The dock's play button carries **its own id and no `data-playkey`** — §1's named trap: `markPlaying`'s document-wide sweep must never own the dock's glyph; `updateDock` is the dock's only sync path. `updateDock` tolerates absence by construction (the element always exists; only its content changes).
- **Progress:** the standing `timeupdate` listener gains one line — the fill's width % = `audioEl.currentTime / audioEl.duration * 100` (0 when duration is not finite yet), reset to 0 by `updateDock` at every track change. **Display-only (D2):** no click, drag, or key handler is attached to the progress line; seek stays on the lock screen, unchanged.
- **Descriptor widening (client-internal, no payload change):** `trackDesc` gains `block: t._block || ''`. For Intentions segment descriptors the episodeId already encodes the block (`seg:<block>`); for chip plays (§2.3) block is empty and the meta line falls back to `state.dateLabel`.

### 2.3 Behavior per surface

- **Prayers:** dock and in-screen controls both live; agreement is structural — both drive the same `audioEl` through `playTrack`/`advanceQueue`, and both glyph sets re-derive from the element's events (`markPlaying` for cards, `updateDock` for the dock). Play All, per-block queue buttons, per-card buttons, and the Sleep button are **unchanged** (D4, D5).
- **Intentions tab:** chip plays call `play(url, btn)` → `playTrack({episodeId: 'url:…', title: 'Intentions', …})` — `state.current` sets, so the dock appears with title **"Intentions"**, meta = the day label, and **next disabled** (no queue stands) (D7). The dock's play/pause toggles that same descriptor through `playTrack(state.current)`.
- **Examination · Confession:** no surface change of their own; the dock is the first playback sign and pause control on either tab (the §1 gap). Nothing in their renders is touched.
- **Read view open:** the sheet (z-50) covers the dock (z-40) (D6); its own play button drives the same descriptor; closing the sheet reveals the dock exactly as `updateDock` last rendered it.
- **Dock transport:** play/pause = `playTrack(state.current)` — one standing path, inheriting the pause handler's resume save and v320's fade-restore-on-play without re-implementation. Next = `advanceQueue(1)` with its standing skip rule (missing tracks passed over and named on their cards); next renders disabled when `state.queue` is empty.

### 2.4 The jump (D3)

- Dock body click (any target inside `#dock` except `#dock-play` / `#dock-next`): **no-op when `state.current.episodeId` starts with `url:`** (an Intentions chip play has no card). Otherwise: `setTab('prayers')` (which synchronously re-renders the day screen), then in the next animation frame `document.querySelector('[data-card="' + episodeId + '"]')` — the v318-landed card identity — gets `.scrollIntoView({block: 'center', behavior: 'smooth'})` plus a transient outline class so the eye lands on it.
- **No-op cases, named:** chip plays (above) · the playing track is not among today's rendered cards (e.g. a resumed stored-day track whose card the current render does not carry) — the tab switch still happens; the scroll simply finds nothing. Playback, position, queue, and sleep state are never touched by the jump.

### 2.5 Sleep interplay (v320, D5)

- The dock carries **no sleep control**; `#sleep-btn` stays the single label-is-state element in the Play All row.
- **Timed stop:** `fireTimedStop` pauses only (queue held, `state.current` stands) — the element's `pause` event fires `updateDock`, so the dock shows the loaded track in the paused state with its progress where it stopped. No dock-specific sleep code.
