### 2.3 Seasonal depth — the day's color AND the day's name, at full-screen scale

The honest mechanism, stated plainly first: **the app holds no season data and must not compute any.** The payload's `liturgicalDay` is `{name, color}` — there is no season field (v352 §2.4), and Decision 4 + the official-day pin forbid the app from deriving one. Seasonal depth is therefore built from what the derivation already sends, at the scale this surface allows:

1. **The color at depth.** The hero is the app's largest themed surface — the day's gradient at full-screen width, where the bars show the same treatment in a band and v353's headers show it in a strip. As the year turns, the field turns with it because the nightly derivation names the color: violet through Advent and Lent, white through Christmas and Easter, red on martyrs and feasts of the Lord, green through Ordinary Time, rose on Gaudete and Laetare. The depth is the treatment's reach, not a new signal.
2. **The name on the surface.** The hero carries the day's name as its own line (`.np-litday`): the `state.liturgical.name` exactly as the derivation gives it (e.g. "Memorial of Saint Leo the Great"), 13px (the floor), letter-spacing .4px (the `.litday` language), color `var(--bar-sub)`, centered, wrapping never truncating (a truncated liturgical name is a wrong name — v352's rule). It paints in `openNowPlaying` from the standing `state.liturgical` and re-paints in `updateNowPlaying` only when the name changes (a one-line guard — the day cannot change under an open sheet except across the midnight re-render, where the sheet is rebuilt anyway; the guard keeps the sync honest at zero cost). Absent record → the line is not rendered (§2.1.5).
3. **What depth is NOT (named, so nobody builds it):** no season computation from the date, no season name invented from the day name, no motif or imagery per season, no second data source. A true season signal (a season field in the liturgical record, a motif set) is a payload change and a scope amendment — Concerns carries it as John's to call, not C's to build.
