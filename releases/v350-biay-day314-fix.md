## 1 · Scope

**The defect (John's report, 2026-11-10 ~04:14 CT).** On Tuesday 2026-11-10 the Bible in a Year slot in the Morning block read "Not available today", although the episode exists: the Fireside feed's newest 2026 item is "Day 314: My Beloved Son (2026)" (published 2026-11-10 03:15 -0500), and the Spotify Morning Praylist carried Bible Day 314 (2026), verified by the 02:30 CT watchdog after a re-run. On 2026-11-09, Day 313 verified fine in Morning.

**Diagnosis — with proof (2026-11-10).**

1. **The matcher is innocent.** The shipped client functions (`extParseFeed` + `extMatchBible`, extracted verbatim from `client.ts` at trunk 3560c16) were run in headless Chromium against the LIVE Fireside feed bytes (9,187,427 bytes, 1,870 items): Day 314 matches and the display title "Bible in a Year — Day 314: My Beloved Son" builds; Day 313 matches identically. The Day 314 item is structurally identical to Day 313's (same title form, same enclosure host chain). The enclosure chain (podtrac 302 → aphid.fireside.fm) and the feed both send `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` at every hop (curl-verified), so the direct leg is not CORS-blocked either.
2. **The parse is expensive.** The same parse measured 749 ms desktop-class and **5,001 ms at 6× CPU throttle** (phone-class) — the parse alone, before any rendering.
3. **The shell is correct.** The agent-door day read for 2026-11-10 presents the right day, and the settings row stands (sourceKey bible-in-a-year, Morning, sortOrder 5, enabled, slotId ext-bible-in-a-year, role primary, dayRule daily). Spotify playlists and the refresh Worker are verified good and untouched.
4. **The mechanism — two standing fragilities, either sufficient.** (a) **Single on-device leg.** The Bible slot was the app's largest on-device feed read (9.2 MB / 1,870 items) under the 8 s fetch abort plus an on-device DOM parse, with NO Worker fallback: `externalsLivePayload` carried no Bible entry (Divine Office slots + the v339 courier class + the v348 exegesis leg only); the Worker's identical `extMatchBible` served only the audio pass-through route. Any fetch, abort, parse, or stale-copy failure settled the slot named-missing. This is the same defect class v348 fixed for the exegesis slot. (b) **Watchdog false-missing.** Under Amendment A1 (download always) a matched slot must download its full enclosure (31 MB for Day 314) before it reads present; the 9 s resolve watchdog settled any uncached slot still 'downloading' as named-missing — a false "Not available today" while a healthy download was in flight, and it stands for the open if the app closes or the download is torn first. Reproduced deterministically on the pristine tree by harness-v350 leg L2: the slot stood 'downloading' at 385 ms, read **missing at 9,478 ms**, and settled present at 15,548 ms when the drip-fed download landed.
5. **Not the mechanism:** the day-number rule. The app's standing pin (Day N = day-of-year capped at 365, current-year edition only — harness-v334 leg 7) is satisfied by the feed's 2026 item; the pin is not implicated and is not changed. (The brief's "prior-year acceptable" is the Spotify-playlist watchdog's rule, not the app's.)

**The fix (smallest that removes both mechanisms).**

- **Worker courier leg for Bible in a Year** (`worker/src/index.ts`): `externalsLivePayload` gains a `bibleP` leg — settings-gated on the ext-bible-in-a-year primary row, started before the Divine Office fetch so it runs in parallel — resolving via the standing Worker machinery (`extFetchFeedItems`, 15 s server budget, `extMatchBible`). A present entry carries the item's RAW title, streamUrl = the item enclosure, and the item's duration/size; it spreads into both return paths.
- **Client courier slot** (`worker/src/client.ts`): the Bible entry in EXT_SLOTS gains `source: 'Bible in a Year'` and `courier: true`. At courier settle the display title is built by the standing rule — `extBibleTitle({title: e.title}, extMatchBibleDayN(date))` — so one title rule stands, computed client-side from the raw item title. **The direct on-device leg is deleted** (feed URL, browser fetch, and the client copy of `extMatchBible`); the Worker matcher is now the single source. Downloads are unchanged (direct-first, pass-through second route).
- **Watchdog** (`worker/src/client.ts`): at the 9 s mark an uncached slot still 'loading' settles named-missing as before; a slot in 'downloading' is NOT settled — a **long-stop at +75 s** settles any uncached slot still downloading at that point (75 s at 31 MB ≈ 3.3 Mbps floor; a slower link still downloads in the background, and the cache listener settles the card present when the download lands). Cached-slot behavior unchanged.
- **Service worker pin** (`worker/src/ui.ts`): `ora-pro-nobis-v350-1`.

**No migration.** Settings rows, the day cache shape, and the download machinery are unchanged.
