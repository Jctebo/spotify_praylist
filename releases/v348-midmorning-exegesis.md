### 2.3 The exegesis robustness design — PINNED: Worker-courier resolution + same-route download retry

The design is chosen and pinned; Implement does not re-open it. The phone stops resolving this slot altogether; the Worker resolves it, through machinery that already exists and already carries three slots (v339's courier class). Client-led in shape (the settle path, cache, and download all stay in the client), one small Worker addition, no new route, no new matcher, no settings or contract change.

- **(a) Worker — the pick joins the live payload.** In `externalsLivePayload` (index.ts:752–798), Implement adds ONE concurrent leg for `ext-gospel-exegesis`, modeled on `extCourierSlotsPayload` (index.ts:721–750): the leg starts as a promise BEFORE the Divine Office fetch (so route latency stays bounded by the slowest single leg, never by a sum — the v339 concurrency rule), and its entry is included in BOTH return branches (the DO-failure branch at index.ts:787–788 and the success branch at index.ts:797). The leg: (i) reads the slot's primary settings row from the `prim` map the function already builds; if the row is absent, disabled, or its day rule fails `extDayRulePasses` for the presented weekday, the leg contributes NO entry (the slot does not stand today — same rule as every courier slot); (ii) otherwise it fetches via the existing `extFetchFeedItems(env.EXT_DGE_FEED_URL || EXT_DGE_FEED_URL)` (index.ts:675 — the 15-second server-side abort, against the phone's 8-second `extFetchText`, client.ts:1676) and picks via the existing Worker `extMatchLatestByDate(items, y, mo, d)` (index.ts:643) — the identical function the `/api/player/externals/audio` pass-through already uses for this slot (index.ts:~1103), so the live pick and the pass-through pick are the same pick BY CONSTRUCTION (the v337 parity property, preserved); (iii) on a pick, the entry is `{ slotId: 'ext-gospel-exegesis', state: 'present', title: <the ITEM's title>, streamUrl: it.url, durationSec: it.durationS, sizeBytes: it.length, block: row.block, sortOrder: row.sort_order }`; on no pick or any fetch/parse failure, the entry is the same shape with `state: 'missing'`, `streamUrl: null`, zeroed numbers, and `title: 'Daily Gospel Exegesis'`. TITLE RULE (named deviation from the courier-class shape, deliberate): the courier-class entries carry their slot's fixed title because those slots display slot titles; the exegesis displays its EPISODE title (the standing direct leg settles `it.title`, client.ts:2628), so this leg's present entry carries the item's title — the card and queue show the same words as a successful direct resolve shows today. No other slot's entry changes; the Divine Office and courier-class legs are untouched; the route's presented-day gate, session posture, and `cache-control: private, max-age=300` (index.ts:1050–1067) are untouched; nothing is stored server-side.
- **(b) Client — the slot becomes courier-class; the direct leg is deleted.** In `EXT_SLOTS` (client.ts:1468) the exegesis entry gains `courier: true` and KEEPS `matcher: 'latest-by-release-date'`, `feedUrl`, and `download: 'passthrough'` verbatim (the matcher/feedUrl fields become documentation of the contract the Worker port executes — the pass-through parity branch keys on the slot id server-side, not on these fields). Consequences, all intended: the phone never downloads the 3.16 MB / 520-item Anchor feed and never `DOMParser`-parses it for this slot; the slot settles through the standing courier settle block (client.ts:2588–2610) with its standing rules untouched — `settleMeta` on a present entry, the cache-stand rule (a cached present record stands over a same-open courier miss), `missingSt` on a missing entry. - **(c) Client — the download gets its second attempt, on the only route that exists.** In `extDownload` (client.ts:2463–2470), the passthrough branch's routes array becomes `[passthrough, passthrough]`, so the standing attempt chain (`attempt.catch(… tryRoute(routes[1]) …)`, client.ts:~2487) retries the SAME Worker pass-through once on any failure of the first attempt's whole chain (fetch, body read, or cache write).
