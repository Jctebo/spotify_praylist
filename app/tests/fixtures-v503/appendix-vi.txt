APPENDIX VI — SAMPLE INVOCATIONS FOR THE PENITENTIAL ACT
Source capture: the USCCB-published Appendix VI PDF (Roman Missal, [In the Dioceses of the United States]).
Sets I, III, IV, VI: carried byte-identical from the v502 capture (app/tests/fixtures-v502/appendix-vi.txt, fetched 2026-11-17).
Set II: added at v503 (2026-11-17) from a re-fetch of the SAME PDF — its sets I, III, IV, VI match the v502
capture word-for-word, line for line, in the same extraction. Witness 2: USCCB Divine Worship newsletter,
Aug-Sept 2010 (all 21 tropes, same order); an independent parish printing of the appendix's sets
(hspal.org, "The Forms of the Penitential Rite") carries set II's three tropes word-for-word.
Lines below are the spoken lines of each set in print order:
trope (Priest) · acclamation (Priest) · response (people), three times.
Note: the PDF text extraction attaches a stray colon to the leader's 2nd/3rd acclamations
(the colon of the following "The people:" table label); the printed acclamation ends with a period,
as the first acclamation of every set shows and as the diocesan typeset witness prints it.
