APPENDIX VI — SAMPLE INVOCATIONS FOR THE PENITENTIAL ACT
Source capture: the USCCB-published Appendix VI PDF (Roman Missal, [In the Dioceses of the United States]),
fetched 2026-11-17. Lines below are the spoken lines of each set in print order:
trope (Priest) · acclamation (Priest) · response (people), three times.
Witness 2: USCCB Divine Worship newsletter, Aug-Sept 2010 (all 21 tropes, same order).
Note: the PDF text extraction attaches a stray colon to the leader's 2nd/3rd acclamations
(the colon of the following "The people:" table label); the printed acclamation ends with a period,
as the first acclamation of every set shows and as the diocesan typeset witness prints it.
