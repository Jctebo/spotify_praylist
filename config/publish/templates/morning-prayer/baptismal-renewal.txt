Baptismal Renewal - O Jesus,
in whom I was made a new creation through Holy Baptism,
receive my life anew this day.

Make me a living offering of praise
to the glory of the Father.

I renounce sin and Satan,
and I renew the promises of my baptism:
to live in the freedom of the children of God,
to reject the empty promises of evil,
and to follow You in faith, hope, and charity.

May the grace of my baptism bear fruit
in every word, thought, and action this day;
and grant that I may persevere in grace
unto life everlasting.

Amen.
