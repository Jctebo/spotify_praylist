Lord, have mercy.

Christ, have mercy.

pray for us.

pray for us.

pray for us.

pray for us.

pray for us.
Saint John Paul II,
pray for us.
Saint Teresa of Calcutta,
pray for us.
Saint Elizabeth of Hungary,
pray for us.
Saint Sebastian,
pray for us.
Saint Pier Giorgio Frassati,
pray for us.
Saint Mark,
pray for us.
Saint Angela Merici,
pray for us.
Saint John the Apostle,
pray for us.
Saint Josemaría Escrivá,
pray for us.
Saint Carlo Acutis,
pray for us.
