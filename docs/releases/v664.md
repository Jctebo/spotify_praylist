### §2.3 The sheet system today

Every sheet except Now Playing mounts through `mountDrawer(sheet, tall)` (v419): the `.sheet` scrim (a tap on the overlay dismisses) + `.inner` panel (card ground, radius-16 top corners), a prepended `.grabstrip`, head/body/foot split, px heights — FIT `max-height 85dvh` or TALL `height 92dvh` — and a grab drag whose only outcomes are dismiss (≥25% of panel height) or snap back. v641's discipline bounds menu/sheet placement by the dock and tab bar.

**Why the queue sheet does not mount through `mountDrawer`** (the brief's named exception, grounded): its drag contract is two-stop — rest at half, drag to full, settle back — and `mountDrawer`'s drag knows only dismiss-or-snap-back, and its heights only the two fixed classes. Forcing the two-stop drag into it would bend every other drawer's mount. The queue sheet therefore stands on the same DRESS (`.sheet` scrim + `.inner` + `.grabstrip`/`.grabpill`, the v419 tokens, the v641 bounds) with its own mount (§3.2) — one new mount for one new drag contract, no change to `mountDrawer` or any standing drawer.

### §2.4 The native leg

Queue edits already re-send the queue to the Android engine at current when it stands engaged (`queueActFinish645`). Reorder and remove ride that exact leg — no contract change, no APK work. The v661 completion drain resolves by queue index with a mediaUri cross-check: a reorder/remove changes indices, so the drain's cross-check is what keeps a finish mark honest after an edit — the harness asserts a completion record drained after a reorder still marks the track its mediaUri names (§5).

## §3 The build

### §3.1 The card fits one page (`ui.ts` + `openNowPlaying`)

- `.sheet.np-sheet` stops scrolling: `overflow:hidden`, and its `.inner` becomes a flex column at the full sheet height (`height:100%`), the hero's fixed rows `flex:none`, the artwork the one flexible element.
- **The artwork is sized to the page**: `.np-art` takes a CSS size var — `--art: clamp(88px, min(72vw, calc(100dvh - <budget>)), 340px)`; `width:var(--art); height:var(--art)` (the square stands; the glyph tile's initial font scales with the box). `<budget>` is the measured height of everything else on the card (grab + hero padding + title + meta + intention + litday + seek + transport + controls + the queue door + sheet padding); Implement measures it against the harness viewports and sets the constant — expected ≈ 470px. On a tall phone the 340px cap binds (today's size); on a standard phone the dvh term binds; on a short phone the artwork keeps shrinking.
- **Second lever for short viewports**: a `@media (max-height: 660px)` step compresses the hero's vertical padding and the seek/transport margins, and the intention line clamps to two lines (ellipsis), so the 88px floor never pushes an element off the page. The title clamps to two lines likewise.
- **The inline queue leaves the card**: the `.npq-head` + `#np-queue` block in `openNowPlaying`'s HTML is replaced by **the queue door** (§3.2). Transport and bottom-row markup, order, and seats are byte-stable (gate d) — their HTML strings do not change; only the queue block below them is swapped, and CSS above them adds no reordering.

### §3.2 The queue door + the queue sheet (`client.ts`)

- **The door**: one full-width row button at the card's foot, `#np-queue-door`, in the `.npq-head` dress: "Queue" · the pos line (`#np-queue-pos` — "Track X of N", or the next-up title when a queue stands; empty-queue reads "Queue" alone) · a chevron. It stands whether or not a queue stands (a single play's sheet shows the single-play line, §3.3). `updateNowPlaying` and every queue edit refresh its line via the new `renderQueueDoor()` split out of `renderQueueList` (§3.4).
- **The sheet**: `openQueueSheet()` builds a `.sheet.npq-sheet` sibling overlay (appended after the player sheet; it is NOT an `.np-sheet`, so the standing `.np-sheet` queries in `updateNowPlaying`/`renderQueueList` stay unambiguous) with the standing dress: scrim + `.inner` + grab strip. Geometry: **rest height 50dvh; full height 92dvh** (the standing TALL bound — the v641-consistent cap; the scrim line stays visible at full). The list body scrolls inside the panel (`.panelbody` discipline); head and grab stay fixed.
- **The drag**: pointer drag on the grab strip moves the panel height live, clamped between 50dvh and 92dvh (a drag below rest height from the half stop translates toward dismiss, per the standing drawer feel). On release: past the midpoint (71dvh) → snap to full; below → snap back to half; from half, a downward drag of ≥25% of the panel height dismisses (the standing `mountDrawer` threshold). Snaps animate on a height transition. A tap on the grab strip does nothing (no accidental geometry change); the scrim tap dismisses (standing `.sheet` behaviour, wired the same way `mountDrawer` wires it). Reopening always starts at half — "settles back to half" across opens, and a snap-back within one open.
- **Dismissal leaves the player untouched**: the sheet is removed; no player state, queue state, or playback is written by opening, dragging, or closing the sheet. The NP sheet beneath never re-renders for the sheet's sake.

### §3.3 Inside the sheet — paint and acts

- **Head**: the sounding track stands first — a pinned row in the queue-row dress marked playing (title · block · duration · played dot), labelled by the head line "Queue" + the pos text. It carries no reorder handle and no remove act.
- **Coming rows**: `state.queue` rows after `queueIdx`, in order, each a queue row carrying: tap target (the row body) · a drag handle (the `.favhandle` pattern — 44px grip, `touch-action:none`) · a remove act (a ✕ button at the row's right, 44px target). Rows before `queueIdx` (already played) do not paint — Spotify's shape, and the gate's "the sounding track heads the sheet".
- **Tap-to-play**: a coming row's tap runs the standing `jumpQueueTo(absoluteIndex)` — indices painted on rows are the absolute `state.queue` indices, so no index translation stands between the sheet and the engine.
- **Drag reorder**: pointer drag on a row's handle lifts the row (the `.favreorder-row.dragging` dress), tracks the pointer, and on release commits the new order among the coming rows only. By construction `queueIdx` and every played row are untouched (the actable range is `queueIdx+1 … end`), so the sounding track's identity and index never move. A drag that ends where it started writes nothing.
- **Remove**: ✕ drops that one coming row from `state.queue`. The sounding row and played rows cannot be removed here; Clear queue (player menu) remains the wholesale act.
- **The edit funnel**: reorder and remove both close through the standing queue-edit funnel — mutate `state.queue` → `saveQueueSnapshot()` → `renderQueueList()` (repaints the sheet if it stands) → `updateDock()` → `queueActFinish645`'s native leg (`nativeSendNow()` → `nativeResendAtCurrent()`), with `opnTouchRestored649()` first, exactly as `addToQueue` does. No new persistence, no new contract.
- **Empty states**: no queue (single play) — head row + "No queue — playing a single prayer." (today's line, carried verbatim). Queue exhausted (current is the last row) — head row + "Nothing up next." No current — the sheet cannot open (the door stands only on the player card, which requires a current).

### §3.4 `renderQueueList` re-point

Today it queries `.np-sheet` and paints `#np-queue` inside the player. After this release: it paints the SHEET's list when the queue sheet stands (its box is the sheet's `#npq-list`), refreshes the door line in all cases (`renderQueueDoor()`), and no-ops safely when neither stands. Its preview branch (painting a preview queue into the preview sheet's `#np-queue`) stands untouched — the paint target resolves in order: queue sheet list → a standing `#np-queue` (the preview sheet's). Every standing caller (`addToQueue`, `jumpQueueTo`'s neighbours, boot restore, `standRestoredQueue`, track-change renders) keeps working by that resolution; the harness covers the boot-restore and add paths with the sheet closed and open.
