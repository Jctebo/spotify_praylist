## §2 Grounding — how the prayer-source settings stand today

All claims grounded firsthand on the shipped client (production pin ora-pro-nobis-v631-1; `app/worker/src/client.ts` on main, tree of the v631 ship).

### 2.1 The Settings page, in standing order

`renderSettings` / `paintSettings` paint, top to bottom: the page sub ("Playback, offline copies, and where each prayer's audio comes from.") · **PRAYER PLAN** (the Configure plan door) · **PLAYBACK** (Autoplay · Intentions) · **OFFLINE** (clear downloads) · **MY DATA ON THIS PHONE** (device shape only) · **SACRED ART** (a pointer to the tab) · the **Source mapping** table (§2.3) with its Reset / Save mapping acts · and at the very foot, `<div id="pin-section">` — the **CONFESSION PIN** section, filled by `fillPinSection`.

### 2.2 The v628 preference: seat, wording, shape, storage

- **Seat:** `fillPinSection` paints the Sing the Hours card (`sthHtml628`) **inside the CONFESSION PIN section**, below the PIN state card, the vault unlock card, and the v622 fingerprint-preference card. It carries no heading of its own.
- **Exact wording today:** "For Morning Prayer and Evening Prayer, **Divine Office** stands first on this phone. The other source stands as the backup and plays when the first does not load." (the standing source's name swaps with the preference) — followed by ONE ghost button naming the opposite action: "Put Sing the Hours first" / "Put Divine Office first" (`#pin-sth-pref`). Both options are never visible together; the standing choice is stated only in the sentence.
- **Standing condition:** the card stands only when `st.pinSet && opnMode() === 'device'` — a PIN is set and the app runs in the device shape. On web the whole PIN section is the pointer card (v623), so the choice does not stand there at all.
- **Storage:** the preference is the field `sthPrimary` **on the on-device PIN record** — the same record that carries v622's `biometricPref` — read by `opnSthPrimary628()` (absent/false = the default, Divine Office first) and written by the card's button through `opnPinSave`. A prayer-audio preference is kept in the security record because that is the one store every surface can read while locked.
- **Effect:** the server watcher stands both pointers for the two slots; `opnExtChoice628` applies the user's order on the device at settle time — the painted title, the named source, and the played pointer all follow the standing-first source, and a failed load of the first choice retries the backup. The preference applies on the phone **at once** — unlike the mapping (§2.3), whose sub-line reads "Changes apply from the next nightly push." Nothing on the page states either clock.

### 2.3 The source roles as seeded, and the mapping table

- `OPN_SEED_SOURCES` seeds 26 sources. The Divine Office rows stand **role "primary"** on all eight office slots (Invitatory · Morning · Midmorning · Midday · Midafternoon · Office of Readings · Evening · Night). The Sing the Hours rows — `sth-lauds` on `ext-morning-prayer`, `sth-vespers` on `ext-evening-prayer` — stand **role "fallback"** with **dayRule "fallback-only"**: the only two fallback rows in the seed, on the only two hours Sing the Hours carries.
- **Role is fixed seed data.** No surface edits it. In the mapping table it surfaces only as the row's family line: `settingsFamilyLabel` paints "Sing the Hours — fallback only" for those two rows.
- The **Source mapping table** groups every source by block (`SET_SECTIONS`: Morning · Midmorning · Midday · Afternoon · Evening · Night · Mass and Homilies · Novenas · The AI Signal · No block · Off) and gives EVERY row the same live controls: an On/Off toggle, a block select, a day-rule select (Daily · Weekdays only · Weekends only · Sundays only · Fridays only · Fallback only), and up/down order buttons — saved as one mapping through `/api/player/source-settings`. The two Sing the Hours rows carry the full control set, yet none of those controls can express the one choice a user actually has over them — which source stands first. That choice stands in §2.2's buried card, and neither surface names or points at the other.

### 2.4 The confusion points (the package's §2, on the evidence above)

1. **Wrong room:** the only control that decides which source plays first for Morning and Evening Prayer lives under the CONFESSION PIN heading, at the foot of the page — a user looking for prayer settings has no reason to look there.
2. **Hidden unless a PIN is set:** a user with no PIN never sees the choice at all, though it has nothing to do with the PIN.
3. **A button that names the other choice:** the control shows only the action ("Put Sing the Hours first"), never both options with the standing one marked.
4. **Two systems, one job:** the Source mapping rows and the buried choice both appear to govern where Morning and Evening Prayer audio comes from, and neither points at the other.
5. **Fixed labels beside live controls:** "fallback only" reads as a setting in the mapping table, but it is fixed — the live choice stands in another section entirely.

### 2.5 The package's decisions (package 91; recommended option first in each)

- **Q1 `seat`** — Where should the choice live? **A (recommended):** its own MORNING & EVENING PRAYER section, high in Settings (after PRAYER PLAN), out of the CONFESSION PIN section entirely · B: stay in the PIN section under its own heading · C: inside Prayer Profiles (Configure plan).
- **Q2 `control`** — What shape should the control take? **A (recommended):** two options always visible, the standing one marked, each with its backup named · B: keep today's single verb button.
- **Q3 `availability`** — Who should see and change it? **A (recommended):** every user of the phone, PIN or no PIN — the choice moves off the PIN record into the phone's own settings, like Autoplay · B: as today, only when a PIN is set.
- **Q4 `mapping`** — What happens to the Sing the Hours rows in the mapping table? **A (recommended):** they stay for placement and order, but their label names the standing choice ("stands as the backup" / "stands first") instead of "fallback only" · B: remove the two rows from the table · C: leave the table exactly as it stands.
- **Q5 `wording`** — What words should the new section use? **A (recommended):** plain words only — which source plays first, and that the other plays when the first does not load; no "primary" or "fallback" in the section · B: keep the "primary / backup" terms.
- **Run level:** recommended **Stop before Ship**.

### 3.1 The new store (Q3) — one helper, a first-read seed, the PIN field retires

Its only production readers are `opnExtChoice628` (the settle-time choice) and the retiring Settings card. New body:
  1. If `OPN_STH_FIRST_KEY` stands, return it (`'on'` → true).
  2. **First read (the seed):** read the PIN record via `opnPinLoad()`; the effective value is `!!(rec && rec.sthPrimary === true)` — exactly today's read, so the carry-over is byte-exact by construction. - **The write helper:** `opnSthFirstSet632(sthFirst)` writes only the new key — never the PIN record. The Settings control (§3.2) is its only caller.
