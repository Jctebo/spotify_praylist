# v650 — Both office episodes as separate tracks; paint by a drop-down setting (John's redesign)

## §1 The item (John, 2026-11-24 13:34–13:37 CT)

Three field failures of the one-record choice design stand
behind this release: v648's choice changed the title in the
field while the audio stayed Divine Office; v649 grounded
two causes (the adoption trap · the source-blind audio
cache) and fixed both — and the field still failed
("Still doesn't work", 13:34 CT). John's word replaces the
design instead of patching it, verbatim:

> "Still doesn't work. Can you pull both episodes in
> seperate tracks and paint one or both depending on
> settings. Change from button to drop down"

Two amendments landed the same minutes and stand settled
in this order: a check-box control was proposed (13:36 CT)
and then WITHDRAWN by John's word (13:36 CT) — "It can be
a drop down like u said. That's fine." The control is the
DROP DOWN as originally briefed; no check boxes are built.

**The design as directed:** for each labeled office slot
(Morning Prayer · Evening Prayer) the app pulls BOTH
episodes — the Divine Office episode and the Sing the
Hours episode — as SEPARATE TRACKS, each standing on its
own record whole (its original title, its stream, its
image, its duration — the server already resolves both:
the standing record and the backup record on the day's
`ext_resolutions` row, both served in the live payload).
A drop-down setting decides the paint, three values:
'Divine Office' · 'Sing the Hours' · 'Both'. Default:
'Sing the Hours' (John's standing preference). Under
'Both', Sing the Hours stands first, then Divine Office.
The drop down stands where the v637 choice chips stand —
the Edit form, under the profile chips. The v632 store
(`opn-sth-first-v632`) and the choice machinery built on
it retire into the new three-value setting; migration at
first read: StH-first → 'Sing the Hours' · DO-first →
'Divine Office'.

**Disciplines that stand:** each track plays its own
stream — no re-labeling, no backup swap, no pointer choice
at play time; the v649 enclosure stamp binds each track's
saved bytes to its own enclosure; per-track played marks
and downloads follow the v610 occurrence discipline; the
block's Play disc queues what paints, in paint order;
unlabeled slots and the Invitatory are untouched. **No new
APK** — every seam below is client-JS; the Worker already
serves both records and the per-source pass-through.

## §2 Grounding — the shipped seams (base = pin ora-pro-nobis-v649-1)

Read firsthand from `app/worker/src/client.ts` on the base
tree. Line numbers are the base tree's.

### 2.1 Where the labeled descriptor is born

One record per slot stands in `state.extSlots[slotId]`.
The courier leg (~20546) applies `opnExtChoice628(slot, e,
src)` to the served entry `e` and settles ONE side — the
choice's — through `settleMeta` → `extDownload` → `settle`;
the other side rides the settled record as a pointer-only
`backup` object. `extRebuildDescs`' `emitExt619` (~19179)
builds exactly one descriptor per contents occurrence from
that record via `extSlotTrack(slot, st)` (~17583):
`{ episodeId: slot.slotId, title, kind: 'external',
source: st.source, streamUrl: st.streamUrl, durationS,
imageUrl, descText, _block }`, copies after the first
stamped `_occ` (the v610 discipline). Marks and intention
draws read the ONE key `descMarkKey(t)` =
`playedKeyOcc(episodeId, block, _occ)` (~4804); card/play
identity reads `descCardKey(t)` = episodeId + optional
`#occ` (~16874). Since v648 the settled record itself
stands WHOLE — standing fields + `backup { streamUrl,
source, title, imageUrl, durationSec }` — and `writeCache`
persists both sides (~20180). The second track therefore
needs no new server fact: it is born by emitting a second
descriptor from the record's backup side, under a sibling
identity (§3.3), once that side's bytes stand downloaded
(§2.2).

### 2.2 Bytes: one cache key, one mint owner per slot — both must gain the side dimension

Every labeled download writes `opn-ext-audio-v1` under
`offlineDayKey(date, slot.slotId)` = `day/<date>/<slotId>`
— ONE entry per (date, slot) (`extDownload`'s put ~20289 ·
`extRestoreCached`'s match ~20373 · the heal's match/delete
~20371 · the offline pass's item key ~20945 · the row
menu's Download-visibility read ~16711, keyed by the
descriptor's episodeId). Two tracks of one slot under this
key would mint each other's bytes — the v649 stamp compare
(`opnStampMatches649`, ~20866) binds an entry to ONE
enclosure, so the key itself must gain the side dimension
(§3.2). The same singularity stands in the mint:
`mintBlobUrl(extSlotOwner(slot), blob)` — one owner per
slot, and a mint under an owner revokes that owner's
previous URL. Two sides minting under the one owner would
revoke each other's standing blob URL; the labeled mint
owner becomes per-side (§3.2). `extDownload`'s sounding
guard (~20289ff) keys on `episodeId === slot.slotId` for
BOTH the current track and the queue — under two tracks it
still answers correctly (a sounding sibling takes the
throwaway owner), and §3.2 keeps it.

### 2.4 The engine hand-off reads the slot record, not the descriptor

`oraSnapshotTrack(d, date)` (~17792) looks the slot record
up by `state.extSlots[d.episodeId]`; its labeled branch
builds the pass-through mediaUri with `&src=` +
**`st.source`** — the SLOT record's standing source, not
the descriptor's. Under two tracks both descriptors share
the episodeId, so the sibling would be handed the standing
side's source. `extSlotTrack` already stamps the
descriptor's own `source`; the labeled branch takes `&src=`
from the DESCRIPTOR's source (§3.3). `nativeContractItem`
(~18220) is per-descriptor and positional
(`nativeContractFor`: indices stay parallel), and the
v610 duplicated hours already stand twice in one queue
under one episodeId — the two-track queue rides that
precedent.

enumerate per SIDE for the labeled slots under §3.2 (the
tomorrow record already carries the backup whole since
v648). The 9 s watchdog's cached re-download (~20600)
hands `extDownload` the standing meta only; per side
under §3.2 likewise.

## §3 The fix (numbered to the grounding)

1. **Both sides settle as sides (§2.1).** For the two
   labeled slots the courier leg no longer applies a
   choice. The slot record stands as served — standing
   fields = the served standing side — and its `backup`
   object is promoted to a full SIDE: the settle downloads
   EACH side's bytes through the standing routes (the
   side's own route order by its own source, the
   pass-through naming that side's source per the v648
   `&src=` rule) and stands each side
   `{ title, source, imageUrl, durationSec, sizeBytes,
   enclosureUrl, streamUrl (minted), state }` on the
   record: the standing side on the record's standing
   fields, the other side on `record.side2` (the cached
   `backup` object gains the same fields at `writeCache`,
   so a cold restore re-mints per side without a courier
   refetch). A side whose download fails settles THAT
   side missing — no cross-side flip. Paint rule: a
   labeled descriptor paints for each side the setting
   selects (§3.4) that stands present; if NO selected side
   stands but another side does, that side paints alone —
   a missing episode never blanks the hour (gate G6).
   The no-blank prior-record guard's labeled purpose is
   served by this rule at paint; its machinery retires
   with the hook (§3.5).
2. **Per-side byte identity (§2.2).** One helper,
   `extAudioKey650(date, slotId, source)`, returns the
   bare `offlineDayKey(date, slotId)` for unlabeled slots
   and `offlineDayKey(date, slotId + '~' + tag)` for a
   labeled side, tag = `'do'` / `'sth'` by the side's
   source (any other source on a labeled side keys by its
   own lower-cased first word — the two named sources are
   the only ones the server seats there). Every labeled
   key call site re-points to it: `extDownload`'s put ·
   `extRestoreCached`'s match/delete (per side) · the
   offline pass item key + its reuse compare · the
   tomorrow leg's items · the row menu's
   Download-visibility read and `refreshGridDlStates`
   (per descriptor side) · the prune's eviction (both side
   keys, and the bare key on sight — pre-v650 bytes under
   the bare key can never mint under a side). The v649
   stamp rule stands per side unchanged: an entry mints
   under a side only when its `x-opn-enclosure` stamp
   equals THAT side's enclosureUrl. The labeled mint owner
   becomes per-side (`extSlotOwner` keyed with the side
   tag for labeled slots), so neither side's mint revokes
   the other's blob URL.
3. **Emission and identity (§2.1, §2.4).** `emitExt619`
   emits, per contents occurrence of a labeled slot, one
   descriptor per painting side, in paint order, each
   built by `extSlotTrack` from that side's standing
   record. The FIRST-painted descriptor takes the standing
   identity exactly as today (episodeId = slotId · `_occ`
   = the contents occurrence stamp). The SECOND-painted
   descriptor additionally carries `_side650 = tag`;
   `descCardKey` appends `'@' + tag` after any `#occ`
   suffix, and `descMarkKey` appends the same suffix to
   the played key — `'@'` appears in no episode id, block
   name, or standing key, so a sibling key can never
   collide with a bare or occurrence key. Identity follows
   PAINT POSITION, not source: under a one-source setting
   the single track takes the standing identity (marks and
   draws a user already holds keep their key); under
   'Both' the first track keeps it and the second takes
   the sibling key. Marks, intention draws, row menus, and
   the unplayed count read the descriptor's own key as
   today — playing one side never marks the other.
   `extSwapCard`'s re-render sweep extends to the
   side-suffixed cards (`[data-card^="<slotId>@"]`
   alongside the standing `#` sweep), each re-rendered
   from its own side's track; a side settle swaps its own
   card and never clobbers the sibling's. The block Play
   disc and section queues consume `extRebuildDescs`'
   output as today — paint order IS queue order.
   `oraSnapshotTrack`'s labeled branch takes `&src=` from
   the descriptor's own `source` (falling back to the slot
   record's), so each track hands the engine its own
   record through the v648 pass-through seats.
4. **The drop down and the store (§2.3).** A new store,
   `opn-office-choice-v650`, carries `'do'` · `'sth'` ·
   `'both'`. Its reader `opnOfficeChoice650()` migrates at
   first read: the effective old value (read through
   `opnSthPrimary628()`, whose PIN-record seed logic is
   the standing precedent) maps StH-first → `'sth'`,
   DO-first → `'do'`; with nothing standing anywhere the
   default is `'sth'`; the migration writes the new key
   once and the old key is left unread thereafter.
   `opnSthChoiceHtml637` is replaced at the same seat by
   the drop down: one setrow naming the Morning & Evening
   choice with a `<select data-p650office>` carrying
   'Divine Office' · 'Sing the Hours' · 'Both', the
   standing value selected. The change handler writes the
   store, then re-paints in-session —
   `extRebuildDescs(state.dayPayload)` +
   `opnRepaintHome604()` — no refetch, no re-settle, no
   day rebuild: both sides already stand on the record, so
   a check change is a paint change only. The Settings
   fallback-row label (`settingsFamilyLabel`) reads the
   new reader and names the standing value ('Morning &
   Evening choice: Sing the Hours' / '… Divine Office' /
   '… Both').
5. **Retirements (§2.3) — named, with their replacements.**
   `opnExtChoice628` retires (callers: the courier leg,
   re-pointed by §3.1 · the hook, retired). The v638
   re-settle hook, the v649 flip heal, and the epoch/prior
   maps + `settle`'s epoch gate retire (no other arming
   site or caller stands — §2.3). `extDownload`'s backup
   flip retires for labeled metas (a side falls alone,
   §3.1); unlabeled downloads never carried a backup and
   are byte-unchanged. The v637 prune's choice test
   retires; its whole-record test stands (a cached labeled
   record missing either side, or its standing image,
   leaves and re-settles — the standing v648 discipline),
   and its eviction names both side keys + the bare key.
   `opnSthPrimary628` / `opnSthFirstSet632` /
   `OPN_STH_FIRST_KEY` retire as governing machinery once
   the migration reader and the Settings label have moved
   (§3.4) — the migration reads the old value through the
   reader one last time. What the retired code served
   elsewhere is answered per item above; nothing else
   calls any of it (caller search, §2.3).
6. **Nothing else moves.** The server, the payload, the
   pass-through `&src=` seats, the v645 queue discipline,
   the v617 marks machinery, the v647/v649 adoption rule,
   and every unlabeled slot's settle/download/restore path
   stand as shipped.

## §4 Gates — harness-v650 (fail-on-old, pass-on-new; fixtures per §2.6)

  image · duration 1034/869), and the play path loads the
  castos stream. On the base client the painted track is

  standing at first open is evicted and never mints;
  a stamped entry matching its side's enclosure restores

  `nativeContractItem` for the Sing the Hours descriptor
