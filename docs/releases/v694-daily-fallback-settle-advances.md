# v694 — Daily rows stand NOT AVAILABLE until the day's own episode lands (no fallback)

## §1 Scope

**Direction (John, 2026-11-26, verbatim):** "This saint of the day is a day behind"

**Correction (John, 2026-11-26 09:26 CT, verbatim):** "No falbsxk. Not available yet" — read by John in the same turn as: **"No fallback. Not available yet."**

**REJECTED / SUPERSEDED SHAPE, named so the record stays honest:** this release was first briefed as a *provisional fallback* — a fallback settle (yesterday's episode under the NOT-UPDATED warning) that ADVANCES to today's episode when it lands mid-day. John's correction rejects that shape whole. There is **no fallback at all**: yesterday's episode never paints in today's place, no fallback settle exists, and nothing plays from the row until today's episode stands. Every section below is grounded on the corrected shape; no part of the provisional-advance design survives.

**The defect, verified in the field (2026-11-26):** the server snapshot rebuilt 13:34Z headed "11/26 - St. John Berchmans", and John's phone still showed 11/25 at 09:24 CT. The cause stands in two shipped layers:

1. **v666's NOT-UPDATED fallback** (`opnDailyMatch666`, client:20707): when no entry matches the presented day, every series arm returns the newest EARLIER entry with `stale: true` — the last episode that loaded paints in today's place, under the v625 line "Today's episode has not updated yet — showing the last one that loaded."
2. **v677's settle discipline** (`resolveDailyDynamic666`, client:20783; store `opn-daily-settled-v1`, client:5148): the first compose's pick — the fallback included, per v677 §2.3.5 ("a late-landing episode stands tomorrow, never mid-day") — is written to the settle store before the repaint and held all day. Even a compose after today's episode lands rebuilds from the settled fallback record (client:20848 ff) and never re-runs the matcher.

**The rule (the corrected fix shape, whole):**

- A `daily` (v666) row whose day's episode has not landed stands in a **NOT AVAILABLE YET** state (§2.1's exact paint). Yesterday's episode NEVER paints in today's place.
- Nothing plays from the row until today's episode stands; when it lands, the next standing compose (§2.3, seat 7) paints today's episode — because no settle was ever written for the unresolved day, the matcher runs fresh at that compose and settles the real episode.
- A settle taken on a PRESENT episode stays final for the day (the v677 discipline stands for real settles: dress drift, supplier-edition swaps, and later snapshot changes never move it).
- The same rule binds the `massdaily` sibling kind (§2.4): no stale fallback on the Mass cards either.
