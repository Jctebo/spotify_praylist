# v655 — Composed office rows stand whole: Details in the row menu, the share line names the source

## §1 The item (DevOps Board row 1632 · the two follow-ons named by the v654 Plan's seam-consumer audit, §2.3 of docs/releases/v654.md; live pin ora-pro-nobis-v654-1)

**The standing shape (v650/v654, NOT re-opened):** for each labeled office slot (Morning Prayer · Evening Prayer) the app paints the Divine Office episode and the Sing the Hours episode as separate tracks. A real day row is a COMPOSED descriptor, built by `extRebuildDescs → trackDesc`: it carries the side's own `source`, title, duration, descText and `_side650` stamp — but **no `kind`** (the seam copies `kind` only for podcast, the v620 repair). Two more consumers key on `kind`, so the composed office row stands short of every other playable row in exactly two places:

1. **No Details item.** `epCardIsEpisode` (client.ts 10207) recognizes an episode by `kind` (podcast · homily · external) plus the AI Signal / novena-stem forms. A composed office descriptor matches none, so the Praylist home row's ⋮ menu (the seat at 17063) carries no **Details** item for either office side. Every other playable row offers Details — the episode card with the track's own title, text, played state and Play.
2. **The share line names no side.** `shareLineFor640` (16480) picks the office line form (`<Day> · <source> · <dur>`) only under `kind === 'external'`; a composed office descriptor falls to the in-day form (`<Day> · Episode · <dur>`). Since v654 the share LINK and its landing are correct — only the sheet's words fail to say which recording is being shared.

**The ask:** a composed office row stands whole like every other row — its menu carries Details opening THAT track's own card (its own title / text / marks, per side), and its share line names the track's own source — Morning and Evening, both sides. Every other row's menu and share line stand byte-identical to today.

## §2 Plan — both consumers grounded, the fix shape picked per consumer (base = standing-clone main 5f2a800, the v654 ship tree; production pin ora-pro-nobis-v654-1, Worker f491ae15; all line numbers are `app/worker/src/client.ts` on this tree)

### 2.1 The seam, restated (from v654 §2.1, verified on this tree)

`trackDesc` (15411) builds the composed descriptor: `source: t.source || ''` rides unconditionally, `_occ` / `_side650` ride by stamp, `kind` rides **only for podcast**. ### 2.2 The discipline call — FOLLOW v654: no seam-stamp; a guarded branch at each consumer

The v654 Plan rejected stamping `kind: 'external'` at the `trackDesc` seam, on grounded side effects: the composed descriptor's missing kind is load-bearing at consumers outside that release's ask. **This Plan follows that call, on this release's own evidence:**

- For consumer 1, the seam-stamp's headline side effect in v654 §2.2 — "it would silently add the Details item" — is no longer a side effect; it is this release's ask. But the stamp's OTHER effects stand unasked and ungrounded for this release: `trackCard`'s external meta branch (17293, fed composed descriptors at the 25324 seat the v654 audit left to verify), `queueLiveDesc`'s kind read (5451), and every future kind consumer would re-dress at once. The ask names two consumers; the fix touches two consumers.
- The v654 shape — widen the branch at the consumer, guarded by the descriptor's own `source` standing AND the labeled-slot id — changed exactly one behavior and proved byte-identity for every other family by harness. The same shape is available at both of this release's consumers (§2.3, §2.4). No evidence on this tree overturns the call.

### 2.3 Consumer 1 — Details: widen `epCardIsEpisode`'s recognition, guarded by source + labeled slot

**The seat.** `epCardIsEpisode` has exactly ONE call site in the client: the rowmenu handler at 17063, gated on the Praylist home, which builds the Details item opening `openEpisodeCard({ desc: t, surface: 'praylist', … })`. (Every other `openEpisodeCard` seat — 9555 · 9660 · 9806 · 10102 · 13288 — is fed by its own surface's descriptors and does not consult the predicate.) Widening the predicate therefore moves exactly one behavior: the home row menu's Details item.

**The card is already per-side whole for a composed office descriptor — verified seat by seat:**

- **Title / duration / text:** the model takes `t.title`, `t.durationS`, `t.descText` — all the side's own on the composed descriptor (the seam copies them from the side's raw track).
- **Series line:** the model's `seriesTitle` is `t.block` for non-podcast — the office block name, as for every other day row.
- **Played state (read):** `epCardPlayedOf` → `epCardDayPlayed(d, false)` on the praylist surface → `isPlayedDesc` (4869), which keys by `playedKeyOcc(…, _occ, _side650)` (4853) — the side's own mark.
- **Intention line:** `trackDrawTextFor(d)` keys by `trackKeyOf` → `descMarkKey` — per-side, the same draw the row itself carries.
- **Play:** the model's `onPlay` is `tapPlay(t)` — the standing per-side play wiring (v650/v653 card-key discipline).
- **The card's placement is NOT touched** — `openEpisodeCard` keeps its own anchor math (the v641 named residual stands as-is).

**One wrinkle the widening exposes — the card's mark toggle writes bare.** `epCardTogglePlayed` (10243) falls through to `writePlayed(d.episodeId, presentedDateStr(), …, d.block)` — no `_occ`, no `_side650`. Its own refresh read is per-side, and the row menu's Mark item writes per-side (`dayRowMarkApply639`: `writePlayed(…, t._occ || 0, t._side650 || null)`, whose header names the card toggle as its precedent "followed exactly" — the precedent's intent is the descriptor's own key; the toggle is the one seat that never received the stamp parameters, predating v610). Left standing, the second-painted side's card would DISPLAY its own state but FLIP its sibling's mark — gate (a)'s "its own marks, per side" would fail in substance. **The fix therefore includes the toggle's fall-through write:** pass `d._occ || 0, d._side650 || null` — for every descriptor carrying neither stamp, `playedKeyOcc(id, block, 0, null)` is exactly the key written today, so every standing card user's toggle is byte-identical in effect; for stamped descriptors the write lands on the key the card itself reads.

**The pick:** in `epCardIsEpisode`, after the standing `kind` line, add the guarded clause: a descriptor whose `episodeId` is a labeled slot AND whose `source` stands is an episode. Placement is inert by construction — the two slot ids are fixed strings, never an AI Signal id, never a dated novena stem, and `novenaInfo` is false for them in production (grounded by the defect itself: were any later check true, office rows would carry Details today). The star on the card keys at slot level (`starEntryForDesc`'s episode form, id = episodeId) — standing card furniture, shared by both sides as the slot's own identity; not named in the ask, not moved.

The line built is the standing office form with the descriptor's own source — for the widened path the source always stands, so the `(t.source || 'Divine Office')` default never mislabels a side.
