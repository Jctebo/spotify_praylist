### Q3 — Why the server pointer does not govern for Morning/Evening: the download seam, in two halves

The Morning/Evening slots DO ride the same courier/live-payload leg as every other slot — the divergence is what happens AFTER the payload lands, and it has two halves:

1. **The client never learns the filling source.** `externalsLivePayload` (index.ts) builds a present entry from the row's state/title/streamUrl/duration/size/resolvedAt (+ backup/route/stale) — **it serves no `source` field**. The courier settle (client.ts) therefore defaults the source: `src = slot.courier ? slot.source : 'Divine Office'`, and v628's `opnExtChoice628` returns that default for a no-backup entry. An StH-filled row carries NO backup (backups stand only on Divine Office rows), so the slot settles with **source 'Divine Office'** and the castos pointer. `extDownload` picks its routes by that source: 'Sing the Hours' ⇒ [Worker pass-through, direct]; anything else ⇒ [direct, pass-through]. Mislabeled, the download goes DIRECT to episodes.castos.com first — a host that sends no Access-Control-Allow-Origin (verified live at Implement: the enclosure answers 200 with no ACAO header), so the browser read fails, exactly as Amendment A1 recorded when the pass-through was built.
2. **The pass-through re-resolves — and its Divine Office fetch throws before its own fallback.** The `/api/player/externals/audio` route (index.ts) does NOT read `ext_resolutions`; it re-resolves the slot from the feeds. For a Divine Office slot it first runs `extMatchDivineOffice(await extFetchFeedItems(DO_FEED_URL))` — and `extFetchFeed` THROWS on a non-OK response. The Divine Office feed stands at **HTTP 500 live** (verified at Implement, and standing since the v625 record). The throw lands in the route's outer catch ⇒ **502 "feed fetch failed"** — the Sing the Hours fallback branch, which stands INSIDE the same try AFTER the DO match, is unreachable exactly when the feed is down, which is the only condition under which an StH fill stands in the slot. (v626 fixed this shape in the watcher — failure fills — but the pass-through kept the pre-v626 shape.) Both routes fail; `meta.backup` is null; the catch settles the slot missing under the payload title ⇒ the field paint.
- **Why Readings/Night Prayer resolve:** their rows' enclosures stand on hosts that answer direct browser reads (the libsyn/blubrry Divine Office enclosure chains, per the standing A1 posture), so route 1 succeeds and the source mislabel is harmless there. Morning/Evening are the only present rows whose enclosure host forbids direct reads AND whose pass-through re-resolution is gated behind the broken feed fetch. Reproduced verbatim in harness-v631's fail-on-old: the old pass-through answers 502 `{"error":"feed fetch failed"}` for both labeled slots, and the old choice function settles the real payload entry as source 'Divine Office'.

### Decisions

- **D1 — The payload names the source.** `externalsLivePayload` serves `source` on a present entry when the row carries one (index.ts). The client can now route by the source that actually filled the slot.
- **D2 — The choice honors the served source.** `opnExtChoice628` prefers `e.source` over the slot default in every return (client.ts) — an StH fill settles AS 'Sing the Hours', so the standing `extDownload` ternary routes it pass-through-first; a Divine Office entry settles as before, and the v628 toggle re-orders through the same seam (the flipped backup now also names `e.source` instead of a hardcoded 'Divine Office' — identical on every row that can carry a backup, since backups stand only on Divine Office rows).
- **D3 — A failed Divine Office read no longer aborts the pass-through.** In the audio route's DO-slot branch, the DO feed fetch gains its own try: a failure reads as zero items, the DO match finds nothing, and the standing Sing the Hours fallback branch runs (index.ts) — the v626 failure-fills discipline, applied to the route that was left behind. Nothing else in the route moves: the fallback stays settings-gated (fbRow enabled + fills) and Morning/Evening-only by its token test. Named side effect: a non-labeled DO slot requested while the feed is down now answers 404 "no episode resolved for that slot today" instead of 502 — the same verdict, in the route's own not-resolved shape.
- No matcher, no stale predicate, no watcher, no nudge, no cron byte moved. The v626-retired client StH firing block stays retired — the server pointer now governs AND plays; no second governor is restored.
