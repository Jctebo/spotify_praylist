# v641 — Pop-up row menu usability study (Scope)

## §1 Study — the row menu on John's screens (measured, 2026-11-23)

**Order (John, voice call, main chat, 2026-11-23 17:52 CT, verbatim):** "And also let's add another feature usability study on that pop out menu it doesn't display right on my screen. Let's also take a look at that little pop up menu when you go to more details under our thing. Let's do that as a separate request." STUDY ONLY — no fix code in this release; the fix stands as a Scope package (§2) for John's approval. Study seat: chain-v641-study, contract 965.

**The menu.** The small pop-up that opens from a track row's ⋮ act. One shared component, `openSheetMenu(title, items, anchorEl)` (`app/worker/src/client.ts`), dresses every caller in the same card (`.menusheet.anchmenu`, `app/worker/src/ui.ts`): the Praylist day rows (`openDayRowMenu`, items Share · Add to queue · Download · Mark as read · Details — v639's shape), the podcast rows (`openPodRowMenu`), the Favorites rows (`openFavRowMenu` — delegates to the day/pod menu, or its mirror with added Move-to-folder items), plus the header menu, block-page menus, addition menus, intention menus, playback and profile menus. Every geometry rule below is the shared component's, so every caller inherits the defects.

**How it is built (grounded in the shipped v639 tree, 82fa503).**
- Width: `width = min(max(232, anchorWidth), vw − 24)`. The anchor is the ⋮ button (`.iconbtn`, 44×44 CSS px), so the 232px floor is the width on every screen ≥256px wide. The card's right edge aligns the anchor's right edge, clamped to 12px margins.
- Placement: below the anchor if `anchorBottom + 6 + cardHeight ≤ vh − 12`; above it if that fits; otherwise the roomier side with `maxHeight` set and the card scrolling internally (`overflow-y:auto`). The only vertical bound in any branch is `vh − 12` — the Now Playing dock and the tab bar do not exist in the math.
- Z-order: catcher 65, card 70, dock 40, tab bar 30 — the card paints OVER the dock and the tab bar.
- Header: the track's full title in `.anchmenu h1.screen` — 12px sans, uppercase, letter-spacing 1px, no truncation, no line cap, no ellipsis.
- Rows: `.menurow` min-height 44px.

**Method.** The shipped client (v639 tree) rendered in the estate's playwright rig with production-shaped fixtures; the dock stood via the app's own `updateDock()`; the anchor was the shipped `.iconbtn` control (44×44) seated mid-screen (top at 45% of vh) and low (bottom 96px above the viewport foot). Viewports (CSS px): **unfolded 604×725** — John's Fold (SM-F971U1) inner display ≈1812×2176 device px at the panel's ~3.0 density; **cover 360×840** (1080×2520 at 3.0); **phone 412×915**; plus unfolded landscape 725×604 and a 768×922 control. Renders: `~/workspace/projects/ora-pro-nobis/v641-study-renders/`.

**Defects (observation → measured value → cause).**

- **F1 — The card's width never answers the screen.** Measured: the card is 232 CSS px wide at vw 360, 412, 604, 725 and 768 — 64% / 56% / 38% / 32% / 30% of the screen. On the unfolded Fold it stands as a narrow right-edge strip under the ⋮ while most of the screen sits empty. Cause: the width derives from the 44px anchor and the 232px floor is the whole rule; no viewport term exists beyond the `vw − 24` cap.
- **F2 — The header wraps without limit and eats the card.** Measured at 232px wide (content width ≈204px): "Morning Prayer - Divine Office" (31 chars) = 2 lines / 28px; John's screenshot title "Daily Mass Reading Podcast for November 23, 2026" (48 chars) = 3 lines / 42px; a 98-char episode title = 5 lines / 70px, pushing the card to 314px tall. Cause: 12px uppercase + 1px letter-spacing in a 204px measure, with no truncation, no line cap, no smaller treatment for long titles.
- **F3 — The card's foot lies across the Now Playing bar.** Measured at 604×725 with the screenshot header and a mid-screen anchor (his exact geometry): the card seats below the anchor (the below-test passes: bottom 662 ≤ vh−12 = 713), its foot covers the dock by **70px** and reaches **2px into the tab bar**; the last act, Details (bottom 655), stands entirely inside the dock's band (590–660). With the 98-char header the card bottom is 690 — 70px over the dock, 30px into the tab bar. At 360×840 the same mid-anchor menu covers the dock by 24px; at 412×915 a low anchor's card covers it by 4px. Cause: the placement tests bound only on `vh − 12`; neither the dock's top nor the tab bar's top is a term, and z-70 over z-40 makes the overlap paint as the menu "cutting" the bar — exactly John's screenshot (2026-11-23 16:38 CT).
- **F4 — When neither side fits, the last act can stand cut with no sign of a scroll.** Measured at 725×604 (unfolded landscape), mid anchor, screenshot header: the roomier-side branch sets `maxHeight: 270px`; card `scrollHeight` 284 > `clientHeight` 268; the Details row is not fully inside the card — reachable only by scrolling the card, and nothing on the card says it scrolls. Cause: the cap is `vh − 12 − top`; the internal `overflow-y:auto` carries no fade, no shadow, no partial-row hint.
- **F5 — Taller menus widen F3/F4's window (risk note, same causes).** The Favorites mirror menu (day set + Move items) measured 7 items = 374px tall at 232 wide (vs 272–286 for the 5-item day menu); every added folder lengthens it. In portrait shapes it flipped above the anchor with clearance; its height engages F4's branch sooner on shorter screens.

**Passes (measured, no defect).** Every row stands 44.0px tall at every shape (the 44px minimum holds in the card dress); the ⋮ anchor is 44×44. With a low anchor the card flips above and clears the dock (bottom 579 vs dock top 590 at 604×725). With no anchor the fallback seats the card full-width above the dock line (`top = vh − 76 − h`).

**John's screenshot, read against the measurements.** 2026-11-23 16:38 CT: header wrapped to two lines (his pre-v639 item set; the same title measures 3 lines in the v639 dress at 232px), card foot across the Now Playing bar, Details standing over the bar — F2 + F3 in one frame.

## §2 The fix package (held at Scope — John's approval sets the shape and the run level)

Scope package 93 in the Scope Management Space + the interactive document (`v641-scope-popup-menu-fix`). Recommended run level: **Stop before Ship**. Deviation: none — every decision below answers the display defects John named; the one scope question (shared component vs row menus only) is put to John as Q4, not decided for him.

- **Q1 — Width rule on wide screens.** A (recommended): the card takes a real width rule — 232px floor stands on phone widths; on screens ≥600px wide the card widens to 320px, still anchored to its control. B: a bottom sheet at wide widths (full-width, above the dock) instead of an anchored card. C: leave the width; fix only placement and header.
- **Q2 — Placement vs the Now Playing bar.** A (recommended): the dock and tab bar bound the placement like the viewport edges — the card never covers either; it flips or caps above the dock's top. B: the card may cover the dock but never the tab bar. C: leave placement; only the header changes.
- **Q3 — Header treatment.** A (recommended): the header caps at 2 lines with an ellipsis; the full title stands in the Details card as today. B: the header is dropped — the menu opens on its acts, the row behind names the track. C: leave the header as is.
- **Q4 — Reach.** A (recommended): the shared component — every menu that opens this way takes the same geometry (header menu, block menus, podcast and Favorites rows alike). B: the track-row menus only (day rows, podcast rows, Favorites mirror); the other callers keep today's geometry.
- **Q5 — The capped card's scroll sign.** A (recommended): when the card caps and scrolls, its foot shows a fade + a partial row (the standing sheet pattern) so the scroll reads. B: no sign — the scroll stays invisible as today.

Field facts for the fix's harness (from §1): fixtures = the screenshot title (48 chars), a 98-char title, the 5-item day set, the 7-item Favorites mirror set; shapes = 604×725, 360×840, 412×915, 725×604; the gates = card width per the chosen rule, header lines ≤ the chosen cap, card bottom ≤ dock top (or the chosen bound) at every anchor seat, every act reachable, rows 44px.
