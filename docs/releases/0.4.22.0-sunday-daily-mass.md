## Scope

### Problem
- The Sunday Spotify playlist does not include the Daily TV Mass episode the user selected from the linked Daily TV Mass show.
- The playlist is rebuilt from repository contracts, so an ad hoc Spotify edit would be removed by the next refresh.
- The “Sunday” name does not describe its mix of Mass and homilies.

### Audience
- Primary user: the owner and listeners of the Sunday prayer playlist.
- Secondary stakeholder: the automated Spotify playlist refresh.

### Current Status Quo
- Sunday contains the Fr. Mike Sunday Homily and Bishop Barron Sunday Sermon contracts.
- The linked show has a full episode titled “Daily TV Mass Friday October 23, 2026,” distinct from a separate Rosary upload published the same day.

### Goal
- Include the full October 23, 2026 Daily TV Mass episode in Sunday and retain both existing homilies.
- Rename the playlist and prayer-home group to “Mass and Homilies.”
- Ensure the episode remains in Sunday after the repository-driven Spotify refresh.
- Verify the refreshed Spotify playlist and deployed prayer-home data.

### Constraints
- Use the exact episode URI resolved from the user-provided Spotify show.
- Preserve the current Sunday playlist ID and existing item order.

### Narrow Wedge
- Add the single dated Mass episode as a fixed Spotify episode URI, rename the existing playlist and prayer-home group to “Mass and Homilies,” then refresh and verify production.

### Non-Goals
- Automatically selecting a new Daily TV Mass episode every Sunday.
- Adding the Rosary episode or changing the other prayer playlists.

### Risks And Unknowns
- The direct Spotify item edit may be replaced by scheduled refresh until the updated playlist definition is deployed.
- This is a fixed episode for October 23, 2026; future Sunday Mass episodes are not selected automatically.

### Recommended Next Step
- Confirm the fixed URI contract pattern and Sunday playlist refresh tests, then implement the contract addition and publish it.

## Research

- `config/spotify/playlists/sunday.json` lists two named queue contracts in the order Fr. Mike, then Bishop Barron.
- `jobs/playlist/spotify_contracts.py` loads a queue contract with a fixed `spotify_uri`; `jobs/playlist/refresh_playlist.py` resolves that URI without a lookup, making the exact episode survive the regular refresh.
- `scripts/build_pages_artifact.py` builds the prayer-home Sunday group from the same ordered contracts, so one new contract updates the visible episode link as well.
- `tests/test_spotify_contracts.py` verifies playlist membership and ordering; `tests/test_refresh_job_contract_path.py` covers contract resolution and refresh behavior; `tests/test_build_pages_artifact.py` verifies playlist group data.

- Invalid URI or contract shape: contract loader/test failure before deployment.
- Spotify refresh failure: workflow conclusion is non-success or expected episode is missing from the playlist.
- Pages publish failure or stale edge cache: workflow failure or live JSON missing the added entry; retry deployment and refetch with cache bypass.
