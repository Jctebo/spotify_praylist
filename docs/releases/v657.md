## §2 Plan — the saint slot

All cites are `app/worker/src/index.ts` on the base tree unless named.

### 2.1 The resolution discipline, end to end

- **Passes.** One resolver pass (`resolveExternalsPass`, index:2594)
  resolves Chicago **today + tomorrow only** for every slot-class
  feed. It runs from: the Worker cron (`scheduled`, index:4415, every
  30 minutes) · the client nudge (POST /api/player/externals/nudge,
  index:5739, need-tested, 120 s debounce) · the agent-door forced
  pass (POST /api/agent/externals/resolve, index:4701).
- **Per-feed shape** (`runSlotFeed`, index:2626): stand-down test →
  one feed fetch → the standing matcher per date → `extResolutionWrite`
  upserts 'present' or 'missing' per standing slot. Failure rule
  (v624 §1 item 5): a failed read (HTTP error · parse failure ·
  timeout · zero-yield) writes NOTHING; a slot is written 'missing'
  only after a SUCCESSFUL read with no match.
- **The stand-down** (`extFeedStoodDown`, index:2425): a feed is
  skipped ("stood-down") when every standing slot for TODAY carries
  **any** row — 'present' OR 'missing' — whose `resolved_at` Chicago
  date is today. **A 'missing' row counts as settled.** That is the
  trap.
- **The saint leg** (index:2766): `runSlotFeed('saint-of-the-day', …)`
  with matcher `extMatchSaint` (index:2111) = `extMatchLatestByDate`
  (latest item with pubMs ≤ target-day end, enclosure required) +
  the title check: the picked item's title must start with the
  target's "MM/DD" prefix, else null (a wrong-day saint never
  stands). The parser (`extParseFeed`, index:2004) reads the whole
  feed — no window limit.
- **The nudge's need test** (`extNudgeFeedKeys`, index:3143): a feed
  is needed when a standing slot has NO row today, or its
  `last_success_at` is older than EXT_STALE_MS (3 h). A 'missing'
  row defeats the first branch; the midnight read stamps
  last_success 06:00Z, so from 09:00Z a client nudge CAN force the
  saint feed and land the episode. **Nov 23's 01:46Z landing
  (19:46 CT) is that shape — an evening app open, not a discipline.**
  Nov 24/25 drew no effective nudge and stayed missing. A fix that
  depends on the user opening the app is not a fix.
- **Past days are never revisited:** passes resolve today + tomorrow
  only, so a 'missing' that survives its day is permanent (until the
  35-day prune, index:3004) absent backfill.

### 2.3 The failure, named

**Timing + stand-down. Not matching, not window.** The Chicago-
midnight pass (06:00Z) runs two hours BEFORE the publisher's 08:00Z
release. Its read succeeds; the newest item is yesterday's; the
matcher rightly returns null for today (title-prefix check) and for
tomorrow; the pass writes 'missing' for both — and that write stands
the feed down for the rest of the day, so none of the day's 47
remaining cron passes ever sees the 08:00Z episode. The saint slot
stands in NO retry set: the cron IS the watcher, and the stand-down
dismisses it.

### 2.4 The fix (saint leg)

The saint feed's stand-down counts only a **'present'** row:

- New `extFeedStoodDownPresent(env, slotIds, today)` beside
  `extFeedStoodDown`: same read, with `state === 'present'` required
  alongside `chicagoDateOf(resolved_at) === today`.
- `runSlotFeed` gains a trailing optional parameter
  `presentOnlyStoodDown?: boolean` (after `routeFor` — the
  `sthAwareStoodDown` precedent for per-feed stand-down variants);
  the stand-down branch selects the present-only test when set. The
  saint call site passes `true`; every other call site is untouched
  by this section (§3 widens the flag's use).
- **Walk of a day under the fix:** 06:00Z pass writes 'missing'
  (unchanged) → 06:30Z–08:00Z passes re-read the feed (not stood
  down: the row is 'missing'), re-write 'missing' → **08:30Z pass —
  the first cron after the 08:00Z release — writes 'present'** →
  the feed stands down normally from then. Tomorrow's row rides the
  same fetches (re-written 'missing' until its own release morning,
  when it is "today" and lands by the same walk). A failed read
  mid-window writes nothing and never stands the feed down (the
  variant reads resolution rows; the standing 'missing' is not
  'present'), so the next pass retries. A publisher-skip day:
  passes re-read all day and 'missing' stands at day's end — the
  truth, and the next day's cycle is unaffected (the row's
  resolved_at date rolls).
- **Cost, named:** the Buzzsprout feed (~370 KB class) is re-read
  at most ~5 times on a normal day (06:00Z–08:30Z), at most 48 on a
  skip day. No other feed's fetch count changes under this section.

### 3.2 (A) The retry discipline, generalized

The present-only stand-down (§2.4) extends to **every slot-class
feed in the enumeration**:

- `extFeedStoodDownDo` (the Divine Office variant) gains the same
  present requirement inside its standing rules: a slot counts
  toward stand-down only when its row is 'present', resolved today,
  and — for the two labeled slots — not standing on a Sing the
  Hours fill (the v626 rule, unchanged). One change inside the one
  function; the DO leg's call site is untouched.
- The exegesis and TV Mass call sites pass
  `presentOnlyStoodDown: true`, like the saint's. The bible leg's
  call site does NOT (not enumerated — §3.1).
- While any enumerated slot stands unresolved ('missing' or
  absent), its feed re-reads on the watcher's 30-minute cadence
  until it lands; once every standing slot for today is 'present',
  the feed stands down for the day. The Divine Office ladder's
  rungs 2–4 already re-run per pass for unresolved slots; under
  this change rung 1 (the feed read) joins them instead of being
  dismissed by its own 'missing'. Cost, named: on an outage day
  the DO feed is fetched per pass (one fetch per 30 min) rather
  than once — the standing failure rule (a failed read writes
  nothing) bounds the harm, and the StH fills + synthesis/scrape
  rungs carry the slots meanwhile.

### 3.3 (B) Feed freshness — the watcher keeps the snapshots inside their max ages

Grounded gap: the pass already carries a snapshot watcher (v624 §1
item 3, index:2919) over `rss_snapshots` (POD_RSS_SERIES_LIST +
the novenas catalog), `podcast_sequences` (POD_SERIES_LIST) — but
its stand-down (`snapStoodDown`, index:2924) is **calendar-day
based**: a snapshot built at 00:05 CT is not re-fetched until the
next Chicago day, up to ~24 h. The read side's own max ages are
far tighter — `RSS_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS` 2 h (index:3845) ·
`POD_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS` 24 h (index:3175) ·
`VIDEO_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS` 2 h (index:3525) — and **the read path
is the refresh path** (index:4005): a read that finds its snapshot
past max age rebuilds it inline, so the read WAITS on the rebuild,
and with no reads a snapshot stands stale for hours past its age.
Live evidence: at 06:00Z today every rss_snapshot was rebuilt by
the midnight pass (new day) — and the 'summa-in-a-year' row stands
at built_at 2026-11-20, days past any age, its rebuilds failing or
skipped in silence between midnights.

The fix — the watcher's stand-down becomes the read side's own
max-age discipline, per store class:

- `snapStoodDown(table, key, maxAgeMs)`: stood down iff a row
  stands AND `now − built_at < maxAgeMs`. The rss loop and the
  novenas block pass `RSS_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS`; the sequences loop
  passes `POD_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS`. Forced keys still bypass
  (nudge/agent-door semantics unchanged). No blind refetch: a
  snapshot inside its age is never re-fetched by the watcher.
- **Video snapshots join the watcher:** a new block in the pass
  beside the standing loops — for each video series def, skip
  while inside `VIDEO_SNAPSHOT_MAX_AGE_MS`; else build through
  the standing builder's own calls (`ytFetchFeedText` +
  `ytParseFeed`; zero-yield = failed build, writes nothing) and
  upsert into `video_snapshots`, with `extFeedStateWrite`
  bookkeeping under `video:<key>`. The read paths are untouched —
  their stale-rebuild branch becomes the failure backstop, not
  the freshness mechanism: in steady state every read finds its
  snapshot inside its max age and never waits on a rebuild.
- A build that keeps failing keeps retrying on the cadence while
  past its age (the standing failure rule writes nothing; the old
  snapshot stands and serves, flagged stale at read as today).
