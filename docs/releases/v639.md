# v639 — Row menu: 'Mark as read' replaces 'Open in Spotify'

## §1 Scope

John's words (2026-11-23 16:39 CT, with his screenshot of a Praylist row's ⋮ menu — Share · Add to queue · Download · Open in Spotify · Details): "Can you replace open in Spotify with Mark as read?"

The fix, in one shape:

- In the track row's menu, **'Open in Spotify' is removed** and **'Mark as read' stands in its place** — the same position in the menu (after Download, before any caller-appended items such as the Praylist home's Details). On a track that already stands read, the same position reads **'Mark as unread'** and clears the mark.
- The mark writes through the **standing stores only** — no new store. Per family (§2): a day track → its played mark in the played store, occurrence-aware for the v610 Divine Office copies; a podcast episode standing in the day → its listen mark in the core store; a recorded homily standing in the day → its played flag in the homily store.
- After a mark, the row's played dress, the block head's unplayed count (v615) and disc (v616) refresh **in place** through the standing `refreshPlayedChrome` seam — no repaint of the whole day, no blank.
- A row kind that carries **no mark** carries **no such act** (§2 names the families).

Run level: To Ship (DevOps Board row 1616, streamlined small-release path: Plan · Implement · light Review · Ship).

## §2 Grounding

Seams, all in `app/worker/src/client.ts`, verified firsthand on the base (local main `366007b`, the v637 ship tree; v638 in flight in parallel, not yet merged at branch time):

**The one builder.** `openDayRowMenu(t, anchorEl, extraItems)` composes the day-row menu: Share · Add to queue (kind-bound) · Download (only while not cached) · **Open in Spotify (only where `spotifyUrlFor(t)` stands)** · caller extraItems. It is reached from `wireRowActs`' `[data-rowmenu]` wiring, so it serves every day-row surface: **Praylist home rows · block-page rows · episodes-page rows · the `state.readTracks` fallback rows**, and **Favorites live day rows** (openFavRowMenu delegates to it when the live descriptor stands). The screenshot's Details item is the Praylist home's extraItem (v607) and stands after the Spotify position — so the replacement reads Share · Add to queue · Download · **Mark as read** · Details, exactly the swap John asked for.

**The mirror menu.** `openFavRowMenu`'s day-track side, when only the Favorites snapshot stands (no live descriptor), composes its own mirror menu carrying the same 'Open in Spotify' act for the same row kind. The replacement applies there too: the mirror row's own played dot already writes the played store from that surface (its `favMarkHtml` returns `playedDotHtml`), so a mark act is honorable there in a way the mirror's Download act is not (the mirror comment's own distinction). Its podcast side already carries 'Mark as listened' and no Spotify act — untouched.

**Left standing, named.** (1) The block page's **page** menu (the header ⋮ group) carries 'Open in Spotify' for the block's own Spotify playlist URL — a block-level act, not a row act; it stands. (2) `shareUrlFor` still prefers the Spotify URL for Share — not a menu act; it stands. (3) `openPodRowMenu` (the podcast series page's row menu) carries no Spotify act and already carries the mark act in its own vocabulary ('Mark as listened' / 'Mark as not listened') — untouched; this release's 'read' vocabulary belongs to the day-row menu only. (4) The episode title card's own played toggle (v585, `epCardTogglePlayed`) is a different surface — untouched, and its per-family write discipline is the precedent this release follows.

**Per-family mark discipline (the write the menu act performs):**

- **Day tracks** (payload tracks, Divine Office hours, prayers, the readings seat, novena rows — every non-podcast, non-homily descriptor): the played store. Read: `isPlayedDesc(t, presentedDateStr())`. Write: `writePlayed(t.episodeId, date, on ? 'marked' : null, block, occ)` with **exactly the key the row's own played dot writes** — `playedDotHtml` stamps `block = t.block || t._block` and `occ = t._occ || 0`, and `playedKeyOcc` keys a v610 occurrence copy per copy, so each Divine Office copy takes its own mark and no other copy moves. `writePlayed` closes with `refreshPlayedChrome()` itself.
- **Podcast episodes standing in the day** (v614 pinned episodes · v590 course picks · v611 day picks · v620 composed podcast rows — all `kind === 'podcast'` descriptors): the core listen store, by the descriptor's own identity (`podSeries` + `seriesKey`, the v589/v620 identity). Read: `!!t.listened` (the v515 rule — a podcast descriptor's mark is its series listen, never a day-store row; its dot is a plain span precisely so no tap writes the wrong store). Write: the `epCardTogglePlayed` podcast discipline from the descriptor — `podToggleMark(d.seriesKey)` when the open series payload is that series; else mark → `writePodMarkQuiet(seriesKey, podSeries, 'manual')`, unmark → the unmark post with the mark-queue fallback + `podSyncQueueListened(..., false)`; the descriptor's `listened` flag flips and `refreshPlayedChrome`'s poddot/count passes repaint the row dress, the head count, and the disc in place. Course and day-selector picks carry this same discipline — their played state is the core store's truth (v614 Q5 A).
- **Recorded homilies standing in the day** (`kind === 'homily'` descriptors): the homily store's played flag — `homilySetPlayed(t.homilyId, on)`, the episode card's discipline.
- **No mark, no act:** an **unavailable** track (the standing mark discipline never writes one — the mark-all handler skips them by rule) and any descriptor with no episode identity. Intention/segment rows never route through `openDayRowMenu` (no `[data-rowmenu]`) — out by construction. Where a row previously showed 'Open in Spotify' but carries no mark, it now shows neither act — the Spotify act retires whole from this menu.

**Label and position.** The item is computed inside `build()` (the menu's async build, where Download's presence is computed) at the retired item's position, labelled from the row's standing state at build time: 'Mark as read' when unmarked, 'Mark as unread' when marked. Unlike the retired act, it does not depend on a Spotify URL standing.
