# Scope: Ora Pro Nobis in Night

## Problem
The recurring Spotify playlist contracts place the evening Marian Antiphon in Evening and the Ignatian pre-Compline reflection in Night. Direct Spotify edits do not persist through scheduled playlist refreshes.

## Goal
Make playlist refreshes put the evening Marian Antiphon (Ora Pro Nobis) first in Night, followed by Night Prayer, and remove the Ignatian pre-Compline reflection from Night. Evening retains Office of Readings, Daily Examen, and Evening Prayer.

## Constraints
- Keep the existing dynamic `angelus-evening` contract and its source resolution.
- Do not modify unrelated playlists or the shared `night-pre-compline` episode contract.
- Preserve Night ordering in the playlist definition.

## Success criteria
- Evening contract membership omits `angelus-evening`.
- Night contract membership is ordered `angelus-evening`, `night-prayer-loh`.
- Tests assert the exact playlist membership and order.
- Change is reviewed, merged, refresh deployed, and production contents verified.
