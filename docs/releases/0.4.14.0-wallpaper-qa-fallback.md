# Release 0.4.14.0 - wallpaper-qa-fallback

- Status: implementing
- Created: 2026-10-01
- Source: production Daily Devotional Wallpapers run 36847761186.

## Scope

### Problem
- The Oct. 1 full generation run passed rotation but failed after all three renders for Oct. 10 were rejected because OCR did not confirm the overlaid title/subtitle.
- The last candidate was saved for inspection but never queued, so the workflow failed even though the day could rotate.

### Goal
- Let the third candidate load after QA retries are exhausted, retain the QA failure details in the run artifact, and finish successfully when assets are delivered and no slots remain missing.

### Constraints
- Keep three QA-guided render attempts.
- Mark fallback candidates as unapproved and surface their exact QA issues.
- Keep asset upload and checksum validation intact.
- A completed run with fallback warnings must exit successfully; genuinely missing assets still keep an incomplete status.

### Non-Goals
- Changing calendar resolution, rotation order, generated overlay text, or QA retry count.

## Research

- Run 36847761186 confirmed the unclassified Saturday entry was skipped and Our Lady of the Rosary on Oct. 10 remained resolved.
- The stale-checksum rotation fix worked; the run advanced past rotation and spent 18 minutes rendering before OCR rejected three watch candidates.
- `render_variant` saved the final rejected image locally and raised, preventing delivery and completion reporting.

## Plan

- Return the final rendered candidate after three QA rejections with an explicit fallback marker and QA detail.
- Deliver and checksum-verify that candidate through the existing staged-asset path.
- Mark complete runs with fallback candidates as `complete_with_warnings`; preserve `incomplete` for missing slots.
- Test renderer fallback output and pipeline success/reporting.
