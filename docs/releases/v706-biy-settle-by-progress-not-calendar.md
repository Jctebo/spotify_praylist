### §1.4 The two pick sources, grounded in the shipped code

- **The calendar pick.** `opnDailyMatch666(series, list, presented)` (client:21499): per-series date rules, hit-only since v694. For `bible-in-a-year`: the entry whose `day` equals `opnDailyDoy666(presented)`. It answers "what is today's episode?" — a question about the DATE, never about the listener.
- **The progress pick.** For a sequence-kind series the server payload's `currentKey` IS the first-unlistened derivation: `podPayload` (server) folds `podcast_listens` over the snapshot entries and derives (index:3798) `const current = entries.find((e) => !e.listened && e.kind !== "bonus") ?? entries.find((e) => !e.listened);` — the lowest-position unlistened day entry (a bonus only when no unlistened day entry stands). The client derivation is the same rule: `opnPodCurrentKey(entries)` (client:2491). It answers "where does this listener stand?" — a question about the LISTENER, never about the date.
- **Which question a daily row must answer depends on the series' nature.** A daily series published against the calendar (an RSS head: today's saint, yesterday's exegesis, today's readings, the newest homily) has no progress state — its day episode is a date fact, and the matcher is its only pick. A SEQUENCE series is a course wearing a daily row: its episodes are numbered days of a journey the listener walks in order, and its "today's episode" for THIS listener is the next unlistened one. v705 §1.5 already held this distinction in its hands (its class verdict turned on exactly the sequence payload's first-unlistened `currentKey`); §2.1 of v705 then prescribed the calendar matcher for every daily-set series at the course seat, sequence kind included. That prescription is the defect this release repairs.

### §1.6 The invalid-settle rule, grounded

- **The standing settle discipline (v677/v694/v705, unchanged):** a settle record for (presented, jobKey) in `opn-daily-settled-v1` — shape `{ episodeId, stale, desc }`, written by `opnDailySettlePut677` (client:5286) — rebuilds in place at every compose of that date; `listened` is read fresh; a finish flips the settled copy played in place. Settle-and-hold is NOT weakened by this release (§2.1 step 3 names the at-or-behind case explicitly).
- **The new defect class inside that discipline:** v705's arm can write a settle whose episode the listener has not reached — a settle AHEAD of progress. Once written, settle-and-hold faithfully holds the WRONG episode all day (and, in John's live case, the record stands on his phone now). A pick-source fix alone (§1.5) repairs future settles but leaves the standing bad record in force for its date. The correction rule is therefore part of the fix, not an optional extra.
- **"Ahead", defined precisely (sequence payloads):** let the PROGRESS PICK be the entry named by `payload.currentKey || opnPodCurrentKey(payload.entries)` over the CURRENT payload (listens folded as served at this compose). Let the SETTLED ENTRY be the payload entry whose `key === rec.episodeId`. The settle is AHEAD iff **an unlistened day entry stands BEFORE the settled entry in the payload** — operationally: the settled entry's position in `payload.entries` is greater than the progress pick's position, where the progress pick is by derivation the FIRST unlistened non-bonus entry. For the sequence build this is equivalently a `day`-number comparison (`podBuildSequence` sorts day entries by ascending day, bonuses anchored after their day): settled `day` > progress pick's `day`. §2.1 prescribes the payload-order form as primary (it needs no sort assumption) and names the `day` form as its bible-case reading.
- **Boundary cases, each grounded:**
  - **Settled == progress pick:** stands (the normal morning settle).
  - **Settled BEHIND the progress pick:** stands. The standing instance: settled this morning at the progress pick, finished since — the finish advanced the progress pick PAST the settled episode; the settled episode is behind, listened, and rebuilds played in place for the rest of the day. This is v705's whole discipline and §2.1 does not touch it.
  - **Settled entry no longer in the payload** (aged out of the served window): ahead cannot be proven from the payload; the settle STANDS (conservative — the correction fires only on proof).
  - **No progress pick stands** (`currentKey` null — every day entry listened, no unlistened bonus): nothing unlistened stands before anything; the settle STANDS.
  - **Progress pick is a bonus** (all day entries listened, an unlistened bonus remains): no unlistened DAY entry stands before the settled entry unless the settled entry is itself that bonus or a later bonus — the payload-order form decides it without special-casing; a settled day entry (necessarily listened, since all day entries are) stands.
  - **Non-sequence series:** the rule never runs — their settles are date facts, and no progress derivation exists against which "ahead" could be measured (§1.5).
- **John's live case under the rule:** the standing record for (2026-11-27, `Night:bible-in-a-year`) holds `biy:day-331`, unplayed; the current payload's progress pick is `biy:day-326` (Day 325 finished today); unlistened day entries 326–330 stand before Day 331 in the payload → the settle is AHEAD → invalid → on v706's first compose the arm discards it and re-settles on **Day 326** (§2.1 step 2). The row falls back to his progress, fresh/unplayed, and holds there for the rest of the date.
