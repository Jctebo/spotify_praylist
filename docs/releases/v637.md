### §2.1 The defect — named mechanism

The flip wrote **only** the localStorage key. Nothing re-settled the standing day, because the day cache never heard of the preference:

- The day cache (`opn-ext-cache-v1`) validity key in `resolveExternals` is **date + settings version + profile fold** — the Morning & Evening choice is not in it. A cache standing from before the flip therefore stays "valid" whole after it.
- On the return build, a valid record **owns** the two labeled slots: the seeding loop stands them `downloading` from the record, `extRestoreCached` restores them, and the courier leg skips any slot whose cached enclosure equals the served primary URL (`c.streamUrl === e.streamUrl`). The live payload's backup pointer — the only place the new preference could act — never reaches those slots. Proven in the harness on the base client (L5a-old): after a flip to Sing the Hours first, the return build leaves Morning **and** Evening Prayer standing on Divine Office.
- The cache record carries **no backup** (the `writeCache` slot shape drops `meta.backup`). So when the cache-owned restore cannot complete — the old choice's enclosure unreachable, its routes down — `extDownload`'s failure path has no backup to flip to and settles the slot **missing**. On a client-composed day that is the blank: the hour stands empty in the set. Proven on the base client (L5b-old): with the old choice's routes down on the return build, Morning Prayer settles `missing`.

**The fix** (smallest change at the defect's seam, inside `resolveExternals` only): after the cache record stands, a cached labeled slot (`ext-morning-prayer` / `ext-evening-prayer`) whose standing `source` is not the preference's current first choice is pruned from the record — before the seeding loop and the courier leg read it. The courier then re-settles the slot in place from the live payload, both sources standing, exactly as on a cold open, and the flipped-to source's own download carries the payload backup as its failure flip. A slot that settled on its backup (first choice genuinely down) is likewise pruned next open and retries its first choice — the standing per-open flip discipline. No other slot, record field, or path changes.

This fix is why one brief guard fell: the original brief held every seam byte-untouched; the amendment overrides that guard for the defect's seam only. The four choice functions themselves remain byte-identical — the defect lived in the cache validity seam, not in them.
