# Scope: Divine Office Last in Each Prayer Playlist

## Scope

### Problem
- The Mid-morning, Midday, and Mid-afternoon playlists place their Divine Office prayer before other content, while the user wants the Divine Office prayer to be the final item.

### Goal
- Place the Divine Office prayer last in the Mid-morning, Midday, Mid-afternoon, Evening, and Night playlists.
- Treat Mid-afternoon as the afternoon playlist because the repository has no separate Afternoon playlist.
- Leave Morning unchanged, as the user explicitly corrected the request to exclude it.

### Constraints
- Keep each playlist's non-Divine Office content and relative ordering intact.
- Keep Evening's Office of Readings followed by Evening Prayer, and Night's Daily Reflection followed by Night Prayer; both already place the Office prayer last.
- Keep the change in repository-owned Spotify playlist contracts so scheduled refreshes preserve the order.

### Success Criteria
- Mid-morning ends with `midmorning-prayer`.
- Midday ends with `midday-prayer`.
- Mid-afternoon ends with `afternoon-prayer-loh`.
- Evening ends with `evening-prayer-loh`; Night ends with `night-prayer-loh`.
- Morning and Sunday playlist contracts remain unchanged.
- Tests assert the ordering; production playlists are refreshed and read back to confirm the requested order.

## Plan
