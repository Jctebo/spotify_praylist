### 2.3 Leg 1, seam two — the seat's own settle never repaints

`resolveReadingsSeat590`'s settle (client.ts:19681-19682) sets
`day.readingsSeat` and calls `extRebuildDescs(day)` — and
nothing else. Its sibling resolvers (courses · day · episodes ·
videos) settle with `extRebuildDescs(day); if (state.dayPayload)
paintPrayersView(state.dayPayload);` (client.ts:19488, 19613).
`extRebuildDescs` itself repaints the home only at the
pending-settle moment (`wasPending642`, client.ts:20082).

So when the seat's fetch — the ~212 KB daily-readings payload,
the slowest leg of the build — lands after the pending settle,
the composed list and `state.dayDescs` gain the seat and the
queue (which rebuilds fresh at the disc tap, client.ts:22268)
plays it, while the painted home gains the row only if a later
unrelated repaint happens. The one repaint that can follow is a
side effect: the seat rebuild's `settleTrackDraws` asks for the
seat's new draw key, and the draw answer's
`refreshTrackDrawSurfaces(true)` (client.ts:5156) repaints —
when the draw batch runs and answers with draws. The row's
paint is therefore not a property of the seat's own settle: it
is borrowed luck, and on a day or a session where the draw leg
answers without draws (or the key already stands drawn), the
row stays unpainted while it plays. Probe proof (probe-v660,
§2.4): with the draws leg not repainting, the seat descriptor
stands in `state.dayDescs` and in every fresh rebuild while no
seat card stands in the painted home.
