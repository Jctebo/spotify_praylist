### 2.1 The old entry

Before v659, `paintFavoritesFolderRow()` emitted exactly one row into `#favorites-folder-slot`:

```html
<div class="card blockrow" data-openblock="__favorites"><div class="row">
  <div class="blockcover tone-gold sm"><span class="blockcover-initial">★</span></div>
  <div class="grow"><h3>Favorites</h3><div class="meta">…</div></div>
</div></div>
```

Facts grounded from that code:

- Dress: `.card.blockrow`, gold ★ tile, `<h3>Favorites</h3>`, and a meta line.
- Meta: `N track(s) · <total duration>` when starred tracks stand, otherwise `No starred tracks yet`. The duration is the sum of the track entries' `durationS`, formatted by `fmtDurLong`.
- No Play disc, no section ⋮, no live unplayed count, no chevron, and no + / − toggle.
- Paint discipline: the row painted once; later settles updated only its `.meta` text if the row already stood, so the wired row was not replaced.
- Its slot then stood above `#home-body`, between the plan row and block rows. Only placement changes under v662; placement is governed by §3, not restored to the head.

### 2.2 The old tap and page

The row carries `data-openblock="__favorites"`. The standing `[data-openblock]` wiring in `wirePrayersView` special-cases `__favorites` out of the home fold path and calls `openBlockPage('__favorites')`; that function immediately calls `openFavoritesPage()`. `openFavoritesPage()` sets `state.prayersView = { kind: 'favorites' }`, repaints, and scrolls to top.

The destination page is `paintFavoritesPage(day)`. It was not changed by v659 and is not changed here: it paints the standing Favorites header and `favFolderSectionHtml` over the visible folders, with v613's empty-folder hiding and all standing folder, row, menu, reorder, Play All, and back behavior.

### 2.3 What v659 changed at this seam

At shipped v659:

- `paintListenHome` moved `#favorites-folder-slot` below `#home-body`, before the offline line. **This placement stays.**
- `paintFavoritesFolderRow` re-formed the entry as `.p659card.p659favcard`, with a `.p659favhead`, meta, and + / − toggle.
- When open, its body reused `favFolderSectionHtml` over `favVisibleFolders()`.

- `paintFavoritesSurfaces` gained a comment and behavior around repainting the home when the Favorites card stood open.
- The fold helpers treat `__favorites` as an accordion participant: `opnToggleBlock604` closes it when another card opens, `opnOpenOnly659` closes it for landings, and `opnExpanded604` reads it closed by default.
