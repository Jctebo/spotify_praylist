# v645 — Playback without autoplay: only the selected scope plays

## §1 The item (John, 2026-11-23 20:38 CT)

"their is something squirly about autoolay. I want to strip back playing features to only play the scope that is selected and nothing else..remove the concept if autoplay from the app. Every selection replaces the current auto queue like if I hit play on one item a replaces the queue if I hit add to the queue it adds to the end of the queue I also want to add the option to play next in the queue."

John's model, in his words:

- **The autoplay concept is removed from the app** — no preference, no continuation past a selection.
- **Only the selected scope plays, and nothing else.**
- **Every selection replaces the current queue** — Play on one item replaces the queue with that item.
- **Add to queue adds to the end of the queue.**
- **A new act: Play next in the queue.**

## §2 Grounding — the autoplay inventory (shipped client, base = pin ora-pro-nobis-v642-1)

Everything "autoplay" names today, read from `app/worker/src/client.ts`
on the v642-1 tree. Each item: what it is · where it stands · what
John's model does to it.

### The preference itself

1. **The Autoplay store (v358)** — `AUTOPLAY_KEY = 'opn-autoplay-v358'`
   (localStorage), read by `autoplayOn()`, written by `setAutoplay(on)`
   (the "Autoplay (v358)" section, ~line 5926). Only an expressly stored
   `'off'` reads OFF; a fresh store reads ON — continuation is the
   default. `setAutoplay` also sends the native transport command
   `{command: 'autoplay', on}` (v466: the Android engine re-windows —
   the chain tail joins or leaves the loaded window).
   **Model:** the concept retires — store, reader, and writer go. Every
   read site takes today's stored-'off' behavior permanently.
2. **The Settings dress** — Settings → PLAYBACK section (~line 10620):
   an "Autoplay" row ("Keep playing through the day when a queue ends.
   Off restores the stop at the end of a block.") with the `#set-autoplay`
   toggle, click-wired at ~line 10766. **Model:** the row retires with
   the concept.
3. **The menu toggles** — an "Autoplay On/Off" stay item stands in both
   Playback menus: `openPlayerMenu` (~line 16239) and `openDockMenu`
   (~line 16267), each calling `setAutoplay(!autoplayOn())`.
   `syncAutoplayToggles()` also still writes two retired face elements
   (`#dock-autoplay`, `#np-autoplay` — no paint site stands; dead ids).
   **Model:** both menu items and the sync retire.
4. **The native contract field** — `nativeContractFor` (~line 18230)
   sends `autoplay: autoplayOn()` in every queue contract, and the
   engine windows its playback on it. **Model:** the contract carries
   the stop discipline permanently (the field's fate — dropped or sent
   fixed — is a Plan call against the Android shell's reader).

### The continuation machinery the preference drives

5. **`chainForward(fromDesc)`** (~line 6001) — the behavior John calls
   squirrely. When a queue exhausts or a single play ends, the `ended`
   handler (~lines 6045, 6050) calls it whenever `autoplayOn()` (and
   the queue is not Starred-born): it builds the unplayed day tail past
   the ended track and starts it as a fresh queue. Playback continues
   past the scope the user selected, into the rest of the day.
   **Model:** retires whole. The end of the standing queue — and the
   end of a single play — is a stop.
6. **The native chain tail** (~line 18215, inside `nativeContractFor`) —
   the same continuation on the phone's engine: with autoplay ON, the
   contract appends the unplayed day tail after the queue's last item
   (`queueEnd` marks where the user's queue ends and the tail begins).
   **Model:** retires — the contract carries exactly the standing
   queue, nothing after it.
7. **`tapPlay`'s block build** (~lines 5245–5252; build =
   `sectionQueueFrom`, ~line 5438) — a prayer-card tap today builds a
   queue from the tapped track: the tapped track heads it, the block's
   unplayed remainder after it follows. With autoplay OFF the same tap
   plays the single track only. This is a queue *birth* (a selection),
   not an end-of-queue continuation — but its scope is wider than the
   one tapped item. **Model:** Q1 names the scope; the Plan settles
   whether a row tap's scope is the track alone or its block-from-here
   build, and the build follows the pick.
8. **Clear queue's end rule** (~line 5626) — after Clear queue, the
   re-seated current finishes as a single play whose end follows
   autoplay (ON → the day chain stands). **Model:** it finishes alone,
   always.

### The queue acts as they stand

9. **Play** — `playTrack` (~line 5201) clears the queue whole and plays
   the one track. `startQueue(descs, force)` (~line 5464) births a
   queue: with `force` (the card-tap path) a birth REPLACES the
   standing queue; without it, a tap while a queue sounds is the pause
   toggle. Moving *within* the standing queue at a track's end
   (`advanceQueue`, ~line 5488) is the queue playing its own scope —
   not autoplay. **Model:** Play = replace, as John states; the
   within-queue advance stands; the Plan names the pause-toggle edge.
10. **Block / episodes Play All and the block discs** — the
    `[data-queue]` handler (~line 21546): non-disc acts build through
    `queueBuild` (~line 5411) — the block's unplayed tracks, with the
    §2.4 fully-played fallback (an all-played block replays whole). The
    Praylist head discs build through `discQueue618` (~line 5429) —
    unplayed only, no fallback (v618). Both are selections (queue
    births). **Model:** they stand as selections; Q1's "exactly the
    scope tapped" puts `queueBuild`'s fully-played replay fallback in
    question (it plays tracks the unplayed filter removed) — the Plan
    names its fate. The disc build already matches the model.
11. **Continue** — `buildContinueQueue` (~line 5762), painted by
    `paintContinue` (~line 19537): Continue's act starts
    `queueBuild(whole day)` — the day's remaining unplayed sequence
    (with the fallback), rotated to the resume record's track when the
    carry applies. It is a selection — nothing plays until the user
    taps Continue. **Model:** Q3 names it; the recommendation is that
    it stands as a selection.
12. **Add to queue** — `addToQueue(desc)` (~line 5583, v542 Part 2):
    appends to the END of the current queue without interrupting; forms
    the queue on a standing single play; a waiting queue when nothing
    stands; kind-bound (a podcast cannot join a day queue or the
    reverse). It stands in the day-row menu (~line 12419), the
    podcast-row menu (~line 12409), the episode-card menu (~line
    16649), the homily menu (~line 14675), and the Favorites menus
    (~line 21355). **Model:** stands exactly as John states it.
13. **Play next** — does not exist: no `playNext` / `play next` /
    `play-next` anywhere in `client.ts`, `index.ts`, or `ui.ts`.
    **Model:** Q4 adds it — an act that inserts a track directly after
    the current one (`state.queue` at `queueIdx + 1`, with the native
    re-send leg on `addToQueue`'s pattern).
14. **Starred-born queues** — the `queueFrom = 'starred'` provenance
    (~line 13308; v546 §2.9) already never chains, on either engine.
    **Model:** every queue behaves that way now; the exception becomes
    the rule and the provenance carve-out is moot.

### The package (Scope Management Space package 95)

Four decisions, recommendations first; recommended run level **Stop
before Ship**; deviation: **none**.

- **Q1 · replace** — What does a Play tap put in the queue?
  **A (Recommended): the whole queue is replaced with exactly the scope
  tapped** — one item replaces it with that item; a block replaces it
  with that block; nothing from the old queue and nothing from outside
  the scope survives. B: Play behaves as today for block builds (a tap
  inside a block queues the block from that track on).
- **Q2 · endqueue** — What happens at the end of the queue?
  **A (Recommended): playback stops** — the last track finishes and
  nothing starts; the next play is the user's next tap. B: the day
  continues as today (the chain stands) — named for completeness; it
  contradicts the direction.
- **Q3 · continue** — Does Continue stand?
  **A (Recommended): Continue stands as a selection** — tapping it
  plays the day's remaining scope, because the user chose it; it is a
  selection, not an automatic continuation. B: Continue retires with
  the concept.
- **Q4 · playnext** — Where does Play next stand?
  **A (Recommended): the track row menu beside Add to queue, and the
  player's menu** — in the row menu it inserts that track after the
  current one; in the player's menu it stands for the playing track's
  queue neighborhood per the Plan's grounding. B: the track row menu
  only.

### 3.1 The autoplay concept retires whole

- `AUTOPLAY_KEY` (`opn-autoplay-v358`), `autoplayOn()`, `setAutoplay()`,
  and `syncAutoplayToggles()` are deleted, with both `syncAutoplayToggles`
  call sites (the dock paint ~6259, the Now Playing paint ~17253). The
  localStorage key is abandoned unread — no migration; an unread key is
  inert.
- The Settings PLAYBACK Autoplay row and its `#set-autoplay` click wiring
  are deleted. The PLAYBACK section itself stands — its Intentions row
  is untouched.
- The Autoplay stay items in `openPlayerMenu` and `openDockMenu` are
  deleted. No other menu item moves.
- Every surviving read site takes the stored-'off' behavior permanently,
  per §3.2–§3.4. After the change, the word `autoplayOn` stands at zero
  in the client (the YouTube embeds' `autoplay=1` URL parameters are
  named non-members and stand untouched).

### 3.2 The end is a stop (Q2 A) — chainForward retires

- `chainForward` is deleted with both call sites in the `ended` handler:
  the queue-exhausted branch (advanceQueue's own clear is the stop) and
  the single-play branch (the handler's standing terminal clear is the
  stop). The sleep timer's divert stands ahead of both, untouched.
- Clear queue's end rule follows without its own edit: the re-seated
  single play's end now always reaches the terminal clear; clearQueue's
  comment is updated to say so.
- `state.queueFrom` stands as a field (its snapshot-persistence read is
  not a chain read); only its two chain reads (the `ended` handler, the
  contract builder) retire. Starred-born queues lose nothing — every
  queue now ends the way they always did.

### 3.3 A track tap queues the track alone (Q1 A)

- `tapPlay`: the `!autoplayOn()` single-track branch becomes the only
  path — the build still re-resolves the tapped descriptor in the fresh
  whole-day list (`build[0]`), and the tap takes `playTrack(build[0])`
  unconditionally. The block's unplayed remainder never enters. The
  tap toggles above the branch (pending-start cancel · playing tap =
  pause · paused-current tap = resume) and the block-less single-play
  fallback stand byte-untouched. The position mark still governs the
  start (v617, through playTrack/startDesc as today).

### 3.4 The native contract carries exactly the standing queue

- `nativeContractFor`: the chain-tail block (the `autoplayOn()` day-tail
  append) is deleted. `queueEnd` is computed as `items.length` before
  the tail today; with the tail gone it equals the contract's length by
  construction, and `nativeQueueDiverged`'s measure keeps working
  unchanged.
- **Plan call — the fields stay, their values fixed.** The contract
  keeps sending `autoplay: false` and `queueEnd: <items length>`
  permanently. Ground: the engine windows its playback on the field,
  and the stored-'off' contract shape is the production-proven one —
  every user who ever turned autoplay off runs exactly this contract
  today. Dropping the field would stake the stop discipline on an
  engine default this release does not control. **No APK change.**
- Named residual: a contract sent by the pre-v645 client inside a
  standing session is replaced at the next send (any start, resume, or
  queue mutation re-sends under the new discipline); nothing of the old
  contract survives an app restart.

### 3.5 Play All's fully-played fallback — a deliberate asymmetry (Q1 A)

- `queueBuild` and its §2.4 fallback stand for the `[data-queue]`
  non-disc acts (the block page's and episodes page's Play All), restated
  as the rule: an explicit Play All act means *play this block* — it
  queues the block's unplayed remainder, and when nothing stands
  unplayed it queues the block whole (the scope tapped).
- The disc asymmetry is deliberate and stands: the Praylist head disc is
  the glanceable act whose grey all-played dress is its signal — its tap
  on a fully played block queues nothing (v618 untouched). Play All is
  the spelled-out command and always plays its scope.
- Continue — queueBuild's third consumer — retires under §3.6, so after
  this release queueBuild's consumers are the non-disc `[data-queue]`
  acts alone. `discQueue618` and `opnTrackUnplayed615` are untouched;
  the v615 live counts are untouched.

### 3.6 Continue retires (Q3 B — John's pick over the recommended A)

- `buildContinueQueue()` and `paintContinue()` are deleted, with the
  Continue card markup, the `#continue-btn` wiring, and every
  `paintContinue(...)` call site in the day paint pass.
- NOT retired: the resume machinery Continue borrowed — `readResume`,

### 3.7 Play next (Q4 A) — a new act on addToQueue's pattern

- New `playNext(desc)` beside `addToQueue`: same live re-resolution
  (`queueLiveDesc`), same availability guard, same kind bound (a
  mismatch returns without act), same native leg
  (`saveQueueSnapshot` · `renderQueueList` · `updateDock` ·
  `if (nativeSendNow()) nativeResendAtCurrent();`).
  - A queue stands: the track inserts at `state.queueIdx + 1`.
  - A single play stands (a current, no queue): the insert forms the
    queue `[current, track]` at index 0 — addToQueue's shape.
  - **Nothing stands: the track plays** — `playTrack(live)`. Play next
    is a Play-family act and never stands silent; addToQueue's
    waiting-queue shape belongs to Add alone, because Add is not a
    play act.
- Menu seats — immediately before that menu's 'Add to queue' item,
  under the same availability/kind conditions the Add item reads:
  `openDayRowMenu` · `openPodRowMenu` · `openHomilyMenu` (where the
  order reads exactly Play · Play next · Add to queue) ·
  `openFavRowMenu`'s podcast mirror and day mirror branches.
- The player's menu (`openPlayerMenu`): 'Play next' and 'Add to queue'
  stand for the current track, after Share (the Autoplay item's removal
  is §3.1) — each acts on `state.current`, inserting a further instance
  of the playing track after itself / at the queue's end (the standing
  addToQueue-of-current behavior; occurrence copies keep their own
  marks under the v610 seam). Both stand only while a current stands.
  The dock menu gains neither (it carries no Add to queue today); only
  its Autoplay item retires.

### 3.8 The section-head ⋮ menu (John's amendment)

- Seat: the Praylist group heads in `opnSetHtml609` — a
  `<button class="iconbtn p645secmenu" data-p645secmenu="<block>">`

- Wiring: a click handler in the Praylist wiring pass beside the
  `[data-p609sethead]` head wiring. The handler stops propagation —

  the block's descriptors from `state.dayDescs` by block name (the

  section's unplayed tracks in order — the disc's set verbatim), and
  opens `openSheetMenu(block, items, btn)`.
- The menu carries exactly two items: 'Play next' and 'Add to queue',
  acting on the whole set through two new set helpers beside
  addToQueue, under addToQueue's kind bound (the set is day-kind;
  against a standing podcast queue both refuse without act):
  - `addToQueueSet(set)`: a queue stands → the set appends at its end,
    in order. A single play stands → the queue forms
    `[current, ...set]`. Nothing stands → a waiting queue of the set
    (Add never starts playback). Native leg mirrors addToQueue.
  - `playNextSet(set)`: a queue stands → the set inserts at
    `queueIdx + 1`, in order. A single play stands → `[current, ...set]`
    at index 0. Nothing stands → `startQueue(set, true)` — the section
    plays (the Play-family rule of §3.7).
  - An empty set (a fully played section): the menu stands, both acts
    no-op — the same truth the grey disc tells.

### 3.9 The pause-toggle edge — kept, named

- `startQueue`'s unforced-tap pause toggle (a disc or Play All tap
  while its queue sounds pauses it) is KEPT. It has stood since v616
  as the control behavior of the very surfaces that birth the queue;
  it governs the transport, never the queue's contents, and it is not
  autoplay machinery. John's model speaks to what enters the queue;
  the toggle changes nothing that enters.
