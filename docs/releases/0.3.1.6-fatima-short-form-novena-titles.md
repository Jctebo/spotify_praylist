# Release 0.3.1.6 - Fatima Short-Form Novena Titles

Date: 2026-05-04
Status: Shipped
Branch: enhancement/fatima-short-form-novena-titles
Depends On: 0.3.1.5

## Scope

### Problem
- When a traditional novena is active, the publisher is only emitting the traditional item and the AI-generated companion is getting lost.
- Our Lady of Fatima exposed the issue because only day 1 of the traditional novena published, which made it look like the paired short-form novena was being suppressed.
- The feed also needs a clearer naming convention so listeners can immediately tell the traditional and AI-generated novenas apart.

### Audience
- Primary user: the maintainer who publishes novenas and needs both versions to appear reliably in the feed.
- Secondary users: listeners who want to know whether they are hearing the traditional or short-form version.
- Secondary stakeholders: future maintainers who need a stable rule for how novena variants are named and selected.

### Current Status Quo
- Traditional novenas already publish with a date-suffixed title like `Traditional Novena to Our Lady of Fatima Day 1 - May 3, 2025`.
- AI-generated novenas still use a more generic title shape and do not clearly identify themselves as the short-form version.
- The novena runtime appears to be collapsing or suppressing one of the active novena variants when both are eligible for the same feast window, so only one version makes it to the published output.

### Goal
- Keep traditional novena titles unchanged apart from their existing publish-date suffix.
- Standardize AI-generated novena titles to `Short-Form Novena to Our Lady of Fatima Day 1 - May 3, 2025` style wording.
- Ensure the traditional and short-form novenas can both publish for the same feast without one replacing the other.
- Success criteria: a Fatima publish produces both novena variants with distinct titles and both remain visible in the feed across reruns.

### Constraints
- Keep the fix narrow to novena resolution, identity, and title generation.
- Do not change unrelated Spotify playlist logic or non-novena publish pipelines.
- Preserve existing traditional novena publish-date behavior.
- Keep the new title format stable so reruns do not rename already-published items.

### Narrow Wedge
- Fix the shared novena selection/title path so same-feast traditional and short-form novenas can coexist, starting with Our Lady of Fatima as the regression case.
- Standardize the AI-generated title prefix across the novena path instead of special-casing just one feast in the UI or feed layer.

### Non-Goals
- Do not redesign the novena engine or liturgical calendar resolution.
- Do not rewrite novena prayer content or change day numbering.
- Do not retroactively rename already-published feed items.
- Do not touch Spotify playlist refresh behavior.

### Risks And Unknowns
- The collision may be happening in resolver selection, episode-id generation, or RSS/feed deduplication, so the exact seam still needs to be confirmed.
- There may be other AI-generated novenas still using the old generic title pattern, which would leave the feed inconsistent if we only fix Fatima.
- If the traditional and short-form novenas still share an identifier, title changes alone will not be enough to make both publish separately.

### Recommended Next Step
- Run `/research-astack` to trace where the novena variants are being collapsed, confirm the right identity/title seam to change, and decide whether the fix belongs in the resolver, contract metadata, or RSS title templates.

## Research

### Scope Baseline
- The requested fix is to let Our Lady of Fatima publish both the traditional novena and a distinct AI-generated short-form novena, while keeping the traditional title format unchanged and standardizing the AI-generated title prefix to `Short-Form Novena ...`.
- The current repo only has one Fatima feast-day contract, and it is a fixed-content traditional novena.
- The current AI-generated novena title pattern is still the generic `Day {day}: Novena to ...` form, so there is no existing short-form naming convention to reuse yet.

### Relevant Files
- `jobs/novena_contracts/resolver.py` - selects active novenas and contains the explicit-feast-vs-selector precedence rule that can suppress one variant when ids collide.
- `jobs/novena_contracts/contracts.py` - defines the default RSS title pattern, builds the publish context, and carries the `saint_name`, `day`, `theme`, and `date_display` fields used by title rendering.
- `jobs/novena_contracts/pipeline.py` - renders the final RSS title from `runtime.publishing.rss.episode_title_pattern`.
- `jobs/novena_contracts/url_import.py` - hardcodes the importer default for traditional novenas as `Traditional Novena to {saint_name} Day {day} - {date_display}`.
- `contracts/novenas/feast-days/our_lady_of_fatima.json` - the only Fatima contract currently checked in, and it is the traditional fixed novena.
- `contracts/novenas/families/standard-9-day.json` - the shared AI-generated family contract, which still uses the generic `Day {day}: Novena to {saint_name} - {theme} - {date_display}` title pattern.
- `jobs/novena_contracts/artifact_writer.py` - writes `episode_id`, `contract_id`, and title into the sidecar that later becomes feed input.
- `jobs/publish/rss.py` - rebuilds the RSS feed and dedupes incoming jobs by `episode_id`, so title changes alone will not keep two variants separate if their ids collide.
- `tests/test_novena_resolver.py` - proves selector-family novenas are intentionally skipped when an explicit feast contract already owns the same celebration.
- `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py` - proves title rendering and feed output for both generic and traditional novena title patterns.
- `tests/test_novena_url_import.py` - proves the importer still emits the traditional title template for imported novenas.

### Current System Map
- Novena identity starts in contract JSON, moves through `load_novena_contracts()`, and is resolved into a `NovenaRuntime` by `resolve_active_novenas(...)`.
- The runtime title is not synthesized from a global “traditional vs short-form” flag; it comes from each contract’s `publishing.rss.episode_title_pattern`.
- The RSS layer dedupes by `episode_id`, and the episode id is derived from date + contract id + active day, so two variants can only coexist if they have distinct contract identities.
- The resolver currently prefers explicit feast contracts over selector-family contracts when ids overlap, which is a deliberate anti-duplication rule but also the most likely place where a paired novena could be getting suppressed.
- There is no existing `Short-Form Novena ...` contract title pattern in the checked-in novena contracts or shared defaults.

### Reuse Opportunities
- Reuse the existing publish context fields: `saint_name`, `day`, `date_display`, and `theme`.
- Reuse the traditional title template path for imported novenas, since that already preserves the existing `Traditional Novena to ...` wording.
- Reuse the standard 9-day family contract as the likely source of the short-form AI-generated title pattern if the fix is contract-based rather than engine-based.
- Reuse the resolver and pipeline test suite to prove both coexistence and title formatting instead of adding a new publish stack.

### External References
- No external API change appears necessary for the naming fix itself.
- If the short-form variant is meant to be selector-driven, Romcal calendar behavior remains relevant because the resolver still uses liturgical rank selection for auto-populated novenas.

### Constraints
- Titles are persisted into sidecars and feed entries, so the new naming scheme must be deterministic for reruns.
- A title change alone will not prevent feed collisions if the two variants share the same `contract_id` or `episode_id`.
- Any coexistence fix must preserve existing traditional novena output and not break other explicit-vs-selector overlaps elsewhere in the novena catalog.

### Risks
- The user-visible symptom may come from missing contract data rather than a rendering bug: Fatima currently has only one checked-in contract, and it is traditional/fixed.
- If the short-form novena reuses the same identity as the traditional one, RSS dedupe will still collapse the pair even after title updates.
- Relaxing the resolver’s explicit-over-selector precedence globally could cause duplicate publishes for other saints that are intentionally deconflicted today.

### Assumptions To Validate
- There is an intended short-form Fatima novena contract or family that should exist but is missing, misnamed, or currently colliding on identity.
- The `Short-Form Novena ...` prefix should become the standard title wording for all AI-generated novenas, not just Fatima.
- The traditional Fatima contract should remain fixed-content and keep its existing traditional title wording.

### Unknowns
- Whether the fix should add a new short-form Fatima contract, rename an existing AI-generated contract, or change the resolver to allow both variants to publish together.
- Whether other AI-generated novenas are still using the generic `Day {day}: Novena to ...` pattern and need a parallel migration.
- Whether the duplicate-suppression rule should be relaxed only for Fatima or refactored into a more explicit variant model.

### Recommended Direction
- Run `/plan-astack` after confirming whether the true fix is a new/renamed short-form contract plus title migration, or a resolver change that allows explicit traditional and AI-generated novenas to coexist without deduping each other.

## Plan

### Summary
- Add a dedicated short-form Fatima novena contract alongside the existing traditional Fatima novena contract, and standardize AI-generated novena titles to `Short-Form Novena to {saint_name} Day {day} - {date_display}`.
- Keep the resolver policy intact because the loader already supports multiple explicit feast contracts on the same date; the bug is missing contract identity and title standardization, not a global selection failure.
- This gives us both outputs for the same feast without loosening the current explicit-vs-selector de-duplication rules for unrelated novenas.

### What Already Exists
- The novena loader already reads explicit feast contracts from `contracts/novenas/feast-days/*.json` and selector-family contracts from `contracts/novenas/families/*.json`.
- `resolve_active_novenas(...)` already returns every active explicit feast contract independently, so two explicit contracts can coexist if their ids are distinct.
- `jobs/novena_contracts/pipeline.py` already renders titles directly from each contract’s `episode_title_pattern`.
- The RSS writer already dedupes by `episode_id`, so distinct contract ids will preserve distinct audio files and feed items.

### Architecture
- We will model the short-form Fatima output as a separate contract with its own `contract_id` and `family_id`, so it can publish on the same feast date as the traditional Fatima contract.
- The short-form contract will reuse the shared 9-day template and the new shared short-form title prefix.
- The traditional Fatima contract will keep its current `Traditional Novena ...` title pattern unchanged.
- The runtime flow stays the same:

```text
contract JSON
  ├─ traditional Fatima feast contract
  └─ short-form Fatima feast contract
        ↓
load_novena_contracts()
        ↓
resolve_active_novenas()   (both explicit contracts survive)
        ↓
render_novena() + title template
        ↓
sidecar JSON + RSS feed
```

### Concrete Changes
- `contracts/novenas/families/standard-9-day.json`
  - Change the shared AI-generated title template from the generic `Day {day}: Novena to {saint_name} - {theme} - {date_display}` form to `Short-Form Novena to {saint_name} Day {day} - {date_display}`.
- `contracts/novenas/feast-days/our_lady_of_fatima_short_form.json`
  - Add a new explicit feast contract for the short-form Fatima novena with its own `id`/`family_id`, the shared novena template, and the short-form title pattern.
- `contracts/novenas/feast-days/our_lady_of_fatima.json`
  - Keep the traditional Fatima contract intact so the existing title stays unchanged.
- `tests/test_novena_resolver.py`
  - Add a regression asserting that two explicit feast contracts for the same feast date both resolve and retain distinct runtime identities.
- `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py`
  - Add a regression proving the traditional Fatima title stays traditional and the short-form Fatima title uses the new prefix.
- `tests/test_novena_contracts.py`
  - Add contract-loading coverage for the new short-form fixture and the shared title pattern change.

### UX And States
- On a successful publish, listeners should see both novena items for the same feast window, with the traditional title and the short-form title clearly differentiated.
- There is no UI loading state change; the observable behavior is the feed and audio archive output.
- Failure should remain fail-closed: if either contract is malformed or disabled, the job should still publish the other valid one rather than collapsing both.

### Implementation Phases
#### Phase 1: Standardize short-form titles
- Goal: make the AI-generated title prefix explicit and uniform.
- Exact files to edit: `contracts/novenas/families/standard-9-day.json`, `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py`, `tests/test_novena_contracts.py`.
- Symbols/interfaces touched: the shared `episode_title_pattern` contract field and the title assertions that depend on it.
- Change summary: remove the generic theme-based title wording from the shared AI-generated family and switch to the short-form prefix.
- Verification steps or commands: `python -m unittest tests.test_novena_contracts tests.test_novena_pipeline`.
- Rollback note: restore the old shared title template if the new wording is too broad for non-Fatima AI-generated novenas.
- Dependencies on earlier phases: none.

#### Phase 2: Add the short-form Fatima contract
- Goal: publish a second Fatima novena without disturbing the traditional one.
- Exact files to edit: `contracts/novenas/feast-days/our_lady_of_fatima_short_form.json`, `tests/test_novena_resolver.py`, `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py`.
- Symbols/interfaces touched: explicit feast contract identity (`id`/`family_id`), novena template reference, and the resolver’s existing explicit-feast path.
- Change summary: add a new explicit feast contract for the short-form Fatima output, with the same liturgical window but a distinct runtime identity.
- Verification steps or commands: `python -m unittest tests.test_novena_resolver tests.test_novena_pipeline`.
- Rollback note: delete the new short-form contract file if we need to revert to the traditional-only state.
- Dependencies on earlier phases: Phase 1 should land first so the new contract uses the final title wording.

#### Phase 3: Full regression sweep
- Goal: prove coexistence, title formatting, and feed uniqueness end to end.
- Exact files to edit: `tests/test_novena_contracts.py`, `tests/test_novena_resolver.py`, `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py`.
- Symbols/interfaces touched: contract loading, active-novena resolution, RSS title rendering, and persisted sidecar metadata.
- Change summary: add test coverage that the two Fatima variants both publish, produce distinct titles, and do not overwrite each other in the feed.
- Verification steps or commands: `python -m unittest tests.test_novena_contracts tests.test_novena_resolver tests.test_novena_pipeline`.
- Rollback note: revert the new regression cases if they prove the coexistence model is not yet supported.
- Dependencies on earlier phases: depends on Phases 1 and 2.

### Failure Modes
- If the short-form contract reuses the traditional contract id, the RSS/archive layer will collapse the two episodes into one.
- If the shared title template is not updated everywhere, the short-form item will still look generic in the feed.
- If the new short-form contract is disabled or malformed, only the traditional Fatima novena will publish.
- We detect these failures by asserting the resolver returns two runtimes, the pipeline writes two sidecars, and the RSS feed contains two distinct titles and guids.

### QA And Test Matrix
- `tests/test_novena_resolver.py`
  - Confirm both explicit Fatima contracts resolve on the same publish date.
  - Confirm the runtime ids and family ids remain distinct.
- `tests/test_novena_pipeline.py`
  - Confirm the traditional title remains `Traditional Novena to Our Lady of Fatima Day 1 - May 3, 2025`.
  - Confirm the short-form title becomes `Short-Form Novena to Our Lady of Fatima Day 1 - May 3, 2025`.
  - Confirm both items survive into the RSS feed with unique `episode_id` values.
- `tests/test_novena_contracts.py`
  - Confirm the new short-form contract loads successfully and preserves the shared title pattern.
- Critical edge cases
  - Same feast date, two explicit contracts.
  - Traditional contract disabled, short-form still publishes.
  - Short-form contract disabled, traditional still publishes.
  - Rerun on the same date should not rename either item.

### Rollout Notes
- No feature flag is needed.
- This is a contract migration plus regression test change; no deploy sequencing is required beyond normal merge and ship.
- Existing published traditional items are left alone; only future publishes pick up the short-form naming update.

### Not In Scope
- Broad resolver-policy rewrites.
- Selector-family de-duplication changes for unrelated saints.
- Retroactive renaming of previously published feed items.
- Spotify playlist behavior.

### Open Questions
- Whether additional AI-generated novenas beyond Fatima should be migrated to the new short-form prefix in the same change or in a follow-up contract pass.
