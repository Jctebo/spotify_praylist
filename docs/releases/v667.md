## §2 Grounding (firsthand, Plan — standing clone, worktree at origin/main `203c624`)

### §2.1 The door's seat, and why it is the first thing lost

The door is unconditional static markup: the **last child** of the player sheet's `.inner`, appended after `.np-controls` in `openNowPlaying` (client.ts:17998), wired at 18005 to `openQueueSheet()`. No render condition hides it — `renderQueueDoor()` (client.ts:5916, called once from `renderQueueList` at 6135) only fills its pos label ("Track X of N · Next: …"). The sheet container is `.sheet.np-sheet .inner { display:flex; flex-direction:column; height:100%; overflow:hidden }` with `.inner > * { flex:none }` (ui.ts:550-552): the column stacks from the top and any overrun is **clipped at the foot** — the door, standing last, is the first element lost, whole and silently.

### §2.2 The budget arithmetic — the cause

v664 sized the artwork to the page by a constant: `--art: clamp(88px, min(72vw, calc(100dvh − 470px)), 340px)` (ui.ts:624), its comment (ui.ts:619-623) pricing the fixed stack at 470px: sheet padding 54 · hero padding/border 34 · grab 15 · art margin 4 · title 33 · meta 30 · **intention 26** · litday 18 · seek 56 · transport 94 · controls 44 · door 56 · slack 6. The intention's 26px is **one line** (14px font × 1.45 line-height ≈ 20.3px + 6px margin). The formula never sees the intention's real height — the artwork yields only for the priced stack.

The field case, from John's screenshot verbatim: "Intention: For Desiree, that God heal every wound from past broken relationships, bless her co-parenting of Levi with Roy, and lead their whole family to peace in Him. Saint Rita, pray for them." At the hero's text width that is **five lines ≈ 108px — 82px over the priced 26**. The title stands at one line (priced), the litday at one line (priced); the intention is the only overrun. Net of the 6px slack, the stack outgrows the sheet by ~76px **at every viewport height** (the overrun is height-independent — the formula subtracts a constant), and the 56px door is clipped whole below the sheet's edge. The ≤660px short-viewport step (ui.ts, `@media (max-height: 660px)`) clamps the intention to two lines and would have bounded the overrun to ~21px against the 88px art floor — John's phone is taller than 660 CSS px, so no clamp stood; his screenshot shows the intention complete at five lines. The screenshot matches the shipped card element for element — v652 bottom-row seats and order, no inline queue — with the door absent exactly where the arithmetic puts it.

**The fixture miss (Improvement D):** harness-v664's fit leg A proves the CSS arithmetic in jsdom (no layout engine) against the 470 constant, and its fixture — the real 2026-11-25 composed day — exercised the *queue*; the card's sounding track stood with no long drawn intention, so the intention's height never entered any proof. Review's Chromium pass measured the same fixture (artwork 281/270/197/98, no scroll). The field case — a real five-line intention — was never a fixture. This release's fixture is that case (§2.4).
