# v624 — Server-side feed resolution: watcher + pointers for every feed, stale warning on the UI

## §1 Scope (with the compressed Plan)

John's direction (voice call, Ora thread, 2026-11-23 ~07:57–08:10 CT): after the 2026-11-23 Divine Office Invitatory + Morning Prayer stood "Not available" in the app, move the matching work OFF the client and onto the server — a scheduled server-side job identifies the day's episodes in the external RSS feeds at intervals, retrying until the day resolves; a client that finds an hour unresolved can nudge the server to resolve it once for everybody. Hard constraint: never violate an RSS provider's terms — the server holds a pointer/index to the provider's own audio; no copying, no re-hosting. Amendments (same call, ~08:06 CT): a stale episode carries a warning on the UI in plain words; the watcher covers EVERY external feed, and the internal Ora feed too as a second net. Approval (verbatim substance): "I approve all the way to ship." Design of record: `v624-server-resolution-design.md` §§1–7 (root cause, exposure table, repair shape, terms position, cost, amendments). Streamlined small-release path: Scope+Plan compressed into this doc, then Implement · light Review · Ship.

Base: main tree f54d8009 (the v623 ship; pin ora-pro-nobis-v623-1, Worker 05096fd5 — the rollback target). Server + client (`app/worker/src/index.ts`, `app/worker/src/client.ts`, `app/wrangler.toml`, one migration); no APK.

### The defect (design §1, grounded)

The Divine Office feed is not in `rss_snapshots`. The eight office hours, the Daily Gospel Exegesis day leg, and the Bible in a Year day leg resolve through the courier leg (`externalsLivePayload`): on each `/api/player/externals/live?date=` request the Worker fetches the provider feed in-request and stores nothing. On 2026-11-23 the provider's feed endpoint returned HTTP 500 / a bot-challenge page / truncated XML all morning (measured, design §1.2); every uncached slot settled "Not available" even though the episodes exist. Saint of the Day and Daily TV Mass carry client-side resolution legs with the same at-open failure shape. The snapshot class (series · courses · novenas catalog · the internal Ora feed) already keeps last-good state in `rss_snapshots`, but refreshes lazily at read time — no scheduled watcher, no flag.

### The repair (design §3 + §7 amendments)

1. **Migration 0047** — new app-D1 table `ext_resolutions`: one row per (date, slot): slot id · state (present/missing) · title · the provider's own enclosure URL (pointer only) · duration · size · image URL · resolved-at timestamp · feed build stamp. Plus `ext_feed_state`: one row per feed key — last successful read at · last attempt at · last error · last build stamp. Prune resolutions older than 35 days (the readings-cache precedent).
2. **Resolver module** in the Worker reusing the STANDING matchers and feed constants (`extMatchDivineOffice`, `extMatchBible`, `extMatchLatestByDate`, `extMatchSaint`, `extMatchTvMass`, `extMatchUsccb`, `extMatchSth`, `extParseFeed`, `extFetchFeed`) — no matcher changes, so no pick can change. One resolver pass resolves, for today + tomorrow (Chicago dates): the eight Divine Office slots · the exegesis day leg · the Bible day leg · Saint of the Day · Daily TV Mass · the Sing the Hours fallback fills (Lauds → Morning Prayer slot, Vespers → Evening Prayer slot, standing fallback rule). Settings gating (enabled + day rule) is read from the standing source-settings payload, exactly as the courier gates today.
3. **Snapshot-class watcher**: the same pass refreshes the standing `rss_snapshots` rows through the standing builders (podRss series incl. the internal `ora-pro-nobis` row · the PodcastIndex course builds · the novenas catalog build), replacing lazy at-read refresh as the primary discipline. The read paths keep their stale-on-failure serve; a watcher-refreshed row simply stands fresh. (Decision D2 below.)
4. **Cron trigger** on the app Worker: every 30 minutes (`*/30 * * * *`), plus a `scheduled` handler that runs one resolver pass. Per-feed cadence inside the pass: the Bible in a Year Fireside feed (~9 MB) is fetched at most every 2 hours (its last-success stamp in `ext_feed_state` gates the fetch; its day-leg resolution stands from the last good read between fetches). Every other feed: every pass.
5. **Failure rule**: a failed read — HTTP error, challenge page, parse failure, timeout, or ZERO-YIELD (a parse with zero items, or a snapshot build with zero entries) — writes nothing for that feed: the last good resolution/snapshot stands. A slot is written `missing` only after a SUCCESSFUL read with no match. "Provider broken" and "episode does not exist" become two different answers.
6. **`/api/player/externals/live` reads the table**: same payload shape as today, built from `ext_resolutions` for the presented date. Each slot entry gains `resolvedAt` (the successful read that produced it) and, where the feed's last successful read is past the freshness threshold, `stale: true`. A slot with no resolution row and no successful read for its date carries `state: "missing"` + `unresolved: true`. A table miss for a slot (a date outside the resolved window) falls back to the standing in-request courier for that slot — never worse than today (design §3.5 step 3).
7. **Client nudge**: `POST /api/player/externals/nudge` (user route) — the client calls it when the live payload carries an unresolved or stale slot for today; the Worker runs one resolver pass for everybody, debounced server-side (a pass that started < 120 s ago is not re-run; the endpoint answers `{ran: boolean}`). An agent-door twin (`POST /api/agent/externals/resolve`) runs a pass on demand for verification and operations.
8. **Client**: the courier-class surfaces consume the live payload as today (it now comes from the table). The client-side Saint of the Day resolution leg retires — the saint slot arrives in the live payload like every other slot (its source-settings row already stands server-side). The Daily TV Mass slot likewise arrives through the payload. The device day cache still stands when the live request itself fails (unchanged).
9. **Stale warning** (§2 D5): a slot entry with `stale: true` paints, on the Praylist row and in the opened view, the plain-words line **"Today's episode has not updated yet — showing the last one that loaded."** An `unresolved: true` slot paints **"Today's episode has not loaded yet — still checking."** in place of the standing "Not available today" dress. A slot missing after a successful read keeps "Not available today", unchanged.

## §2 Decisions

- **D1 — Slot-class store**: `ext_resolutions` (date, slot) rows, per design §3.1. Pointers only: the client streams/downloads from the provider URL exactly as today; no audio bytes are stored or served by the new path; the standing `/externals/audio` pass-through is not widened.
- **D2 — Snapshot-class store**: the watcher refreshes the STANDING `rss_snapshots` rows through the standing builders — no parallel store. Why: one store of record per series; the read paths, their payload shapes, and their stale-on-failure discipline stand untouched; the watcher changes WHEN refresh happens (scheduled + nudged), not where the answer lives. The slot class needs the new table because its answers are (date, slot)-shaped, which `rss_snapshots` (series-shaped) cannot hold.
- **D3 — Freshness thresholds**: slot-class feeds 3 h (design §3.3's proposal, adopted); the stale test reads the FEED's last successful read (`ext_feed_state`), not the slot's resolved-at — the office posts the evening before, so a slot resolved at 20:00 for tomorrow is current, not stale, at 08:00 next morning as long as the feed has answered since 05:00. Snapshot-class: the standing max ages (2 h RSS series · 24 h courses · 2 h novenas) already define staleness and the payloads already carry `stale` — unchanged.
- **D4 — Cadence**: cron every 30 min for every feed except the Bible Fireside feed (2 h gate, design §5). Nudge debounce 120 s.
- **D5 — Warning copy** (final, standing copy dress): stale = "Today's episode has not updated yet — showing the last one that loaded." · unresolved = "Today's episode has not loaded yet — still checking." Both stand on the Praylist row and the opened view.
- **D6 — Shadow deviation (named)**: design §3.5 proposes a one-day shadow before cutover; John's To-Ship approval today overrides the day-long wait. In its place: before the cutover deploy, the resolver runs in shadow for the current day (harness/agent path against the live feeds) and its answers are compared slot-by-slot against the production courier's answers for the same day; the differences are read and recorded in §5. Reason: the approval's urgency + the failure rule makes a wrong pick no worse than today's courier (same matchers, same feeds).
- **D7 — Scope of "every feed"**: Divine Office (8 slots) · Daily Gospel Exegesis · Bible in a Year day leg · Saint of the Day · Daily TV Mass · Sing the Hours fallback · USCCB readings podcast · Exorcist Files · Counsel of Trent · Fr. Mike homilies · Barron sermons · Summa in a Year · the four courses (Bible/Catechism/Rosary/Summa in a Year) · the novenas catalog · the internal Ora feed (second net; the nightly production stays its primary guard). An updated/corrected episode on the internal feed is a different use case (John, named) — not this release's center.
- **D8 — No matcher changes.** Any matcher edit found necessary at Implement is a stop-and-report defect, not an in-chain fix: one matcher, one pick, by construction.
- **D9 — Per-feed stand-down (John, chat, 2026-11-23 08:13 CT)**: once a feed's expected entries for today stand resolved, later cron runs that day SKIP that feed — a cheap stored-state read first (the `ext_resolutions` / snapshot state), NO fetch — until the next day begins. The 30-minute cadence keeps running for feeds still unresolved. The client nudge can still wake a stood-down feed that posts late: a nudge forces one fetch attempt for that feed regardless of stand-down state. "Expected entries stand resolved" reads per class: slot-class — every standing (settings-enabled, day-rule-passing) slot for today carries a resolution written from a successful read today (present or missing-after-successful-read); snapshot-class — the snapshot's built_at stands today (Chicago) from a successful build. Tomorrow's warm-up resolutions do not stand a feed down for today. **Cost (recorded per John's note)**: the whole loop is one Worker invocation per tick covering all feeds — ~1,440 invocations/month at the 30-minute cadence — inside the existing Workers Paid included allowance; marginal cost effectively $0, and stand-down cuts the fetch volume well below even that.
