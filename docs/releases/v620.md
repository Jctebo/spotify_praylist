### The fix (minimal seam, no redesign)

In `trackDesc` (`app/worker/src/client.ts`):

- The URL read gains the resolver fallback: `url: t.streamUrl || t.url || null`. Standing families carry `streamUrl`/`enclosureUrl` and no bare `url`, so their output is byte-identical.
- A `t.kind === 'podcast'` input's identity rides the seam whole — `kind`, `podSeries`, `seriesKey`, `listened`, `podPositionS`, and the family flags (`course590` / `exegesis611` / `episode614`) are copied onto the output descriptor, in `podDesc`'s field set. Consequences, each correct by the standing design: the entry plays from the Praylist (element src = the enclosure); a start seats the podcast resume from `podPositionS` (the v617 discipline now reaches these descriptors on the element path too); completion marks the SERIES listen through the standing mark path; the v615/v618 unplayed predicate reads the descriptor's own listened flag (v515); the native contract builder reads the descriptor as a podcast, as it reads `podDesc`'s.
