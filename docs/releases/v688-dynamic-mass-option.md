### §2.2 The mechanism decision — a SIBLING kind, not an extension of `daily`

**Decision: a new contents kind stands beside `daily`** (working name `massdaily`; the exact kind string is Implement's craft, used identically at every seat below). The v666 kind is NOT extended. Reasons, grounded:

1. **The set is closed and series-keyed by construction.** `OPN_DAILY_SERIES_666` (client:2851) + `opnDailyKeyOk666` (client:2852) admit five keys, every one a series on the podcasts route; the resolver `resolveDailyDynamic666` (client:20729) fetches `/api/player/podcasts?series=<key>` per series and reads `payload.entries` / `payload.videos`. The v666 doc records the set as closed: a `daily` item naming any other key is an error at both validators and filters out at the read seam.
2. **The audio Mass is not on that route.** Source #1 has no series payload at all — it resolves through `ext_resolutions` and the externals/live payload (§2.1 #1). Extending `daily` would force a second source class inside a kind whose validators, settled store, title seats, and ladder row all assume a series key — two mechanisms wearing one kind, with the key predicate (`opnDailyKeyOk666`) no longer saying what a key IS.
3. **One act, one shape for the user.** The three video Masses COULD extend `daily` (they are series on the route) — but then "today's Mass" would stand as two different kinds depending on medium, with two dupe seats, two title seats, and two settled stores. One sibling kind gives every Mass source the same card, the same item shape, and the same rules; the resolver dispatches per key class inside the one kind (§2.4).

**What the sibling kind reuses whole** (the v666/v677 machinery, unchanged): the contents-item model `{kind, key}` · the settled-pick discipline (§2.4) · the not-updated shape and its paint (§2.6) · per-day marks by construction (§2.7) · the gold card's dress and ladder discipline (§2.5).

### §2.4 The resolver — per source, per day

Working name `resolveMassDynamic688(day)`, in the `resolveDailyDynamic666` pattern (client:20729): jobs from the effective contents grouped by key, one fetch per key per day build, results in a resolver map keyed `block + ':' + key`, settle by `extRebuildDescs(day)` + `paintPrayersView`. **The settled-pick store (v677 carried whole).** The v666 daily row settles its episode ONCE for the presented (Chicago) date and holds it all day (`opn-daily-settled-v1`, client:5111; the settle logic in `resolveDailyDynamic666`, client:20729 ff — a late-landing episode stands tomorrow, never mid-day). The Mass kind carries the same discipline in its own store, prescribed shape identical:

- Key: `opn-mass-settled-v1` (localStorage, beside the played store — a per-device presentation fact; no migration, no route).

- The matcher runs ONLY for jobs with no settled entry for (presented, block, key); its result — the not-updated fallback included — is written to the store before the settle repaint. The date turn is the only advance.

**The matchers** (`presented = day.presentedDay || day.date`). NOT-UPDATED fallback: the presented date's entry stands stale, or stands missing/unresolved → read the PREVIOUS date's live payload; its present entry is the fallback descriptor with `stale: true`.
