# v643 — A page for each track

## §1 Scope

Scope-gated feature (DevOps Board row 1620). **The request (John, in the Ora Pro Nobis thread, 2026-11-23 20:13 CT, verbatim):** "Does each track have its own page. The share sheet went to the playlist? I would like it to go to a track page where the user can just hit play"

**The grounded fact:** no track has its own page today. The v640 share link carries the track's identity into the app and lands the receiver on the track **inside its list** (the Praylist) — the playlist landing John saw when he tested the share in the field. There is no per-track page URL and no per-track page view; a shared link can only land inside a list.

**The item, as read:** each shareable track gains **its own page** — a page for that one track, carrying the track, its play act, and its text — and the share link goes to **that page**, where the receiver can just hit play. The v640 share seats (the player's Share button and menu row, the row-menu Share acts) and its app-link discipline stand; what changes is where the link lands.

**The item HOLDS at Scope** until John approves the package and sets the run level. Nothing is built by this phase.

## §2 The package

**The package:** Scope Management Space package **94** (status: awaiting), one self-contained interactive HTML document at `~/workspace/your_files/v643-scope-a-page-for-each-track/v643-scope-a-page-for-each-track.html` — decisions selectable and defaulted to the recommendations, a prompt box with a Copy button, a comment box under each question plus a general box, and a mockup of the proposed track page. **Recommended run level: Stop before Ship** (a new receiver-facing page — worth John's eye on the built surface before it goes live). **Deviation: none** — the package proposes exactly the track page John asked for.

**The three decisions, with the recommended option first:**

1. **Contents — what stands on the track page:** **the track, its play act, and its text** — the track's title and standing line, one clear Play act that plays that track, and the track's readable text where the track has text (the standing Read discipline). The alternative — the play act alone — gives the receiver nothing of the track beyond its name.
2. **Access — who can open the page:** **anyone with the link, in any browser, no account** — the public build's standing rule (no accounts, no sign-in anywhere in the public shape) holds on the new page; the receiver opens the link and plays. The alternative — app shapes only — puts a wall between a shared link and the person it was shared with.
3. **Reach — which tracks get a page:** **every track the share covers** — the same set v640's share reaches (day tracks, podcast episodes, the Divine Office hours, prayers with audio, recorded homilies, Ora's own episodes), each landing on its own page. The alternative — episodes only — leaves the prayer's share still landing in a list, against the ask.

Defaults keep everything else as it stands: the share payload stays title + app link with no personal data, the landing never autoplays (the receiver hits play), and an identity no page can name falls back to the plain app exactly as v640 shipped.

### 3.1 Grounded current state (base = main at pin ora-pro-nobis-v649-1; `app/worker/src/client.ts` unless named)

**The v640 link builder — one seat, four identity forms.** `shareAppUrlFor(t)` (~16391–16414), under the constant `SHARE_APP_BASE640 = 'https://app.orapronobis.media'` (~16389): it composes only the app host + identity parameters, so no external or Spotify URL can reach a share payload. The office identity rides the day form: an office hour's descriptor is born in `extSlotTrack(slot, st)` (~17585) with `episodeId: slot.slotId`, `kind: 'external'`, and its source's whole record (title · streamUrl · imageUrl · descText), so its link is `?s=day&date=<date>&id=<slotId>`. The only fallback is the bare app link. Every Share seat funnels here through `shareTrackSheet(t)` (~16424) → `openShareSheet(o)` (~16338): the player's `#np-share` and menu row, the day-row / podcast-row / Favorites-mirror menus.

**The current landing.** `shareLandingRead()` (~16442; called once at boot, ~25454) strictly validates the query (date shape + real calendar date; ids/series in the descriptor charset, ≤128 chars; a present-but-invalid `b`/`o` refuses the whole landing), stashes `shareLanding`, and `history.replaceState` strips the query whether the landing is consumed or refused. `shareLandingConsume()` / `shareLandingLandDay()` (~16500–16680) then land the receiver **inside the app's lists**: day → `setTab('prayers')`, the descriptor's block page via `openBlockPage`, the row marked `.landed` and scrolled into view, an episode card via `openEpisodeCard` where `epCardIsEpisode(d)`; pod → the series page + the entry's episode card; prayer → `openPrayerPage(slug)`; homily → the Homilies side of the podcasts tab with the entry's card marked. **Text seats per family, as shipped.** Ora own episode: the descriptor's `descText` (feed description) plus the read-along text where `hasText` stands — `textUrl: '/api/player/text/' + date + '/' + episodeId` (~19475), served by the Worker at `index.ts` ~5701 (`GET /api/player/text/<date>/<episodeId>` from R2 `texts/<date>/<episodeId>.txt`, a named 404 when absent). Prayer: the book — `bookFind(slug)` (~11979) over the `/api/prayer/book` payload; `openPrayerPage(slug)` (~15055) paints title, source line, the body (`bookBodyHtml(p.body)`, Latin switch where `bodyLatin` stands), and its audio row from `prayerAudioDesc(p, lang)`. **The public surface already stands.** On the app host the Worker runs the public gate, not `johnOnly`: `isPublicHost` + `publicGate` (`index.ts` ~146–200) — the shell and static assets are the public face, non-GET is refused, personal reads answer their empty shapes, and `PUBLIC_READS` admits exactly the reads a track page needs (`player: day · feed-audio · text · sources · source-settings · externals · podcasts` · `prayer: day · day-manifest · mass · mass-readings · book · audio` · `wallpaper`). The shell itself serves only at `/` and `/index.html` (`index.ts` ~6295); any other pathname answers 404 — a path-form track URL would need a new Worker route, the query form needs none (the v640 precedent: "client only, no Worker change — the query form rides the standing pathname shell serve").

**The homily store — device-local, the only one of its kind.** The v551 module header (~13735–13757) states Q1 C: LOCAL ONLY — "A recording lives on this device, plays from this device, and issues NO network call of any kind." Two device-local stores kept in step: IndexedDB **`opn-homilies`** (`HOMILY_DB_NAME`, ~13760; object stores `takes` + `chunks`) holding the audio, and the localStorage index **`opn-homilies-v551`** (`HOMILY_INDEX_KEY`, ~13758; metadata only — `homilyEntry(id)` / `homilyIndex()`, ~13780–13815). The audio blob is assembled on device by **`homilyTakeBlob(id)`** (~13923): the entry's chunks in `seq` order joined as one `Blob` of the entry's `mimeType`; `homilyMint` (~13933) wraps it in the session blob URL the descriptor plays (`homilyDescFor`, ~13950: `episodeId: 'homily:' + id`, `descText: null`). Note-only entries (state `'note'`, v561) carry no chunks and no audio. A full sweep of the client finds **one** `indexedDB.open` — this store. Every other share family resolves from server or publisher payloads (own episodes from the feed/R2, office slots server-resolved, podcasts from RSS payloads, prayers from the book payload + R2 audio); the Cache Storage offline copies (`opn-ext-audio-v1`) are copies of those same server/feed enclosures, not device-origin content. **Conclusion for §3.6's check: recorded homilies are the ONLY local-only family the share covers.** Personal device data (Conscience, intentions, profiles) has no share seat and is out of reach by construction.

**The v644 native bridge carries text only.** `nativeShareAvailable644()` / `nativeShareTrack(o)` (~16313–16335): in the APK shell the client calls `oraBridgePlugin().shareTrack({ title, text, url })` — three strings. On the Android side (v644 §2/§3/§5, repo Jctebo/ora-pro-nobis-android): `OraDayBridgePlugin.kt`'s `@PluginMethod fun shareTrack(call)` builds `ACTION_SEND` with type `text/plain`, `EXTRA_SUBJECT` + `EXTRA_TEXT`, and starts `Intent.createChooser`. **No file, no URI, no `EXTRA_STREAM`, no FileProvider stands in the bridge today — a file payload cannot ride it.** Out of shell, `openShareSheet`'s Share… row calls `navigator.share({ title, text, url })`; where neither stands, the sheet carries Copy link alone.

### 3.2 The page route — same URL, new landing

**The link does not change.** `shareAppUrlFor` stands byte-for-byte for the page families (prayer · pod · day incl. office), so every link already shared keeps working and lands on the new page. What changes is the landing: `shareLandingConsume`'s per-family branches stop landing inside the lists and instead open **the track page** — one client view for the one track the identity names, at the app host, in the standing shell. No Worker route is added; the query form rides the `/` shell serve exactly as v640 shipped, and the public gate (§3.1) already admits every read the page makes, signed-out, in a plain browser tab.

**One amendment to the builder — the office source parameter.** For a `kind === 'external'` descriptor, `shareAppUrlFor`'s day form gains `&src=<source>`: the descriptor's own `source` label (e.g. `Divine Office` · `Sing the Hours`), URL-encoded; `shareLandingRead` validates it against the standing source labels the slot records carry (a `src` outside that set refuses the whole landing, the `b`/`o` discipline). §3.7 gives the reason: under v650 two tracks share one slotId, and paint position does not name a source — `src` does. A link built before this release carries no `src` and lands on the slot's standing (preference-first) track, exactly the track its sender saw first.

**The view.** A new `openTrackPage643(landing)` in `client.ts`, painted into `view` in the `openPrayerPage` posture (its own `state.prayersView`-style view record so tab switches and the day build's repaints retire it by the standing rules, never under it): the track's image large (`imageUrl`, `/logo.png` fallback, the `rowArtHtml` dress at page size), the title, the standing line (`shareLineFor640`'s per-family line + duration), **one large Play button**, the text block where the family carries text (§3.3), and a quiet "Open in Ora Pro Nobis" act that drops the page view and boots the plain app standing view. Nothing else stands on the page: no queue acts, no star, no mark, no menu — and **no intention draw** (the page never calls `trackDrawTextFor` / `ensureTrackDraw`; §3.5).

**Resolution — the standing machinery, re-aimed.** The page resolves its descriptor through the same reads and bounded polls `shareLandingConsume` already runs, then paints instead of list-landing:

- **day** (own episodes + office): the day fetch/build for the landing's date (`api('/api/player/day?date=')` → `buildDayFromPayload`, as the day branch does today), then select from `state.dayDescs` by `episodeId`, with `b`/`o` matched together on the same descriptor where carried (the standing exact-match rule), and — for an external descriptor — `src` matched against the descriptor's `source` where carried. No exact match at the poll bound → discard → plain app.
- **pod**: `openPodSeries(series)`'s payload read, the entry by `key`, the descriptor from `podDesc(e)` / `podOwnDesc(e)` exactly as the episode card builds it.
- **prayer**: `bookFind(slug)` over the book payload (the prayer branch's poll already retries `renderPrayerBook()` once); the page's descriptor is `prayerAudioDesc(p, lang)` with the identity's language segment (`prayer:<slug>:<lang>`) preserved into the parse — where the prayer's audio stands for that language; the text is the book body (§3.3).

**Play.** The large Play calls the standing funnel on the resolved descriptor and nothing else: `playTrack(desc)` (podcast descriptors ride the same funnel their episode-card `onPlay` reaches — `playPodFrom` / `playPodOwnFrom` where the card's constructor requires the series context, decided at the same construction seat). Play is the receiver's tap only — **never autoplay** (the v640 discipline stands). A tap on the standing current toggles pause through the funnel's own rules, as every other seat does. The page's Play stands disabled until its descriptor resolves `available` with a URL; an identity whose track resolves unavailable (e.g. an office slot standing `missing` for that date) discards to the plain app at the bound rather than painting a dead page.

### 3.4 Stale / unknown identity — the standing rule, unchanged

Any identity that fails validation at the read, or fails to resolve exactly at its poll bound, is **discarded and the app boots as the plain app** — no error page, no partial page, no dead Play. The query is stripped at the read whether consumed or refused (standing). **The homily identity form is retired as a landing**: `shareLandingRead` still parses `?s=homily` (old links in the wild must not error), and its consume branch now discards it to the plain app — no page exists for it, and the Homilies-list landing it used to perform retires with it. Links built by older builds for in-day homilies (`?s=day&id=homily:…`) resolve no descriptor on any other device (the store is device-local) and already fall to the plain app at the bound; that behavior is now the named rule for the family.

### 3.5 Q2 public-surface discipline

The page serves **only what the track itself carries publicly**, from the public reads alone (§3.1): the day payload under `pub`, the podcasts payload (listen state stripped at the route), the book payload, the text route, the externals reads. It must never read or paint: drawn intentions or any intention text, Conscience / exam / confession content, Universal Prayer or personal-prayer folds, day additions, played state, positions, or anything from the device vault — the `pub` builders already withhold every one of those server-side, and the page adds no client-side read of them (no `trackDraws`, no favorites, no played chrome). The page works signed-out in a plain browser tab on the public host; in the APK and the installed PWA the same view stands (the landing is shape-blind, as v640's was). The share payload discipline is unchanged: title + line + app link, no personal data (harness-v640 L5 stands as a regression).

### 3.6 The homily attachment share (the carve-out)

**Sender-side split.** `shareTrackSheet(t)` gains the homily branch at its head — before `openShareSheet` / `shareAppUrlFor` are reached: any descriptor with `kind === 'homily'` or a `homily:` episodeId, **standing anywhere** (the Homilies list, a day addition, the player) routes to the homily file share. This closes a standing leak in the carve-out's favor: today an in-day homily falls through the builder to a `?s=day` link (§3.1) that no receiver can ever resolve. A homily descriptor never produces a link again. Note-only entries (state `'note'`, no chunks) carry no audio file: their Share act shares the note **text** through the standing text-share route (bridge `shareTrack` / `navigator.share` with the note as text, no URL), falling back to the copy sheet carrying the note text — named here so Implement does not invent a file for them.

**The blob.** The file comes from the device store and nowhere else: `homilyEntry(homilyId)` for the metadata (`name`, `mimeType`), **`homilyTakeBlob(homilyId)`** for the assembled audio `Blob` (IndexedDB `opn-homilies`, `chunks` in `seq` order). The shared file's name is the entry's name with the extension its `mimeType` names (`.webm` · `.m4a` · `.mp4` · `.ogg` as recorded). No upload, no server copy, no link is created at any point — the file moves device → share sheet → the receiver's chosen app, only where the platform allows an attachment.

**APK shape — a bridge extension IS required; a new APK IS part of this release.** The v644 `shareTrack` method carries `{ title, text, url }` strings into a `text/plain` `ACTION_SEND` (§3.1) — it cannot carry a file. Implement adds one method to the same `OraDayBridge` plugin in Jctebo/ora-pro-nobis-android:

- `@PluginMethod fun shareFile(call)` — reads `title`, `fileName`, `mimeType`, and the audio as **base64** (`dataBase64`); on the activity's UI thread (the standing bridge posture) it writes the decoded bytes to a file under the app cache dir, obtains a `content://` URI for it through a **FileProvider** (a new `file_paths` entry for the cache dir + the provider declaration, if the shell does not already declare one — Implement verifies and names it), and starts `Intent.createChooser` with `ACTION_SEND`, type = the mime type, `EXTRA_SUBJECT` = title, `EXTRA_STREAM` = the content URI, and `FLAG_GRANT_READ_URI_PERMISSION`. A null activity rejects `share-no-activity: …`; a write/start failure rejects `share-unavailable: <ExClass>: <msg>` — the v644 named-error discipline, so the client fallback below engages on evidence.
- Client side: `nativeShareFileAvailable643()` probes `oraInShell()` + `typeof p.shareFile === 'function'` (the `nativeShareAvailable644` pattern); `nativeShareFile(homilyId)` assembles the blob via `homilyTakeBlob`, encodes it base64, and calls the bridge with `{ title, fileName, mimeType, dataBase64 }`. **Size risk, named for Implement:** a single base64 string inflates the take by ~⅓ and crosses the bridge in one call; homily takes are phone-mic recordings of a few minutes (the store's own `sizeBytes` is on the index entry). Implement measures the standing takes' sizes first; if a realistic take risks the bridge's practical string limits, the method ships in its chunked form instead — `shareFileBegin` / `shareFileChunk` / `shareFileEnd` appends, the shape the bridge already proves for native takes (`readNativeTakeChunk` + `HOMILY_NATIVE_CHUNK` = 262144, ~14175). Either form is one bridge surface with the same client contract; the harness (§3.8, G5) tests the contract, not the chunking.
- **APK consequence, plain:** versionCode 53 → **54**, versionName "1.0.53" → **"1.0.54"**; built and served by the estate's standing APK route (v644 §2 precedent: push to the Android repo main, the Actions signed build produces the APK, signer certificate verified identical to the standing APK's, byte-identical serve at the standing APK URL). **John installs it manually** — that install is a field leg of this release, and until it lands his phone stands on the fallback below. No other release's APK is touched.

**Web shapes (installed PWA / plain tab).** Where `navigator.share` stands with file support — tested at tap time as `navigator.canShare && navigator.canShare({ files: [file] })` with `file = new File([blob], fileName, { type: mimeType })` — the Share… act calls `navigator.share({ title, files: [file] })` and the platform sheet carries the attachment. Where file sharing is not supported (desktop tabs, browsers without `canShare` files), and on an **old APK** whose bridge lacks `shareFile`, the standing behavior stands, named plainly: the share sheet offers **Copy link alone**, the copied `?s=homily` link lands the receiver on the plain app (§3.4) — no recording moves, no dead row, no error; the sheet's line for a homily reads that the recording itself can be sent from the app on the phone. This is a fallback, not the feature: the feature is the attachment, on the APK and on file-capable web shapes.

### 3.7 Overlap with v650 (in flight, its own branch)

v643 composes against main at ship time; either landing order must work. Shared seams, named:

1. **The office identity (the obvious seam).** v650 keeps the identity exactly as today — `episodeId = slotId`, `_occ` per contents occurrence — while standing **two** descriptors on that one identity for the labeled slots (Morning Prayer · Evening Prayer), distinguished by the descriptor's `source`. v643's `&src=` (§3.2) is built for exactly that shape: the share builder takes `src` from the descriptor shared, and the page's selection matches it against the descriptor's `source` — the field `extSlotTrack` already stamps and v650 §3.3 itself re-keys on ("the labeled branch takes `&src=` from the DESCRIPTOR's source"). If v643 lands first, `src` stands unused-but-harmless on the one-track slot (the single descriptor's source matches it) and v650's two-track paint inherits a working disambiguator; if v650 lands first, v643's page never lands a receiver on the wrong source's episode — without `src`, `b`/`o` name paint position, not source, and the gate G2 below would be unpassable under 'Both'.
2. **The slot record shape.** v650 retires the one-record choice (`backup` retires with it; both sides stand as tracks). v643's page therefore reads **descriptors** (`state.dayDescs` after the day fold) and never `state.extSlots[slotId].backup` directly — under either order the fold presents the track(s) the page selects from, and the pre-v650 shape (one descriptor whose record carries the chosen side whole, v648/v649) satisfies the same selection with one candidate.
3. **The landing timers.** `shareLandingLandDay`'s re-present timers carry the v642 composition repair; v643 replaces the list-landing presentation with the page paint at that same seat and keeps the bounded re-present guard's shape (re-paint the page while neither it nor another view stands, retire when the user has moved on) — no timer or hook is added or removed.
4. **No shared files beyond `client.ts`.** v650 is client-JS with no new APK (its §1); v643's Android work stands in the other repo on its own branch of work and cannot conflict. `docs/releases/v643.md` and `app/harness-v643.mjs` are this release's alone.
