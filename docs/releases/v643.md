# v643 — A page for each track

## §1 Scope

Scope-gated feature (DevOps Board row 1620). **The request (John, in the Ora Pro Nobis thread, 2026-11-23 20:13 CT, verbatim):** "Does each track have its own page. The share sheet went to the playlist? I would like it to go to a track page where the user can just hit play"

**The grounded fact:** no track has its own page today. The v640 share link carries the track's identity into the app and lands the receiver on the track **inside its list** (the Praylist) — the playlist landing John saw when he tested the share in the field. There is no per-track page URL and no per-track page view; a shared link can only land inside a list.

**The item, as read:** each shareable track gains **its own page** — a page for that one track, carrying the track, its play act, and its text — and the share link goes to **that page**, where the receiver can just hit play. The v640 share seats (the player's Share button and menu row, the row-menu Share acts) and its app-link discipline stand; what changes is where the link lands.

**The item HOLDS at Scope** until John approves the package and sets the run level. Nothing is built by this phase.

## §2 The package

**The package:** Scope Management Space package **94** (status: awaiting), one self-contained interactive HTML document at `~/workspace/your_files/v643-scope-a-page-for-each-track/v643-scope-a-page-for-each-track.html` — decisions selectable and defaulted to the recommendations, a prompt box with a Copy button, a comment box under each question plus a general box, and a mockup of the proposed track page. **Recommended run level: Stop before Ship** (a new receiver-facing page — worth John's eye on the built surface before it goes live). **Deviation: none** — the package proposes exactly the track page John asked for.

**The three decisions, with the recommended option first:**

1. **Contents — what stands on the track page:** **the track, its play act, and its text** — the track's title and standing line, one clear Play act that plays that track, and the track's readable text where the track has text (the standing Read discipline). The alternative — the play act alone — gives the receiver nothing of the track beyond its name.
2. **Access — who can open the page:** **anyone with the link, in any browser, no account** — the public build's standing rule (no accounts, no sign-in anywhere in the public shape) holds on the new page; the receiver opens the link and plays. The alternative — app shapes only — puts a wall between a shared link and the person it was shared with.
3. **Reach — which tracks get a page:** **every track the share covers** — the same set v640's share reaches (day tracks, podcast episodes, the Divine Office hours, prayers with audio, recorded homilies, Ora's own episodes), each landing on its own page. The alternative — episodes only — leaves the prayer's share still landing in a list, against the ask.

Defaults keep everything else as it stands: the share payload stays title + app link with no personal data, the landing never autoplays (the receiver hits play), and an identity no page can name falls back to the plain app exactly as v640 shipped.
