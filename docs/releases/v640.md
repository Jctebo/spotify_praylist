# v640 — Share a track from the player

## §1 Scope

Scope-gated feature (DevOps Board row 1617). **The request (John, voice call in the main chat, 2026-11-23 17:51 CT, verbatim fragment):** "Specific track and I want to be able to share that directly from the player."

**The item, as read:** a **Share act in the player (Now Playing) for the currently playing track** — while a track sounds, the player itself offers to share that track, without leaving the player for the track's row or series page. Row menus already carry a Share act (it stands in the day-row menu today); what does not stand anywhere is a share for the track that is playing, from the surface where it plays. This release adds the player's own Share for the standing track — nothing else moves.

**The item HOLDS at Scope** until John approves the package and sets the run level. Nothing is built by this phase.

## §2 The package

**The package:** Scope Management Space package **92** (status: awaiting), one self-contained interactive HTML document at `~/workspace/your_files/v640-scope-share-a-track-from-the-player/v640-scope-share-a-track-from-the-player.html` — decisions selectable and defaulted to the recommendations, a prompt box with a Copy button, a comment box under each question plus a general box, and mockups of the player as it stands and as proposed. **Recommended run level: Stop before Ship** (a new act on the player's surface — worth John's eye on the built surface before it goes live). **Deviation: none** — the package proposes exactly the share-from-the-player John asked for, on the seats that exist today.

**The four decisions, with the recommended option first:**

1. **Payload — what a share carries:** the track's **title + its own link where one exists** (the standing `shareUrlFor` discipline: the track's own episode/file link where the track has one). The alternative — title only, no link — leaves the receiver with nothing to open.
2. **Mechanism — how the share leaves the phone:** the **phone's share sheet** (the native share the row-menu Share already uses), with **copy to the clipboard as the fallback** where no share sheet stands (the web shapes). No new sending machinery, no in-app composer.
3. **Seat — where the act stands in the player:** **both** the player's own menu **and a share button beside the transport controls** — the menu for parity with every other player act, the button because the ask is to share *directly* from the player, in the moment, without opening a menu. The button takes the standing icon-button dress of the transport row; no control moves.
4. **Reach — which tracks it covers:** **every track the player can play** — day tracks, podcast episodes, the Divine Office hours, recorded homilies, Ora's own episodes alike; the act stands wherever a track stands in the player, and its payload follows decision 1 per track (a track with no link of its own shares its title with the standing fallback link discipline, named in the package mockup).

Defaults keep today's behavior everywhere else: the row-menu Share is untouched, the player's transport and queue behavior are untouched, and nothing is shared automatically — the act fires only on John's tap.

### The build

**One new builder — `shareAppUrlFor(t)`** in `client.ts`, beside `shareUrlFor`: returns an absolute `https://app.orapronobis.media/…` URL built from the descriptor's identity fields (fact 5), classifying in this order — prayer (`episodeId` begins `prayer:`) → homily (`kind:'homily'` or `episodeId` begins `homily:`, when the descriptor does not stand in the presented day's `state.dayDescs`) → podcast (`kind:'podcast'` with `podSeries`; or an own-series descriptor while `state.podcasts` stands open and the descriptor is not a presented-day row) → day (the descriptor stands in `state.dayDescs`, or a presented date stands) → **bare app link** (no classifiable identity — the only fallback). `shareTrackSheet` and every track-share caller named in the row-menu decision below call this builder instead of `shareUrlFor` / their inline `url:` values. `shareUrlFor` itself stands unedited for any non-track caller; no Spotify or external URL can reach a share payload through the new builder by construction (it composes only the constant host + identity parameters).

**Per-family payload table (under the amendment):** the sheet head and text keep the standing shape — `navigator.share({ title, text: title + ' — ' + line, url })`, the sheet's `line` as in the table; the copy act keeps its standing form (the link only, note `Link copied.`).

| Family | Title | Line | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Podcast episode | `t.title` (the `podDesc` form: `<label>: <episode title>`) | `Episode · <duration>` (standing) | `https://app.orapronobis.media/?s=pod&series=<podSeries>&id=<seriesKey>` |
| Ora's own episode (day) | `t.title` | `<date label> · Episode · <duration>` | `https://app.orapronobis.media/?s=day&date=<presented date>&id=<episodeId>` (+ `&b=<block>&o=<occ>` when the id repeats in the day) |
| Ora's own episode (series page) | `t.title` | `Episode · <duration>` | `?s=pod&series=<state.podcasts.series>&id=<episodeId>` |

**Landing-route spec (to-build, minimal — client only, no Worker change):** a new `shareLandingRead()` in `client.ts`, run once at boot before `setTab(bootTab)`: parse `location.search` for `s` + parameters, validate strictly (date must match `YYYY-MM-DD`; ids/series restricted to the descriptor charset — letters, digits, `-`, `_`, `:`), stash the parsed landing, and `history.replaceState` the query away after it is consumed or refused. Consumption, after the relevant standing payload settles:
- `s=day` — present that date through the standing day machinery (the `/api/prayer/day?date=` fetch + presented-day paint), find the descriptor in the rebuilt `state.dayDescs` by `id` (+ `b` / `o` where carried), then land: Prayers tab → that track's block view → its row scrolled into view and highlighted; where `epCardIsEpisode` holds, open its episode card (`openEpisodeCard`). **Never autoplay.**
- `s=pod` — `openPodSeries(series)` (standing, ~line 9293), then land on the entry whose key is `id` (episode card where the series page offers one, else the row highlighted).
- `s=prayer` — open the Book at the prayer's page for `prayer:<slug>:<lang>` (the standing `praylistLanding` / book-page seam).
- `s=homily` — resolve `homilyEntry(id)`; land on the Homilies surface with that entry presented.
- **Unknown or stale identity** (bad date, a day with no such id, an unknown series/slug/homily, any malformed parameter): discard the landing and boot exactly as today — **the app, never an error**.

**Mechanism spec (Q2 A — per app shape):** unchanged machinery — the act opens the standing `openShareSheet`: in the **APK shape** and the **PWA/tab shape** alike, the `Share…` row stands where `navigator.share` stands (the WebView and the browser both expose it as they do for today's row share; no native bridge is added) and fires the system share sheet with the payload above; where no sheet stands, the `Copy link` row — which always stands — writes the app link to the clipboard with the standing `Link copied.` note (fallback note carries the link in text). No in-app composer, no new sending machinery.

**THE ROW-MENU DECISION: the row-menu Share MOVES to the app link too.** Every track-share caller routes through `shareAppUrlFor`: `shareTrackSheet` (day-row menu · `[data-rowshare]` · the Favorites mirror), `openPodRowMenu`'s inline sheet call, and `openFavRowMenu`'s podcast-mirror sheet call.
