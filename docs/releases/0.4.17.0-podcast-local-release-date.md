## Scope

### Problem
- The Oct 12 audio workflow generated Oct 13 daily prayer and novena episodes, but the Oct 12 devotional-image workflow later redeployed an older podcast feed built from a stale audio cache.
- RSS `pubDate` is noon UTC (7:00 AM Chicago time), which can also keep current-date episodes hidden in podcast apps during the early morning.

### Audience
- Primary user: listeners expecting the daily prayer and novena podcast episodes on their Chicago-local calendar date.
- Secondary stakeholders: podcast apps consuming the shared RSS feed.

### Current Status Quo
- `jobs/publish/rss.py::_published_at` combines each episode's `published_date` with 12:00 UTC.
- The Oct 12 scheduled run logged Oct 13 episode IDs, and its Pages artifact contains Oct 13 items dated `Tue, 13 Oct 2026 12:00:00 +0000`.
- At 5:08 AM Chicago time on Oct 13, the live feed showed Oct 12 as its newest date, while the Oct 12 audio workflow artifact contained Oct 13 items.
- On Oct 12, the devotional-image workflow restored the audio archive cache at 15:27 UTC, before the audio workflow saved its updated cache at 15:45 UTC; it then deployed Pages at 15:56 UTC, after the audio workflow deployed at 15:46 UTC. That legacy image-workflow audio publisher is removed by this release.
- Daily prayer and novena items share the RSS builder, so the same publication timestamp rule affects both.

### Goal
- Retire the entire legacy devotional image workflow, including image generation, OneDrive sync, audio archive rebuild, and Pages deployment. Keep the dedicated audio publisher and separate devotional-wallpaper workflow running.
- Make each episode eligible for podcast app display at the start of its published date in America/Chicago.
- Keep episode IDs, titles, dates, ordering, retention, and audio URLs unchanged.

### Constraints
- Preserve the `published_date` calendar date and existing RSS metadata.
- Disable and remove the legacy devotional image workflow.
- Keep the dedicated audio publisher responsible for the website and podcast feed.
- Use a DST-aware `America/Chicago` timezone when constructing RSS publication timestamps.
- Include the IANA timezone data package in the base dependencies so `ZoneInfo` also works on Windows developer machines.
- Do not change the dedicated audio schedule or delete the dormant generator source, local runner scripts, or stored images.

### Narrow Wedge
- Retire the legacy devotional image workflow and its competing Pages publisher.
- Change the shared RSS publication timestamp from noon UTC to midnight America/Chicago for each episode's `published_date`.

### Non-Goals
- Changing podcast provider refresh behavior or forcing client-side refreshes.
- Changing the daily workflow schedule, target-date selection, episode content, or audio generation.
- Changing audio episode generation or publishing. Changing the separate devotional-wallpaper workflow or deleting dormant generator code, local runner scripts, or stored images.

### Risks And Unknowns
- Podcast apps may cache feeds independently; correctly published items can still take time to appear after RSS is updated.
- Retiring the legacy generator stops its scheduled image generation and OneDrive sync. The separate devotional-wallpaper workflow is unaffected.
- Midnight local publication dates convert to different UTC offsets across daylight saving transitions; tests should verify the Chicago zone is applied rather than a fixed offset.

### Recommended Next Step
- Remove active references to the retired workflow and review the updated audio publication date behavior.

- `gh run 38270148024` (Oct 12 devotional images) restored published audio cache at 15:27 UTC and deployed at 15:56 UTC. The audio run saved its updated archive cache at 15:45 UTC and deployed at 15:46 UTC. The image workflow therefore deployed the older cache after the newer audio feed.
- The live feed at `https://jctebo.github.io/spotify_praylist/podcast.xml` listed Oct 12 as its newest episode date at 10:08 UTC on Oct 13.

- `jobs/novena_contracts/rss_publisher.py` delegates to the same shared RSS builder, so one timestamp fix covers daily and novena podcast episodes.
- `docs/architecture/publish-audio-feed-retention.md` now describes the audio workflow as the sole Pages publisher and records the risk of future competing publishers.
- The legacy workflow is disabled in GitHub Actions and its workflow file is removed; generator source code and stored image assets remain untouched.

## Plan

### Summary
- Retire the legacy devotional image workflow entirely. The dedicated audio workflow remains the sole Pages publisher; the separate devotional-wallpaper workflow remains unchanged.
- Set podcast `pubDate` to midnight America/Chicago so current-date episodes are eligible to appear in Spotify and other podcast apps as soon as the daily build finishes.
- These changes directly address the observed Oct 12 deployment order and the current-date release delay.

### What Already Exists
- The audio workflow saves the refreshed `docs/audio` archive cache and deploys `docs/` to GitHub Pages.
- The legacy devotional image workflow is disabled and removed by this change; its generator source code and local runner scripts remain dormant in the repository.
- Both daily and novena RSS generation call `jobs.publish.rss.build_rss_feed`, which uses the shared `_published_at` function.
- The schedules are staggered by 15 minutes. The dedicated audio workflow already restores devotional images from OneDrive and publishes them together with the site and podcast feed.

### Architecture
```text
dedicated audio workflow (render audio -> site/feed -> Pages)

published_date -> midnight America/Chicago -> RSS pubDate -> podcast app
```

### Concrete Changes
- `.github/workflows/publish_audio.yml`
  - Keep this workflow as the sole publisher of the podcast feed and GitHub Pages site.
- `.github/workflows/daily_devotional_image_remote.yml`
  - Delete the retired image-generation/OneDrive workflow and its embedded audio/Pages publisher.
- `jobs/publish/rss.py`
  - Make `_published_at` construct midnight in `ZoneInfo("America/Chicago")` for the job's `published_date`; Python's timezone database will apply the correct daylight or standard offset.
- `requirements.txt`
  - Add the same pinned `tzdata` version already used by the devotional wallpaper dependency set, ensuring the timezone database is available on Windows.
- `docs/architecture/publish-audio-feed-retention.md`
  - Remove active references to the retired workflow and document the dedicated audio workflow as the sole Pages publisher.
- `docs/releases/VERSION` and this release artifact
  - Track release version `0.4.17.0`, scope, implementation, and QA status.

### UX And States
- No interactive UI changes. Current-date episodes are dated at the beginning of the Chicago-local date.
- If an RSS app caches the feed, it may still take time to refresh after the server publishes the updated feed.

### Failure Modes
- If the retired workflow is re-enabled, it can resume unwanted generation and stale Pages publishing; keep it disabled and absent from the default branch.
- A wrong timezone or fixed UTC offset could publish on the previous local date; use the IANA `America/Chicago` zone.
- Podcast apps may cache RSS after the server has the new feed; the server-side feed date and content are the source of truth for verification.
