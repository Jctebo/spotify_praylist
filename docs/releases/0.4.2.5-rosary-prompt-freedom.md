# Release 0.4.2.5 - rosary-prompt-freedom

- Status: Ready to Ship
- Created Date: 2026-08-20
- Last Updated: 2026-08-20
- Depends On: 0.4.2.4

## Scope

### Problem
- The current Rosary prompt reflects the requested meditation content, but it still reads like a rigid production specification.
- Hard requirements around character counts, five distinct human-need categories, repeated observance anchors, and prescribed field behavior constrain the model's ability to write the natural, contemplative message described in the original example.

### Audience
- Primary user: a Catholic listener who wants a meditation that sounds prayerful and human immediately before each decade.
- Secondary stakeholder: the publishing pipeline, which still needs a valid five-mystery package and safe fallback behavior.

### Goal
- Give the LLM more freedom to craft the opening and each mystery in a natural voice while preserving the user's essential spiritual structure.
- Keep deterministic facts authoritative: local date, weekday, mystery set, selected observance, observance date, and today-versus-upcoming distinction.
- Treat the original requested structure as guidance for content and sequence, not as a brittle sentence-by-sentence template.

### Desired Model Freedom
- The model may choose natural paragraphing, transitions, sentence length, devotional voice, and how much Scripture-grounded scene-setting each mystery needs.
- The model may combine the traditional fruit, observance connection, life application, questions, and petition organically rather than forcing each into a mechanically delimited field.
- The model may choose the most meaningful practical connection to the observance instead of mentioning the observance in every decade by formula.
- The model should avoid repetition, academic biography, filler, and weak associations, but these are quality directions rather than exact prose shapes.

### Hard Invariants
- The deterministic date and calendar context must not be changed or re-selected by the LLM.
- The deterministic weekday mystery set and the five supplied mystery rows/fruits must not be changed, substituted, or reordered by the LLM.
- The output must contain an opening and five ordered mysteries.
- The output must remain faithful to Catholic teaching and supplied Scripture/calendar facts.
- Upcoming observances must be described as upcoming with their actual date, never as though celebrated today.
- The selected observance may be omitted from a particular mystery when the spiritual connection would be forced, while the overall Rosary still identifies and meaningfully uses it.
- Generated output must remain safe, bounded enough for audio/publishing, and able to fall back deterministically when generation fails.

### Current Constraints To Reconsider
- Exact minimum and maximum character limits for every prose field.
- Requiring five distinct human-need categories when the user did not request that taxonomy.
- Requiring the dominant priority anchor in the introduction, overall intention, and every decade pair.
- Requiring the saint witness in fixed locations or a fixed number of reflections.
- Treating `intention` and `reflection` as if they must carry a prescribed rhetorical order rather than allowing the model to write a cohesive meditation.

### Non-Goals
- Removing deterministic liturgical authority.
- Allowing the LLM to search backward, select a different observance, verify dates independently, or invent calendar facts.
- Removing the five-mystery output boundary or safe fallback path.
- Rewriting unrelated devotional prompts, calendar selection, audio providers, or publishing routes.

### Success Criteria
- Prompt text clearly distinguishes hard facts from stylistic/content guidance.
- A valid generated response can use varied natural prose without being rejected solely for missing a repeated anchor, category preference, exact length band, or prescribed sentence shape.
- Validation still rejects malformed output, wrong date/observance claims, unsupported Scripture citations, missing mysteries, and unsafe prompt/schema commentary.
- Focused tests demonstrate both freedom cases and hard-invariant rejection cases.

### Biggest Risk
- Relaxing validators too broadly could let vague or spiritually disconnected prose through. The next planning stage must define the smallest semantic safety boundary that preserves quality without recreating the rigidity this refinement is meant to remove.

## Research

- The current structured response has separate `intention` and `reflection` fields because the renderer creates an intention pause and a reflection audio fragment; removing those fields would expand the change into the publishing/audio contract.
- The current validator combines publication safety with legacy style constraints: exact prose minimums, five distinct human-need categories, repeated priority anchors, and required saint-witness presence are not required by the user's requested meditation format.
- Hard safety boundaries that should remain are JSON/schema validity, exactly five ordered mysteries, non-empty bounded prose, prompt/schema commentary rejection, supported Scripture-citation checks, and deterministic observance authority.

## Plan

### Summary
- Keep the existing JSON and renderer shape for compatibility, but make `intention` and `reflection` flexible prose containers rather than a prescribed rhetorical template.
- Remove legacy style gates that force categories, repetition, exact minimum lengths, or saint mentions in fixed locations.
- Rewrite the prompt to present the original meditation format as a natural writing brief: include the elements when spiritually appropriate, connect them coherently, and let the model choose the prose shape.

### Architecture

```text
deterministic date/calendar facts
             |
             v
     flexible writing brief
             |
     JSON shape: 5 ordered mysteries
             |
     safety validation only
             |
     existing text/audio renderer
```

### Concrete Changes
- Edit `jobs/publish/rosary_reflections.py`:
  - retain `dominant_priority_key`, five ordered decades, and bounded non-empty strings;
  - make `human_need_category` optional/defaulted for backward compatibility rather than requiring five distinct categories;
  - remove minimum character requirements and use generous maximum safety bounds suitable for audio;
  - stop requiring dominant-priority anchors in every decade and stop requiring saint-witness mentions in fixed locations;
  - retain rejection of malformed prose, prompt/schema echo, unsupported Scripture citations, and incorrect decade/mystery alignment;
  - rewrite the prompt so the requested sections are creative guidance, explicitly inviting natural paragraphing and organic transitions;
  - remove wording that tells the model to force a fixed order inside every reflection or satisfy a category taxonomy.
- Edit `tests/test_publish_rosary_reflections.py`:
  - prove short-but-valid natural prose is accepted;
  - prove repeated human-need categories and missing saint-witness mentions are accepted;
  - prove a decade can omit the observance anchor when the overall package identifies it;
  - retain negative tests for missing mysteries, wrong order, empty prose, prompt commentary, unsupported Scripture citations, and malformed JSON;
  - assert the prompt uses soft guidance language and contains no rigid character/category/repetition instructions.
- Do not edit `jobs/publish/contracts.py` or audio fragment keys unless tests prove the existing fields cannot carry the more natural prose.

### Failure Modes
- Missing or empty prose: reject and fall back.
- Wrong number/order of mysteries: reject and fall back.
- Unsupported Scripture citation or prompt/schema commentary: reject and fall back.
- Natural variation in length, category, saint mention, or observance repetition: accept when the package remains coherent and safe.
- Provider failure: preserve the existing deterministic fallback.

### QA And Test Matrix
- Focused Rosary tests for relaxed acceptance and retained safety rejection.
- Prompt assertions for deterministic facts, natural-writing freedom, and absence of rigid style rules.
- Publish-contract, text, audio, and audio-fragment regressions to verify renderer compatibility.
- Python compilation and `git diff --check`.

### Rollout Notes
- No schema migration or public publishing contract change.
- Existing consumers continue receiving `intention`, `reflection`, and `human_need_category` fields; only their validation strictness changes.

### Not In Scope
- Calendar selection, date resolution, observance ranking, audio structure, or unrelated devotional prompts.

### Open Questions
- None blocking implementation. The maximum safety bounds will be chosen conservatively from current audio/publishing limits and covered by tests.
