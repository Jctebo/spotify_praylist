# v618 — Block play disc plays only the unplayed prayers

## §1 Scope

John's word on the v616/v617 block play disc (Ora thread, 2026-11-22 10:30 CT, verbatim): "The play disk should only play unplayed orayers" [prayers].

Streamlined small release (Scope + Plan compressed into one pass). Run level: To Ship on John's word. Base: 6bebff3 (the v617 shipped tree; remote main 9d8186368, tree-exact).

The correction, exactly: the Praylist head disc's queue is the block's UNPLAYED tracks only, in standing order. A fully played block queues nothing — its disc stands grey (the v616 rule, unchanged) and a tap plays NOTHING: no queue starts, no error, no state change.

SUPERSESSION, named side by side: v616 shipped "a greyed disc stays tappable and replays the block in full" (the v344 §2.4 fully-played fallback, carried by `queueBuild`). Under v618 the greyed disc stays tappable and plays nothing. The fallback itself is NOT deleted: it remains `queueBuild`'s discipline for its other consumer (Continue, `buildContinueQueue`) and for the block pages' Play All acts, which are not the disc John named and stand outside this word.

Grounded current state (verified in `app/worker/src/client.ts` on the base tree):

- THE DISC'S PATH: the group head paints one disc — `<button class="playbtn small p615disc" data-p615disc="<block>" data-queue="<block>">` (the block-less Episodes tail head: `data-queue="__episodes"`). The shared `[data-queue]` handler in `wirePrayersView` stops propagation (the disc never folds its head), builds `descs` from `extRebuildDescs(day)` filtered to the block (the tail: the block-less descriptors), and calls `startQueue(queueBuild(descs))`.
- `queueBuild(descs)` = `unplayedOnly(descs)`, UNLESS the filtered build holds no available descriptor, in which case it returns the whole `descs` — the §2.4 fully-played fallback. So today a partly played block already queues unplayed-only; a fully played block replays in full. The replay is the whole of this correction.
- THE TWO PREDICATES, grounded: `unplayedOnly` reads podcast → `!x.listened`, else `!isPlayedDesc(x, d)`, and passes UNAVAILABLE descriptors through untouched (availability is `queueLiveDesc`'s question at advance time). The v615 head count (`opnCounts615`) reads AVAILABLE tracks only — "nothing to play, nothing to mark" — podcast → `!t.listened`, else `!(d && isPlayedDesc(t, d))` (occurrence-aware through `isPlayedDesc`). John's word names the disc's queue as the unplayed prayers, and the brief fixes "unplayed" as the v615 count discipline exactly: the disc's filter is the count's predicate, one predicate with two consumers.
- THE EMPTY BUILD: `startQueue([])` returns at its length check, BEFORE the v351 pause toggle — an empty build is a complete no-op. That is the fully-played tap's exact behavior under this release: nothing starts, nothing pauses, nothing changes.
- THE SOUNDING EDGES, grounded and kept: with a queue sounding and playing, a disc tap carrying a non-empty build is the standing pause toggle (`startQueue`'s no-force guard) — byte-identical after this release, whichever block's disc is tapped. A track sounding now is in-progress (unplayed) by definition and stands in its own block's filtered build; the toggle, not a restart, answers its disc. The queue as built at tap time governs: marks landing during playback do not rebuild a standing queue.
- UNCHANGED, by name: the v615 counts and their paint · the grey rule and its `refreshPlayedChrome` seam · the fold · v617's resume (the start item's mark carry and the native transition seek read the queue's descriptors exactly as before — an in-progress track stands in the filtered build and resumes at its mark) · the block pages' and episodes page's Play All acts · Continue's build.
