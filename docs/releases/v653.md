# v653 — Praylist row-menu Play next / Add to queue act on the wrong identity: the menu wiring reads a bare episodeId where the rows carry a card key

## §1 The item (DevOps Board row 1630 · field failure of v645's row-menu legs, live pin ora-pro-nobis-v652-1)

**John's field report (2026-11-24 16:54 CT), verbatim:** "Play next or add to queue doesn't work when adding from the main Praylist window. Doesn't change the queue visibly or play".

**The model (v645, standing, NOT re-opened):** a track row's ⋮ menu carries Play · Play next · Add to queue. Play next inserts the track directly after the current one; with nothing sounding, Play next plays the track. Add to queue appends at the queue's end (the v542 discipline). A Play tap replaces the queue with exactly the tapped scope; at the queue's end playback stops; there is no autoplay and no Continue.

**The ask:** from the main Praylist, Play next puts the tapped track directly after the current one and Add to queue puts it at the end — visibly in the player's Queue section and truly in playback order — for every track family; the rest of the v645 model untouched.

## §2 Plan — the tap path, grounded end to end (base = origin/main 8b62b06, tree a3915d75, production pin ora-pro-nobis-v652-1; all line numbers are `app/worker/src/client.ts` on that tree)

### 2.1 The chain, link by link

1. **The row.** Every Praylist day row paints through `trackCard(t)` (17199). Its ⋮ button carries `data-rowmenu` = the row's **card key** (17264), and its play disc carries `data-playkey` = the same key (17266). The card key is `descCardKey(t)` (17192): `episodeId`, plus `#<n>` when the descriptor is a v610 occurrence copy (`_occ`), plus `@<tag>` when it is a v650 second-painted office side (`_side650`). Two descriptor families therefore carry keys that are **not** bare episodeIds: duplicated Divine Office copies (`_occ`, stamped by the contents-occurrence emission in `extRebuildDescs`) and the second-painted office side (`_side650`, stamped by `extPaintTracks650` at 2246 — every painted side after the first, i.e. under the Edit-form drop down set to **Both**, the Divine Office track of each labeled slot, Sing the Hours painting first).
2. **The menu wiring — THE BREAK.** `wireRowActs(scope)` (17035) wires every `[data-rowmenu]` click in the Prayers paint (the paint calls it at 21926; the settled-card repaints at 17946 / 18008 / 18089 / 18112 / 19891). Its handler (17039) resolves the tapped row's descriptor as:
   `(state.dayDescs || []).filter(function (x) { return x.episodeId === key; })[0] || state.readTracks[key]`
   — a **bare-episodeId comparison against a card key**. For a stamped row the filter can never match (`'ext-morning-prayer@divineoffice' !== 'ext-morning-prayer'`, `'ext-…#1' !== 'ext-…'`), and the fallback misses too (`state.readTracks` is keyed by bare episodeId). The handler then returns at `if (!t) return;` — **the menu never opens**, so Play next / Add to queue can never fire: no queue change, nothing plays, in the sounding case and the nothing-sounding case alike. That is John's report, exactly.
   The contrast stands in the same file: the **play** wiring resolves the same attribute by full card key — `descCardKey(x) === b.getAttribute('data-playkey')` (17939, 17998) — which is why tapping a second-side or copy row **plays** the right track while its ⋮ menu is dead.
3. **The menu.** Where the lookup does land (unstamped rows), `openDayRowMenu(t, …)` (17008) stands Share · Play next · Add to queue (17013–17014, gated on `t.available` and the kind bound) and the items call `playNext(t)` / `addToQueue(t)` on the resolved descriptor.
4. **The acts.** `playNext` (5736) and `addToQueue` (5664) re-resolve the descriptor live (`queueLiveDesc`, 5405 — own/own-standing rows by (episodeId, block) against the fresh build, externals by their slot state, homilies against the device store), apply the kind bound (`queueKindOk645`), and mutate the one queue store: `queueInsert645` (5716) splices at `queueIdx + 1` or at the end into `state.queue`; with nothing standing, Play next falls through to `playTrack(live)`. `queueActFinish645` (5703) / `addToQueue` then persist (`saveQueueSnapshot`), repaint (`renderQueueList` + `updateDock`), and run the native leg (`nativeSendNow` → `nativeResendAtCurrent`).
5. **The paint and the engine.** The player's Queue section paints from that same store — `renderQueueList` (5830) reads `state.previewQueue || state.queue` fresh on every call — and the engine consumes the same array: the web leg advances `state.queue` directly (`advanceQueue`), the native leg rebuilds its contract from it (`nativeQueueExpected` / `nativeContractFor`, divergence read at `nativeQueueDiverged`). **There is one queue store, and the acts write the store the paint and the engine read.** The chain breaks at exactly one link — link 2, the identity read — and only for stamped rows.

### 2.4 The fix (Implement's brief)

The `[data-addmenu]` handler (17057) carries the same bare read for addition cards; Implement verifies the attribute's key form at its emission seat before touching it — addition descriptors are placed payload tracks (no occ/side stamps), so it stands unchanged unless that verification contradicts. Nothing else moves: no change to the acts, the queue store, the paint, the kind bound, the native leg, or the v645 model.

### 3.3 The readTracks fallback — decision + evidence

**Decision: keep the fallback, key it by the bare episodeId parsed off the card key** — `state.readTracks[String(key || '').split(/[#@]/)[0]]` — in both handlers.

Evidence:

- `state.readTracks` is keyed by bare episodeId only, at every writer: the day build (payload `day.blocks[].tracks` + `day.episodes`, keyed `t.episodeId`) and `ownRegisterReadTracks` (own-slot tracks, keyed `m.episodeId`). No writer ever stamps a `#` or `@` into a key, so the old `state.readTracks[key]` read with a stamped card key could never hit — for stamped rows the fallback was as dead as the filter.
- The rows the fallback exists for are payload/own tracks that stand in `readTracks` but not in the composed `state.dayDescs` list (e.g. own-slot tracks registered by `ownRegisterReadTracks` for the settled-card surfaces that also call `wireRowActs`). Parsing the bare id off the card key (the segment before any `#` or `@`) recovers exactly the key those writers used; for unstamped rows the parsed key is the card key itself, so the standing unstamped behavior is byte-identical.

### 3.4 The [data-addmenu] verification

The handler's bare-episodeId read therefore matches its attribute's key form exactly. **The handler stands unchanged**, as the Plan directed (harness leg S2 pins both halves of this).
