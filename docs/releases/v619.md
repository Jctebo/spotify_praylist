### Diagnosis — the order seam (confirmed by harness reproduction)

The stored contents order survives intact end to end: the editor's drag mutates `opn604.lists[block]`; `opnWrite604` writes the whole list verbatim into the standing profile contents (scope=standing) or the today layer (scope=today); `opnStandingContents597` / `opnApplyTodayLayer594` preserve that order into `day.profile.contents` (eff.contents).

The order was LOST at composition in `extRebuildDescs`. Per block it emitted in FIXED KIND GROUPS, ignoring interleaved stored order: (1) payload tracks `b.tracks` in payload order; (2) settings rows (own/ext 'source' selectors) sorted by contents index via `selIdx590` — contents order honored only WITHIN sources; (3) course selectors in contents order (within kind); (4) day selectors (within kind); (5) episode selectors (within kind). Then `foldDayAdditions` wove additions at the block TAIL: contents prayers reach the day only as payload additions synthesized by `opnFoldDay590` (`{block, slug:key, lang:'en', afterId:null, kind:'prayer'}`) adopted into `state.dayAdditions`, so a contents prayer ALWAYS landed at the block tail regardless of its contents position. `foldReadingsSeat` then spliced the readings seat before the daily-rosary descriptor in Morning (the deliberate v549 anchor), regardless of the seat item's contents position.

Consequence: any drag that crossed a kind boundary (episode above course, course above source, prayer above anything, the seat) changed nothing in play order. Same-kind moves stuck. Both scopes share the seam because both feed `day.profile.contents`.

Reproduction (harness-v619, base tree): standing Night [source daily-reflection, source ext-night-prayer, course bible-in-a-year, course exorcist-files]; drag the bible course above daily-reflection through `opnDragOver604` + `opnWrite604` — the stored list carries the move, the composed Night still reads [daily-reflection, ext-night-prayer, biy:day-1, exorcist-files:ep-1] (OA1). A contents prayer dragged above a source lands at the block tail (OA2). The Midday pin dragged to the middle falls to the episode-group tail (OA3).

### Diagnosis — the loss seam (the video's disappeared pin)

Grounded by probe on the base tree, both constructions (pin in the today layer; pin in the standing contents), driving the real local route handlers in a real-envelope world built from John's own document:

- NOT the validators: `opnCheckContents590` (client) and `validateProfileContents` (server, index.ts) both carry kind 'episode' since v614 (malformed keys are a named error, never a silent strip); `opnCheckTodayLayer594` routes today-layer contents through the client check; the standing save route uses the same pair.
- NOT the write or the fold: after an open → move → write round trip on the base tree, the stored todayEdits entry AND the rebuilt day's effective contents still carry the pin, in the saved order, verbatim (`opnWrite604` writes the materialized list whole; `opnApplyTodayLayer594` merges the entry whole — its v601 sourceless-snapshot restore does not trigger on an entry naming sources, and rebuilds nothing from `opnTodayUniverse594`, which carries no episode items but is only ever a fallback for blocks with no stored entry at all).
- The drop is COMPOSITIONAL, the same seam as the order defect: on the base tree the pin stands only at the fixed episode-group position of its block, never at its saved contents position — and it stands NOWHERE in a rebuild whose episode leg does not settle: `resolveProfileEpisodes614` resolves nothing at all when its series fetch fails (the v614 rule, shared with courses — a loaded payload with a missing entry stands the available:false miss card; a failed load stands nothing). A contents item the editor lists and the store carries can therefore be absent from the composed day while standing in every store — the video's total disappearance, and the reason the surviving pair composed in standing sortOrder ([Angelus, Midday Prayer]) with no contents entry in effect for the block's own kinds. The fix below removes the compositional half: the pin stands at its contents position in every rebuild whose resolver leg settles, exactly where the editor put it. The resolver's failed-fetch rule itself is a named v614 design rule and is NOT reversed by this release.

### The fix (minimal seam, no redesign)

In `extRebuildDescs`, when contents stand for the block (`c590` non-null), the block's contents-driven descriptors emit in ONE PASS over the stored contents list, at each item's own position:

- 'source' item → the gated merged settings entry it names (own entry by sourceKey; ext entry by slotId or sourceKey — the standing `selIdx590` filter decides WHICH rows stand, unchanged; the pass only changes WHERE they emit). Own emission is the standing code verbatim (slot present → tracks via `ownSlotTrack`/`expandDescs`, incl. the Novenas `opnNovenaMetas603` selection; missing → the named-missing placeholder with the standing Novenas exception). Ext emission is `extSlotTrack` with the v610 occurrence discipline: the nth contents occurrence of the same selector emits the nth copy, copies after the first stamped `_occ`.
- The Novenas own row stood by kind 'all-novenas'/'novena' items (the gate exception): when the pass reaches the FIRST item that stands that row (an 'all-novenas' or 'novena' item, or a 'source' item naming it) and it has not emitted, the row emits there (its full metas, internally contents-ordered by `opnNovenaMetas603` as before); later novena items emit nothing more for the row.
- 'course' item → the `state.courseRes590` descriptor (standing leg verbatim). 'day' item → the `state.exegesisRes611` descriptor. 'episode' item → the `state.episodeRes614` descriptor (incl. the homily raw-push branch).
- 'prayer' item → its addition descriptor (`dayAdditionDesc` over the record `opnFoldDay590` synthesized on the payload / the standing per-day addition record for that block+slug — the fold's have591 dedupe already treats them as one item, and the one standing copy takes the contents position) AT the contents position; the additions weave then SKIPS that same addition so it never doubles. The skip is a per-rebuild placed set (`placed619`, keyed by block + `dayAdditionIdOf`) built during the pass and consumed by `foldDayAdditions` in the same `extRebuildDescs` call — provably exact, same call, same records. Per-day additions that do NOT correspond to a contents prayer keep their anchor/tail weave exactly as before.
- 'seat' item (Morning 'daily-readings'): the seat descriptor stands at its contents position when the pass reaches the seat item, and `foldReadingsSeat` then skips (no double). Feasibility grounded: `day.readingsSeat` is payload/resolver data present at rebuild time (the resolver's settle re-runs `extRebuildDescs`), and `opnFoldDay590` already nulls it when the seat does not stand in Morning contents. The seat descriptor's construction is factored into one helper (`readingsSeatDesc549`) used by both the fold and the pass. The fold's misplace guard stands: if no daily-rosary descriptor stands in Morning today, a seat placed by the pass is pulled back out and the fold decides exactly as before. NAMED SUPERSESSION: the seat's default position is now its contents position (the tail of John's Morning list, where the mig609 migration and the designed defaults put it) instead of the v549 before-rosary anchor; a drag of the seat item now moves it, which is the defect's own rule.
- When `c590` is null (no contents entry for the block): the standing behavior stands byte-for-byte (merged sortOrder emission; no course/day/episode legs).
- Payload tracks (`b.tracks`) still emit first within the block, as today — they are not contents items and carry no editor rows (the boundary of this fix: the defect and the fix govern the contents list; the block's own day episodes stand ahead of the contents as they always have). `(day.episodes || [])` tail and the Episodes group: unchanged.

Plan (compressed, streamlined path): (1) factor the seat descriptor; teach `foldDayAdditions` the placed-set skip; (2) restructure the per-block emission in `extRebuildDescs` into the single contents pass with the no-contents path preserved verbatim; (3) harness-v619 (below); (4) regression table against a pristine base worktree, reversed legs named and updated only where the fix deliberately reverses them.
