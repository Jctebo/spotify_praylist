The correction, exactly:

2. Each Praylist group head's disc becomes a PLAY DISC: the gold play-disc dress the old Play All row carried pre-v615, standing in the head at the right beside the count. A greyed disc STAYS TAPPABLE (replays the block).
4. The live unplayed count logic (opnCounts615) is NOT touched — v615's counts stand as shipped.
5. The block-less 'Episodes' tail head carries the same play disc (plays the tail's tracks). Vault-locked paint unchanged. Zero-track blocks paint no head (standing rule).

- Vault: `paintListenHome` substitutes the unlock gate while locked — `opnSetHtml609` never runs; no head state is read or written under lock by construction (unchanged by this build).
