### The fix

1. **One standing entry point** — `runner/wallpaper_set.py`:
   - `spec --date D --gospel-title T` builds the date's set manifest from the canonical calendar resolver (`jobs/novena/devotional_calendar.resolve_calendar` over the same canonical ICS the saint resolution reads): one spec per calendar observance for the date, plus the daily Gospel-scene spec (`gospel_scene=True`, subheading "TODAY'S GOSPEL", v300 — its scene title is the one caller-supplied input), plus the month's monthly devotion named as a set member (it stands from monthly staging, not built daily — one picture per subject, v416). The manifest names every spec'd subject with its role, production filename, raw names, desktop wide-raw name, and the set's pick under the standing pick rule.
   - `build --date D --rundir DIR` consumes the manifest: each buildable member × variant reads its raw from `wp-raw-<date>/<slug>-<variant>.webp` and writes the final through the standing render path (`overlay_wallpaper_text` — names composed in code). A missing raw fails the build (exit 3, subjects named) — a short set can no longer slip through silently.
   - `prompt` prints a member's standing art prompt (`build_art_prompt`) so the night's renders follow the standing composition contract.
2. **The rotation checks the spec** — `runner/wallpaper_rotate.py` gains `--spec-manifest`: the plan refuses a queue short any spec'd daily subject (MISSING_QUEUE, subjects named per variant; a subject the monthly extra carries is satisfied by the extra), and the rotation record (`rotation-<date>.json`) records `spec_subjects` + `spec_source`. Without the flag, behavior is the historical behavior — statuses, moves, and the rotation manifest are unchanged (Review's one observation: the ROTATED stdout summary line additionally carries a `"spec_subjects": null` key).
3. **The nightly job routes through it** — the `ora-pro-nobis-nightly-run` cron body (updated via cron.update, verified by read-back): step W(a) generation + rotation go through `wallpaper_set.py` with `--spec-manifest`; step W(b) specs TARGET's set through the entry point; W(d)'s wide-render subject list is the set manifest's member list; step 6's Wallpaper field names the spec'd subjects per set, with a shortfall as a Flag.
4. **Unchanged, by name:** subject composition rules · the production filename contract · Drive folder ids · the desktop set's three-slot shape (principal saint · Gospel · monthly). `site_desktop.py` is not touched — its v554 enumeration consumes the phone set, which the entry point now guarantees complete; the subject list for its wide renders comes from the set manifest.
