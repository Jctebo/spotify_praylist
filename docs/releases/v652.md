## §2 Plan

**The move (client.ts, `openNowPlaying` sheet markup only).** The `#np-share` button element — same id, same aria-label, same Material glyph SVG, byte-identical — leaves the `.np-transport` string and stands in the `.np-controls` string, seated **between `#np-star` and `#np-menu`**: the bottom row reads **Intentions · Read · Star · Share · ⋮**. One-line defense: every standing act keeps its relative order and the ⋮ keeps the terminal seat its anchored menu expects, while Share takes the end-cluster seat that mirrors the end-of-row seat it held on the transport row. On the bottom row Share takes that row's button dress (`btn ghost`, like the star and the ⋮ beside it) so the row reads as one set; the glyph itself does not change. The `#np-share` wiring (~17498) stands exactly as shipped — it queries by id, so no handler moves.

**The alignment (ui.ts, `.np-transport`).** The five remaining controls stand in five equal flex cells: each transport button gains `flex:1 1 0; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center;` and the row gains a `max-width:420px` with `margin:16px auto 20px`, the fixed 26px gap retired. Equal cells make the spacing exactly even at any width, and the middle cell — the play disc's — is exactly the row's center, at phone width and at ≥600px alike; the max-width keeps a wide sheet from stretching the set edge to edge. The play disc keeps its 58px size, centered in its cell. No other `.np-transport` consumer exists (the class stands only in this sheet).

**What does not move.** The seek row and hero above, the Queue head/list below, the dock, the player menu (Share still first in it), the intentions/star/read/menu handlers, and the whole share path (`shareTrackSheet` and everything under it). The bottom row's CSS stands as shipped — five acts fit its centered wrap at phone width.
