## Scope

### Problem
- Ongoing generated watch queue pictures need to reserve clear space at the top for the watch's date/time display, as in the user's current watch-face photo.
- The devotional subject should retain its current large, centered composition. Only the locally overlaid devotional title/subtitle should move to the bottom so neither competes with the clock.
- The approved preview's footer text was too small, and bottom text must stay inside the circular watch safe area so its corners are not clipped.
- The currently synced watch-current and watch-queue files still contain the old top labels and need the same treatment as future images.

### Audience
- Primary user: operator using generated devotional watch wallpapers in the watch queue on a Galaxy Watch Classic.
- Secondary stakeholders: the scheduled wallpaper generator and the existing OneDrive current/queue folders.

### Current Status Quo
- Watch prompt and renderer changes were added to PR #130, reserving the upper 35% and placing exact labels at the bottom.
- The OneDrive watch-current folder contained today's St. Jerome image and watch-queue contained nine dated images through 2026-10-09. All ten have now been updated; the October 5 source was 1023x896 rather than square.
- User reviewed the St. Jerome preview and asked for a slightly larger footer, kept inside the circular safe area.
- Each routine generation creates a new dated watch wallpaper, so changing prompt and renderer updates ongoing queue entries without making a one-off picture.

### Goal
- Future watch-queue images consistently keep the upper 35% visually quiet and dark for the watch clock/date.
- Devotional subject keeps its existing prominent centered scale and composition; exact title and subtitle render slightly larger than the approved preview at the bottom, fully inside the circular safe area and away from corners.
- Reformat all ten existing watch-current/watch-queue files in place after creating byte-identical local backups, preserving full subject scale and producing square output. Completed.
- Phone wallpapers, queue naming, scheduling, and rotation behavior remain unchanged.

### Constraints
- Apply the composition on every future scheduled/manual watch generation through the shared watch prompt and deterministic renderer; migration touches only current/queue watch folders.
- Preserve square 1024x1024 JPEG output, exact devotional text, circular safe-area fitting, OCR/local QA, and vision QA.
- Preserve originals in a local backup directory and verify image hashes/dimensions and rendered text bounds after rewriting synced OneDrive files.
- Do not change phone images, historical archives, or queue/rotation metadata.

### Narrow Wedge
- Revise the watch-only prompt to reserve the upper 35% for clock/date UI while keeping the devotional subject large and centered; move only the overlaid labels.
- Increase footer text modestly from the displayed preview, use text outline/shadow only without a broad fade or banner, and fit every line inside the circular safe chord with a bottom margin.
- Add a guarded migration for exactly `watch-current/*.jpg` and `watch-queue/YYYY-MM-DD/*.jpg`; back up original bytes outside OneDrive before applying, clear safe title bands or use reviewed text-free art overrides where labels overlap subjects, overlay exact text, and verify all ten files.

### Non-Goals
- Editing archived watch images or phone images.
- Changing phone image composition or overlays, watch device configuration, folder names, generation cadence, or rotation logic.
- Adding clock/date text into artwork; the real watch face supplies that UI.

### Risks And Unknowns
- Long labels near the bottom have less circular chord width; renderer must shrink/wrap them safely and tests must cover long labels without clipping while preserving the art composition.
- Several JPEGs have labels baked over faces or halos, so deterministic cleanup would erase subject details. Those scenes need reviewed text-free image edits; exact JPEG backups are required before writing. The October 5 image also needs non-scaling square padding and a small bottom crop.
- Future generated art may place nonessential details in the clock-safe area despite instructions; QA should explicitly inspect top space and text overlap.

### Recommended Next Step
- Use the shared watch prompt and bottom-label renderer for future queue images; the rollback-safe migration updated and verified all ten existing current/queue images.


### Failure Modes
- Lower labels may exceed available circular chord width; fitting shrinks/wraps them, and rendering fails clearly if they still cannot fit.
- A local OneDrive sync may not have hydrated every queue file; migration stops before writing if a target is missing, and requires an explicit reviewed override for a malformed or text-obscured original.
- A partial filesystem/cloud sync failure may leave a mixed set; local backups permit restoring all targets, and file-by-file verification identifies incomplete updates.
- Generated art may place a face/halo in the top clock region; vision QA rejects and existing retry behavior applies.
- OCR crop misses text if coordinates drift; focused tests assert crop bands and rendered pixel bounds.
